3. **S3 Proven Local Reduction**

Let \(\mu(H)\) be the maximum order of a nilpotent subgroup of \(H\). For a nonabelian simple group \(S\) and \(\alpha\in \operatorname{Aut}(S)\), let
\[
c_S(\alpha)=\#\{\text{\(S\)-conjugacy classes in the coset }S\alpha\}.
\]

If \(N=S_1\times\cdots\times S_\ell\) and an element \(x\) normalizes \(N\) while permuting the factors in one \(\ell\)-cycle, then the \(N\)-classes in \(Nx\) reduce to twisted classes in one simple factor. Concretely, after identifying the factors with \(S\), every element of \(Nx\) is \(N\)-conjugate to \((s,1,\ldots,1)x\), and two such representatives are conjugate exactly when their first coordinates are \(\alpha\)-twisted conjugate for the product automorphism around the cycle. Hence the number of \(N\)-classes in that cycle-coset is \(c_S(\alpha)\).

For several cycles, the count multiplies over cycles. Thus the radical-free branch would follow from a simple-factor estimate of the form
\[
c_S(\alpha)\le \mu(S)\quad\text{for every nonabelian simple }S,\ \alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(S),
\]
plus the product construction: if \(P_i\le S_i\) are nilpotent of order \(\mu(S_i)\), then \(P_1\times\cdots\times P_t\) is nilpotent of order \(\prod_i \mu(S_i)\).
