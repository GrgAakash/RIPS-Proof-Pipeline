1. **Assigned Claim**

For a central finite abelian step
\[
1\to A\to H\to \bar H\to 1
\]
construct, for every \(\chi\in \Sha^1(K,A^D)\), an algebraic unramified Brauer class \(B_\chi\) on the relevant homogeneous space \(X\), and prove
\[
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v(B_\chi(x_v))
=
\langle \Delta(\xi),\chi\rangle_{\mathrm{PT}}
\]
for adelic local neutralizations.

2. **Verdict**

Not solved. The missing point is not a minor sign or notation issue: the requested construction of \(B_\chi\in Br_{nr,alg}(X)\) is exactly the hard Brauer-descent compatibility, and I cannot derive it from the allowed standalone material plus standard background alone.

3. **What Can Be Set Up**

Given a local neutralization \(x_v\), one can represent the local lifting obstruction through \(H\) by an \(A\)-valued cocycle/cochain. If \(\Delta(\xi)\in \Sha^2(K,A)\), the Poitou-Tate pairing with \(\chi\in\Sha^1(K,A^D)\) can be written cochain-theoretically as a finite sum of local invariants, after choosing local trivializations of \(\Delta(\xi)_v\). That part is standard.

4. **Where The Route Breaks**

The central extension class naturally gives an \(A\)-valued obstruction to lifting \(\bar H\)-data to \(H\). Pairing this obstruction with \(\chi\in H^1(K,A^D)\) does not by itself produce a degree-2 Brauer class on \(X\). Naively, the degrees land in the wrong place unless one supplies an additional transgression/descent construction.

Even if a class in \(Br(X)\) were constructed, unramifiedness is not automatic. For nonproper homogeneous spaces, algebraic Brauer classes can ramify along boundary divisors. Finite stabilizer plus centrality does not, by itself, prove vanishing of all divisorial residues.

5. **Concrete Branch Guidance**

To close S4, one needs an additional lemma/theorem of the following precise form:

For the central extension \(1\to A\to H\to \bar H\to1\), there is a canonical map
\[
\theta:\Sha^1(K,A^D)\to Br_{nr,alg}(X)
\]
such that, for every adelic system of local neutralizations \((x_v)\),
\[
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v(\theta(\chi)(x_v))
=
\langle \Delta(\xi),\chi\rangle_{\mathrm{PT}}.
\]

This must be proved either by an explicit Čech/Hochschild-Serre cocycle construction with a full residue computation, or cited as a standalone finite-gerbe descent/Brauer-Manin compatibility theorem. Without that lemma, S4 remains open.
