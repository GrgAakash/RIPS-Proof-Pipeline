1. **Claim**

Solved. For a central extension of finite \(K\)-kernels/groups
\[
1\to A\to H\to Q\to 1
\]
with \(A\) finite abelian and central, a locally liftable class \(\xi\in H^1(K,Q)\) has a canonical obstruction
\[
\Delta(\xi)\in \Sha^2(K,A)
\]
whose vanishing is equivalent to the existence of a global lift \(\tilde\xi\in H^1(K,H)\).

2. **Setup**

Let \(\Gamma_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar K/K)\). After choosing a geometric model, regard \(H\) as a finite \(\Gamma_K\)-group, \(A\subset Z(H)\) as a finite \(\Gamma_K\)-stable abelian subgroup, and \(Q=H/A\).

Assume \(\xi\in H^1(K,Q)\) is locally liftable: for every place \(v\) of \(K\), its restriction \(\xi_v\in H^1(K_v,Q)\) lies in the image of
\[
H^1(K_v,H)\to H^1(K_v,Q).
\]

3. **Definition of \(\Delta(\xi)\)**

Choose a continuous \(1\)-cocycle \(q_\sigma\in Q(\bar K)\) representing \(\xi\). Choose arbitrary lifts \(h_\sigma\in H(\bar K)\) of \(q_\sigma\). Since \(q\) is a cocycle,
\[
q_{\sigma\tau}=q_\sigma\,{}^\sigma q_\tau.
\]
Therefore
\[
a_{\sigma,\tau}:=h_\sigma\,{}^\sigma h_\tau\,h_{\sigma\tau}^{-1}
\]
maps to \(1\in Q\), so \(a_{\sigma,\tau}\in A(\bar K)\).

Because \(A\) is central, \((a_{\sigma,\tau})\) is a usual abelian \(2\)-cocycle. Its class in \(H^2(K,A)\) is independent of the chosen representative \(q\) and the chosen lifts \(h_\sigma\). Define
\[
\Delta(\xi):=[a_{\sigma,\tau}]\in H^2(K,A).
\]

4. **Why \(\Delta(\xi)\in\Sha^2(K,A)\)**

For each place \(v\), local liftability gives an \(H\)-cocycle \(\tilde h_\sigma\) mapping to \(q_\sigma\) over \(\Gamma_{K_v}\). Using these lifts, the corresponding obstruction cocycle is trivial:
\[
\tilde h_\sigma\,{}^\sigma\tilde h_\tau\,\tilde h_{\sigma\tau}^{-1}=1.
\]
Hence \(\Delta(\xi)_v=0\in H^2(K_v,A)\) for all \(v\). Thus
\[
\Delta(\xi)\in \ker\!\left(H^2(K,A)\to \prod_v H^2(K_v,A)\right)=\Sha^2(K,A).
\]

5. **Vanishing iff Lift Exists**

If \(\xi\) has a global lift \(\tilde\xi\in H^1(K,H)\), choose an \(H\)-cocycle \(h_\sigma\) representing it. Then
\[
h_\sigma\,{}^\sigma h_\tau=h_{\sigma\tau},
\]
so the obstruction cocycle is trivial and \(\Delta(\xi)=0\).

Conversely, suppose \(\Delta(\xi)=0\). Then
\[
a_{\sigma,\tau}=b_\sigma\,{}^\sigma b_\tau\,b_{\sigma\tau}^{-1}
\]
for some \(1\)-cochain \(b_\sigma\in A(\bar K)\). Replace the chosen lifts by
\[
h'_\sigma=b_\sigma^{-1}h_\sigma.
\]
Since \(A\) is central, the modified lifts satisfy
\[
h'_\sigma\,{}^\sigma h'_\tau=h'_{\sigma\tau}.
\]
Thus \(h'_\sigma\) is an \(H\)-valued \(1\)-cocycle mapping to \(q_\sigma\), so it defines a global lift of \(\xi\) in \(H^1(K,H)\).
