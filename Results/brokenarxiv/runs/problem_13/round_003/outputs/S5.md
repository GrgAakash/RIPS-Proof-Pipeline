**1. Assigned Claim**

Conditional on S4: if every central abelian character obstruction `B_chi` is constructed in `Br_nr,alg(X)` and its evaluation on adelic local points equals the relevant Poitou-Tate pairing with the Springer class, then the global induction proves the target theorem.

**2. Induction Object**

Induct on `|\bar H|`, where `\bar H` is the finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer of `X_{\bar K}`.

Use the Springer gerbe/class `\eta_X` banded by the finite `K`-kernel/stabilizer form `H`. Standard fact: for a homogeneous space of `SL_n`, `X(K) != empty` iff `\eta_X` is neutral.

Local points imply every localization `\eta_{X,v}` is neutral.

**3. Induction Step**

If `H=1`, then `X` is an `SL_n`-torsor. Since `H^1(K,SL_n)=1`, `X(K)` exists.

Assume `H` nontrivial finite nilpotent. Its center has a nontrivial finite abelian characteristic subgroup `A`, hence `A` is stable under descent. Consider

```text
1 -> A -> H -> H' -> 1.
```

The image of `\eta_X` in `H^2(K,H')` is locally neutral. By induction applied to the smaller nilpotent stabilizer `H'`, this image is globally neutral.

Thus, after choosing a neutralization downstairs, the remaining obstruction to neutralizing `\eta_X` lies in an abelian cohomology class attached to `A`. For each character `chi` of `A`, S4 gives a class

```text
B_chi in Br_nr,alg(X)
```

whose local evaluation equals the Poitou-Tate pairing of `chi` with that residual obstruction.

**4. Use Of Brauer Constancy**

The hypothesis says

```text
Br_nr,alg(X) = im Br(K).
```

Therefore every `B_chi` is constant. Its evaluation on any adelic family of local points is independent of places and has total invariant zero.

By S4 compatibility, all Poitou-Tate pairings of the residual abelian obstruction with all characters `chi` vanish. Poitou-Tate nondegeneracy then forces the residual obstruction itself to vanish. Hence `\eta_X` is neutral, so `X(K)` exists.

**5. Audit: No Hidden Assumptions**

No quotient homogeneous space over `K` is required. The induction is on the Springer gerbe/band, not on an actual geometric quotient `X/A` or `X/H'`.

No constant-stabilizer assumption is inserted. The subgroup `A` is chosen characteristic in the finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer, so it descends as a central abelian sub-kernel of the stabilizer kernel.

The only nonformal dependency is exactly S4: construction of `B_chi`, unramifiedness, algebraicity, and the equality between Brauer-Manin evaluation and the Poitou-Tate pairing.
