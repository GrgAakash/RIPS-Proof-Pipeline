**1 Target Decomposition**

Goal: prove the Springer obstruction gerbe of `X` is neutral.

1. Let `G = SL_n`. Choose `xbar in X(Kbar)` with finite nilpotent stabilizer `Hbar`. This gives a finite nilpotent `K`-kernel/link `L` and a Springer class  
   `eta_X in H^2(K,L)`.
2. Local points `X(K_v) != empty` imply `eta_X` is locally neutral for every place `v`.
3. Use the characteristic upper central series of `Hbar`. Since it is characteristic, it descends to a filtration of `K`-kernels whose layers are finite central abelian `K`-groups `A`.
4. At each central step  
   `1 -> A -> H -> Hbar -> 1`,  
   reduce lifting a quotient neutralization to vanishing of a class  
   `Delta(xi) in Sha^2(K,A)`.
5. For every `chi in Sha^1(K,A^D)`, construct an algebraic unramified Brauer class `B_chi` on `X` and prove
   `sum_v inv_v(B_chi(x_v)) = <Delta(xi), chi>_PT`.
6. Since `Br_nr,alg(X)` is constant, each `B_chi` comes from `Br(K)`, so the left side is `0` by global reciprocity. Poitou-Tate duality forces `Delta(xi)=0`.
7. Induct through the nilpotent filtration. Then `eta_X` is neutral. Because `SL_n` is special and `H^1(K,SL_n)=1`, neutrality gives `X(K) != empty`.

**3 Subclaim Support Graph**

- `SC0`: `X(K_v)` gives local neutralizations of `eta_X`; neutral `eta_X` gives `X(K)`.
- `SC1`: finite nilpotent `Hbar` admits a Galois-stable central filtration with finite abelian layers.

`SC1 + SC2 + SC3 + SC4 + SC5 => SC6`, and `SC0 + SC6 => theorem`.

**4 Hardest Step Prediction**

The hardest step is `SC4`.

It must not be cited as a known lemma. It needs an internal proof:

- Construct `B_chi` using transgression/cup product from the central `A`-gerbe and `chi in H^1(K,A^D)`.
- Prove `B_chi` is algebraic and unramified, by checking extension/residue vanishing at codimension-one valuations using finite stabilizer and centrality.
- Prove the local evaluation identity by writing cocycles for local points, lifting through `H`, extracting the resulting `A`-valued `2`-cocycle, and comparing cup product invariants with the Poitou-Tate pairing.

If this cocycle comparison or unramifiedness check cannot be supplied, the proof remains unresolved exactly at `SC4`.

**5 Failure-Mode Checks**

- Ensure `Delta(xi)` really lies in `Sha^2(K,A)`, using compatible local neutralizations.
- Use only `chi in Sha^1(K,A^D)` for the perfect pairing.
- Check sign conventions in the evaluation formula.
- Do not apply the target theorem inductively to quotient spaces unless those spaces and their Brauer hypotheses are proved.
