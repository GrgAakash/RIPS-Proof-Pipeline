1. Assignment Restatement

S4 assignment: prove SC4 only. In one step of a central nilpotent filtration, let
\[
1\to A\to H\to \bar H\to 1
\]
be a central extension of finite \(K\)-groups with \(A\) finite abelian. For a global lifting problem whose local liftings exist everywhere, identify the remaining global obstruction via Poitou-Tate duality, and show that its pairing with \(H^1(K,A^D)\) is exactly the corresponding algebraic unramified Brauer-Manin evaluation.

2. Subproof or Failure

Let \(A^D=\operatorname{Hom}(A,\bar K^\times)\) be the Cartier dual. For a central extension
\[
1\to A\to H\to \bar H\to 1
\]
and a class \(\xi\in H^1(K,\bar H)\), nonabelian cohomology gives a connecting obstruction
\[
\Delta(\xi)\in H^2(K,A).
\]
Because \(A\) is central, this obstruction is well-defined in ordinary abelian cohomology, and \(\xi\) lifts to \(H^1(K,H)\) if \(\Delta(\xi)=0\). The same construction over each completion gives
\[
\Delta_v(\xi_v)\in H^2(K_v,A).
\]

Assume \(\xi\) is locally liftable everywhere. Then \(\Delta_v(\xi_v)=0\) for every place \(v\), so
\[
\Delta(\xi)\in \Sha^2(K,A):=\ker\Bigl(H^2(K,A)\to \prod_v H^2(K_v,A)\Bigr).
\]
Poitou-Tate duality for finite Galois modules over number fields gives a perfect pairing
\[
\Sha^2(K,A)\times \Sha^1(K,A^D)\to \mathbf Q/\mathbf Z.
\]
Thus the global lifting obstruction is detected by the functional
\[
\chi\mapsto \langle \Delta(\xi),\chi\rangle_{\mathrm{PT}},
\qquad \chi\in \Sha^1(K,A^D).
\]
In particular, local liftability plus vanishing of this functional for every \(\chi\) implies \(\Delta(\xi)=0\), hence the desired global lift exists.

It remains to identify this functional with the relevant Brauer-Manin evaluation. For \(\chi\in H^1(K,A^D)\), the cup product pairing
\[
H^2(K,A)\times H^1(K,A^D)\to H^3(K,\bar K^\times)\cong \mathbf Q/\mathbf Z
\]
is locally represented by
\[
\operatorname{inv}_v\bigl(\Delta_v(\xi_v)\cup \chi_v\bigr).
\]
Equivalently, the central extension class defining \(1\to A\to H\to \bar H\to 1\), paired with \(\chi\), gives an algebraic Brauer class on the corresponding homogeneous space or lifting torsor. Evaluating this Brauer class at a local point \(x_v\) whose associated \(\bar H\)-torsor class is \(\xi_v\) gives
\[
\operatorname{inv}_v\bigl(\mathcal B_\chi(x_v)\bigr)
=
\operatorname{inv}_v\bigl(\Delta_v(\xi_v)\cup \chi_v\bigr).
\]
This is the standard compatibility of the nonabelian connecting map with cup products and the local invariant map.

Therefore for an adelic family \((x_v)_v\),
\[
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v\bigl(\mathcal B_\chi(x_v)\bigr)
=
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v\bigl(\Delta_v(\xi_v)\cup \chi_v\bigr)
=
\langle \Delta(\xi),\chi\rangle_{\mathrm{PT}}.
\]
Only finitely many summands are nonzero, as usual for algebraic Brauer-Manin evaluations on adelic points.

Hence, for one finite abelian central quotient, the obstruction to global lifting is precisely the Poitou-Tate dual obstruction in \(\Sha^2(K,A)\), and its pairing with \(\Sha^1(K,A^D)\) equals the algebraic unramified Brauer-Manin pairing attached to the same quotient.
