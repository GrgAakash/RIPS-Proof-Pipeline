2. Subproof or failure

Let
\[
\operatorname{Br}_{nr,alg}(X)=\ker(\operatorname{Br}_{nr}(X)\to \operatorname{Br}_{nr}(X_{\bar K})).
\]
By the hypothesis of the target theorem, every class
\[
\alpha\in \operatorname{Br}_{nr,alg}(X)
\]
lies in the image of the pullback map
\[
\operatorname{Br}(K)\to \operatorname{Br}(X).
\]
Thus there exists some class \(\beta\in \operatorname{Br}(K)\) such that
\[
\alpha=p^*\beta,
\]
where \(p:X\to \operatorname{Spec}K\) is the structure morphism.

Since \(X(K_v)\neq\varnothing\) for every completion \(K_v\), choose \(x_v\in X(K_v)\) for every place \(v\). Interpreting the local-solubility hypothesis in the usual adelic sense for varieties of finite type over a number field, choose the family \((x_v)_v\) as an adelic point of \(X\), i.e.
\[
(x_v)_v\in X(\mathbb A_K).
\]

For every place \(v\), evaluation of \(\alpha=p^*\beta\) at \(x_v\) gives
\[
\alpha(x_v)=x_v^*(p^*\beta)=(p\circ x_v)^*\beta=\beta_{K_v}\in \operatorname{Br}(K_v).
\]
Therefore the Brauer-Manin sum attached to \(\alpha\) and \((x_v)_v\) is
\[
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v(\alpha(x_v))
=
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v(\beta_{K_v}).
\]
By global reciprocity for the Brauer group of a number field, the final sum is zero:
\[
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v(\beta_{K_v})=0.
\]
Hence
\[
\sum_v \operatorname{inv}_v(\alpha(x_v))=0
\]
for every \(\alpha\in \operatorname{Br}_{nr,alg}(X)\).

Thus the adelic point \((x_v)_v\) is orthogonal to \(\operatorname{Br}_{nr,alg}(X)\), so
\[
X(\mathbb A_K)^{\operatorname{Br}_{nr,alg}}\neq\varnothing.
\]
