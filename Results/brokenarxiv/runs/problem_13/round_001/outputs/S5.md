2. Subproof or failure

Prerequisites used:

SC2: for a homogeneous space \(X\) of \(SL_n\) with finite geometric stabilizer, the Springer/Giraud obstruction \(\eta_X\) is locally neutral if \(X(K_v)\ne\varnothing\) for all \(v\), and global neutrality of \(\eta_X\) implies \(X(K)\ne\varnothing\).

SC3: because the geometric stabilizer is finite nilpotent, it admits a finite central filtration whose successive quotients are finite abelian \(K\)-group objects after descent along the obstruction gerbe.

SC4: at each abelian central lifting step, the obstruction to global lifting is exactly the Brauer-Manin/abelian obstruction pairing.

Let \(\eta_X\) be the Springer obstruction attached to \(X\). Since \(X(K_v)\ne\varnothing\) for every completion \(K_v\), SC2 gives that \(\eta_X\) is locally neutral everywhere.

Choose the central filtration supplied by SC3. We induct along this finite filtration. At the initial quotient, the obstruction is trivial or already neutral by construction of the bottom stage. Suppose that at some stage the obstruction has been made globally neutral modulo the next central subgroup. The remaining obstruction to lifting neutrality through the next central abelian quotient is, by SC4, the corresponding abelian Brauer-Manin obstruction.

By SC1, the adelic local neutralizations coming from the local points are orthogonal to the relevant Brauer classes, because the unramified algebraic Brauer group is constant:
\[
\ker(\operatorname{Br}_{nr}(X)\to \operatorname{Br}_{nr}(X_{\bar K}))
=
\operatorname{im}(\operatorname{Br}(K)\to \operatorname{Br}(X)).
\]
Thus the abelian obstruction at this stage vanishes. Therefore the neutralization lifts globally one step further.

Since the filtration is finite, induction gives global neutrality of the original Springer obstruction \(\eta_X\). Applying SC2, global neutrality of \(\eta_X\) implies that \(X\) has a \(K\)-rational point. Hence \(X(K)\ne\varnothing\), as required.
