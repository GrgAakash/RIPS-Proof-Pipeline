## 1. Assignment restatement

S3 is assigned SC3: prove that the finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer admits a Galois-compatible characteristic central filtration, and formulate the induced sequence of lifting problems in which each step has finite abelian kernel.

## 2. Subproof or failure

Let \(\bar H\) be the finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer of a geometric point of \(X_{\bar K}\). Since \(X\) is defined over \(K\), the Springer/Giraud band of \(\bar H\) carries a continuous \(\Gamma_K=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar K/K)\)-action through outer automorphisms. Any characteristic subgroup of \(\bar H\) is therefore preserved by this descent action, so characteristic filtrations are Galois-compatible at the level of bands.

Use the upper central series of the finite nilpotent group \(\bar H\):
\[
1=Z_0(\bar H)\subset Z_1(\bar H)\subset \cdots \subset Z_c(\bar H)=\bar H,
\]
where \(Z_1(\bar H)=Z(\bar H)\), and \(Z_{i+1}(\bar H)/Z_i(\bar H)=Z(\bar H/Z_i(\bar H))\).

Standard group theory gives:

1. Each \(Z_i(\bar H)\) is characteristic in \(\bar H\).
2. Since \(\bar H\) is nilpotent and finite, the series reaches \(\bar H\) after finitely many steps.
3. Each quotient
\[
A_i:=Z_{i+1}(\bar H)/Z_i(\bar H)
\]
is finite abelian.
4. More strongly, \(A_i\) is central in \(\bar H/Z_i(\bar H)\).

Thus we get finite quotients
\[
\bar H_i:=\bar H/Z_i(\bar H),
\]
and central extensions
\[
1\to A_i\to \bar H_{i+1?}\to \bar H_i\to 1
\]
up to indexing. To avoid ambiguity, set instead
\[
Q_i:=\bar H/Z_i(\bar H).
\]
Then
\[
1\to A_i=Z_{i+1}(\bar H)/Z_i(\bar H)
\to Q_i
\to Q_{i+1}
\to 1
\]
is a central extension with finite abelian kernel \(A_i\). Because all \(Z_i(\bar H)\) are characteristic, these are \(\Gamma_K\)-compatible extensions of bands.

Now let \(\eta\) denote the Springer/Giraud obstruction class of \(X\), viewed as a gerbe or nonabelian cohomology class banded by \(\bar H\). Passing from \(\bar H\) to \(Q_i\) gives pushed-forward obstruction classes
\[
\eta_i\in H^2(K,Q_i)
\]
in the corresponding nonabelian/banded sense.

The successive lifting problem is:

Given neutrality, or a chosen object/lift, for the quotient obstruction \(\eta_{i+1}\) banded by \(Q_{i+1}\), determine whether it lifts across the central extension
\[
1\to A_i\to Q_i\to Q_{i+1}\to 1
\]
to a neutral obstruction banded by \(Q_i\).

Because \(A_i\) is central and finite abelian, the obstruction to this lift lies in ordinary abelian Galois cohomology
\[
H^2(K,A_i)
\]
with the \(\Gamma_K\)-module structure induced by the band. If the obstruction vanishes, the set of possible lifts is governed by the corresponding \(H^1(K,A_i)\)-torsor ambiguity.

Thus the nilpotent obstruction can be reduced to finitely many central abelian lifting problems, one for each finite abelian layer
\[
A_i=Z_{i+1}(\bar H)/Z_i(\bar H).
\]

This proves SC3 as a structural reduction. It does not prove that the abelian lifting obstructions vanish; that is exactly the kind of input expected from SC4.
