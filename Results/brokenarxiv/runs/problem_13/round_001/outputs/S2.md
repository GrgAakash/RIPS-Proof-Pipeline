2. Subproof or failure

Let `G = SL_n` over `K`, and let `Γ = Gal(\bar K/K)`. Since `X` is a homogeneous space of `G`, choose a geometric point `\bar x in X(\bar K)`. Let

```text
\bar H = Stab_{G_{\bar K}}(\bar x).
```

This is finite by hypothesis. The absolute Galois group acts semilinearly on `G_{\bar K}` and `X_{\bar K}`. For each `σ in Γ`, `σ(\bar H)` is the stabilizer of `σ(\bar x)`. Since `G_{\bar K}` acts transitively on `X_{\bar K}`, choose `g_σ in G(\bar K)` such that

```text
g_σ σ(\bar x) = \bar x.
```

Then conjugation by `g_σ` identifies `σ(\bar H)` with `\bar H`. The class of this identification modulo inner automorphisms by elements of `\bar H` is independent of the choice of `g_σ`. These outer semilinear descent data define the Springer/Giraud link, or `K`-kernel,

```text
L_X = (\bar H, κ),
```

where `κ` is the resulting homomorphism `Γ -> Out(\bar H)` in the standard nonabelian sense.

Now define the nonabelian 2-cocycle attached to the chosen `g_σ` by

```text
c_{σ,τ} = g_σ σ(g_τ) g_{στ}^{-1}.
```

Because both `g_σ σ(g_τ)` and `g_{στ}` send `στ(\bar x)` to `\bar x`, their quotient fixes `\bar x`; hence

```text
c_{σ,τ} in \bar H.
```

Together with the descent identifications above, the pair `(g_σ, c_{σ,τ})` satisfies the usual Giraud cocycle identities for a 2-cocycle with band `L_X`. Its cohomology class

```text
η_X in H^2(K, L_X)
```

is independent of the choices of `\bar x` and `g_σ`, up to the standard equivalence of links. This is the Springer/Giraud obstruction class of the homogeneous space `X`.

For any completion `K_v`, base change gives the same construction over `K_v`, producing the localized class

```text
η_{X,v} in H^2(K_v, L_{X,v}).
```

If `X(K_v)` is nonempty, choose `x_v in X(K_v)` and choose the geometric base point to be `x_v`. Then `σ(x_v)=x_v` for all `σ in Γ_v`, so one may take `g_σ = 1`. The resulting 2-cocycle is trivial, hence `η_{X,v}` is neutral. Thus local points everywhere give local neutralizations of the Springer/Giraud obstruction.

It remains to prove that global neutrality gives a `K`-point. Assume `η_X` is neutral. By the standard Springer-Giraud interpretation of neutral classes for homogeneous spaces, neutrality means that there exists a `K`-form `H` of the geometric stabilizer link and a `G`-torsor `P` over `K` equipped with a `G`-equivariant morphism

```text
P -> X.
```

Equivalently, `X` is reached from a principal homogeneous space under `G`; this is the usual neutral gerbe/descent datum of the pair `(G_{\bar K}, \bar x)`.

Since `G = SL_n`, the standard specialness theorem for `SL_n` gives

```text
H^1(K, SL_n)=1.
```

Indeed, an `SL_n`-torsor is the same as a rank `n` vector space with trivialized determinant after descent; over a field every rank `n` vector space is isomorphic to `K^n`, and the determinant trivialization removes the residual scalar obstruction. Thus every `SL_n`-torsor over `K` is trivial.

Therefore the `G`-torsor `P` has a `K`-point `p in P(K)`. Applying the `K`-morphism `P -> X` gives a point

```text
x = f(p) in X(K).
```

Hence global neutrality of `η_X` implies `X(K) nonempty`.

This proves SC2.
