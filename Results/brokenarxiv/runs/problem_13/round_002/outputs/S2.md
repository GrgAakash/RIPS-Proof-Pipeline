1. **Assigned Claim**

Let \(H\) be the finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer of \(X_{\bar K}\), viewed with its Springer \(K\)-kernel action, i.e. a continuous \(\Gamma_K\)-action on \(H\) well-defined up to inner automorphism. Then \(H\) admits a finite filtration
\[
1=H_0\subset H_1\subset \cdots \subset H_r=H
\]
by characteristic subgroups such that each quotient \(A_i=H_i/H_{i-1}\) is finite abelian and central in \(H/H_{i-1}\). Hence each step is a central finite abelian extension
\[
1\to A_i\to H/H_{i-1}\to H/H_i\to 1
\]
compatible with the \(K\)-kernel structure.

2. **Construction**

Use the upper central series of the finite nilpotent group \(H\):
\[
Z_0(H)=1,\qquad Z_{i+1}(H)/Z_i(H)=Z(H/Z_i(H)).
\]
Since \(H\) is nilpotent, this series terminates at \(H\). Each \(Z_i(H)\) is characteristic in \(H\), because centers and preimages of characteristic subgroups are characteristic. Therefore the Springer \(K\)-kernel action preserves every \(Z_i(H)\), even though the action is only defined up to inner automorphism.

The quotients
\[
A_i=Z_i(H)/Z_{i-1}(H)
\]
are finite abelian, and by definition
\[
A_i\subset Z(H/Z_{i-1}(H)).
\]

3. **Compatibility With The \(K\)-Kernel**

Because each \(Z_i(H)\) is characteristic, every automorphism of \(H\) preserves it. Thus the outer \(\Gamma_K\)-action defining the \(K\)-kernel restricts to each \(Z_i(H)\) and descends to each quotient \(H/Z_i(H)\) and \(A_i\).

Moreover, since \(A_i\) is central in \(H/Z_{i-1}(H)\), inner automorphisms of \(H/Z_{i-1}(H)\) act trivially on \(A_i\). Hence the induced \(\Gamma_K\)-action on \(A_i\) is an honest Galois action, not merely an outer one. So each \(A_i\) is a finite commutative \(K\)-group, or equivalently a finite discrete \(\Gamma_K\)-module.

4. **Reduction To Central Abelian Lifting Steps**

Let \(L_i\) denote the \(K\)-kernel attached to \(H/Z_i(H)\). Then the theorem for stabilizer kernel \(L_r=H\) can be approached inductively through the tower
\[
H=H/Z_0(H)\twoheadrightarrow H/Z_1(H)\twoheadrightarrow \cdots \twoheadrightarrow H/Z_r(H)=1.
\]

At step \(i\), one has a central finite abelian extension of \(K\)-kernels
\[
1\to A_i\to H/Z_{i-1}(H)\to H/Z_i(H)\to 1.
\]
Thus the only obstruction to lifting a neutralization from the quotient kernel \(H/Z_i(H)\) to \(H/Z_{i-1}(H)\) is the usual central obstruction class
\[
\Delta_i\in H^2(K,A_i),
\]
with local analogues in \(H^2(K_v,A_i)\). If the quotient gerbe is globally neutral and the local liftings exist everywhere, then \(\Delta_i\) lies in
\[
\Sha^2(K,A_i).
\]

Therefore, proving that every such central obstruction \(\Delta_i\) vanishes is enough to lift neutrality successively from the trivial kernel \(H/Z_r(H)=1\) back to the original stabilizer kernel \(H\). This reduces the global rational-point theorem to the repeated analysis of central finite abelian lifting steps.

5. **Boundary Of This Subproblem**

This solves only the filtration and reduction step. It does not prove the Brauer-Manin/Poitou-Tate compatibility needed to kill each \(\Delta_i\). That is precisely the hard SC4 step from the blueprint.
