## 1. Assigned Subproblem

Let
\[
1\to A\to H\to \overline H\to 1
\]
be a central extension of finite \(K\)-group schemes, with \(A\) finite commutative. Given a global \(\overline H\)-object/class \(\xi\) which is locally liftable to \(H\) at every completion \(K_v\), define an obstruction
\[
\Delta(\xi)\in \Sha^2(K,A)
\]
and prove:
\[
\Delta(\xi)=0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \xi \text{ admits a global lift to } H.
\]

## 2. Precise Setup

Use standard nonabelian Galois cohomology for finite group schemes over a number field.

Because \(A\) is central in \(H\), the usual lifting obstruction for
\[
H^1(K,H)\to H^1(K,\overline H)
\]
lands in the abelian group \(H^2(K,A)\). For a class
\[
\xi\in H^1(K,\overline H),
\]
choose a cocycle representative \(\bar c\colon \Gamma_K\to \overline H(\bar K)\). Locally liftability means that for every place \(v\), the localized class \(\xi_v\in H^1(K_v,\overline H)\) lies in the image of \(H^1(K_v,H)\).

## 3. Definition of \(\Delta(\xi)\)

Choose set-theoretic lifts \(c_\sigma\in H(\bar K)\) of \(\bar c_\sigma\). Define
\[
a_{\sigma,\tau}
=
c_\sigma\cdot \sigma(c_\tau)\cdot c_{\sigma\tau}^{-1}
\in A(\bar K).
\]
Since \(\bar c\) is a \(1\)-cocycle and \(A=\ker(H\to \overline H)\), this indeed lies in \(A\). Centrality of \(A\) implies \((a_{\sigma,\tau})\) is a genuine \(2\)-cocycle with values in \(A\). Its cohomology class is independent of the chosen lifts and of the cocycle representative of \(\xi\). Define
\[
\Delta(\xi):=[a_{\sigma,\tau}]\in H^2(K,A).
\]

If \(\xi\) is locally liftable everywhere, then \(\Delta(\xi)_v=0\in H^2(K_v,A)\) for every \(v\). Hence
\[
\Delta(\xi)\in \ker\Bigl(H^2(K,A)\to \prod_v H^2(K_v,A)\Bigr)
=\Sha^2(K,A).
\]

## 4. Vanishing Implies Global Lift

Suppose \(\Delta(\xi)=0\). Then the cocycle \(a_{\sigma,\tau}\) is a coboundary. Thus there exist \(b_\sigma\in A(\bar K)\) such that
\[
a_{\sigma,\tau}=b_\sigma\cdot \sigma(b_\tau)\cdot b_{\sigma\tau}^{-1}.
\]
Replace the chosen lifts \(c_\sigma\) by
\[
c'_\sigma=b_\sigma^{-1}c_\sigma.
\]
Using centrality of \(A\), one checks:
\[
c'_\sigma\sigma(c'_\tau)(c'_{\sigma\tau})^{-1}=1.
\]
Therefore \(c'\colon \Gamma_K\to H(\bar K)\) is a genuine \(1\)-cocycle. Its image in \(\overline H\) is \(\bar c\), so it defines a global lift
\[
\tilde \xi\in H^1(K,H)
\]
mapping to \(\xi\).

## 5. Global Lift Implies Vanishing

Conversely, suppose \(\xi\) is the image of some \(\tilde \xi\in H^1(K,H)\). Choose a cocycle \(c_\sigma\) representing \(\tilde \xi\). Its image \(\bar c_\sigma\) represents \(\xi\), and now
\[
c_\sigma\sigma(c_\tau)c_{\sigma\tau}^{-1}=1.
\]
Thus the defining \(2\)-cocycle for \(\Delta(\xi)\) is trivial, so
\[
\Delta(\xi)=0.
\]
