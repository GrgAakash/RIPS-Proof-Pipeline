## 1. Assignment Restatement

S1 must set up the Springer/stabilizer gerbe attached to the homogeneous space \(X\) of
\[
G=\mathrm{SL}_n
\]
over \(K\), with finite geometric stabilizer. It must prove:

1. For every field extension \(F/K\), an \(F\)-point \(x\in X(F)\) gives a neutralization of the stabilizer gerbe over \(F\).
2. If the stabilizer gerbe is neutral over \(K\), then \(X(K)\neq \varnothing\), using
\[
H^1(K,\mathrm{SL}_n)=1.
\]

No global Brauer-Manin or nilpotent-filtration argument is part of this assigned subproblem.

## 2. Subproof Or Failure

Let \(G=\mathrm{SL}_n\). Since \(X\) is a homogeneous space of \(G\), over \(\bar K\) one may choose a geometric point
\[
\bar x\in X(\bar K).
\]
Let
\[
\bar H=\operatorname{Stab}_{G_{\bar K}}(\bar x).
\]
By hypothesis \(\bar H\) is finite. Over \(\bar K\) there is a \(G_{\bar K}\)-equivariant identification
\[
X_{\bar K}\simeq G_{\bar K}/\bar H.
\]

The descent datum on \(X_{\bar K}\) does not necessarily descend \(\bar H\) as a subgroup of \(G\), but it determines its \(K\)-kernel/link, usually called the stabilizer link of \(X\). The associated Springer class is the class of the gerbe \(\mathcal G_X\) whose objects over an extension \(F/K\) are, informally, \(F\)-forms of the geometric stabilizer together with compatible descent data realizing \(X_F\) as a homogeneous space of \(G_F\) with that stabilizer. This is the standard stabilizer gerbe attached to a homogeneous space.

More concretely, over \(F\), an object of \(\mathcal G_X(F)\) may be represented by a point \(y\in X(\bar F)\), its stabilizer
\[
H_y=\operatorname{Stab}_{G_{\bar F}}(y),
\]
and descent data compatible with the \(F\)-structure on \(X_F\). The gerbe is neutral over \(F\) if such stabilizer descent data is represented by an actual \(F\)-form in the usual nonabelian sense.

Now suppose \(x\in X(F)\). Then \(x\) is fixed by \(\Gamma_F\), and its stabilizer
\[
H_x=\operatorname{Stab}_{G_F}(x)
\]
is an \(F\)-subgroup scheme of \(G_F\). After base change to \(\bar F\), \(H_{x,\bar F}\) is conjugate to \(\bar H_{\bar F}\). Thus \(x\) supplies an \(F\)-defined representative of the stabilizer link. Hence \(x\) gives an object of \(\mathcal G_X(F)\). Therefore:

\[
X(F)\neq \varnothing \implies \mathcal G_X(F)\text{ is neutral}.
\]

In particular, the assumed local points \(X(K_v)\neq \varnothing\) give neutralizations of \(\mathcal G_X\) over every completion \(K_v\).

Conversely, suppose \(\mathcal G_X\) is neutral over \(K\). Then there exists a \(K\)-form \(H\) of the geometric stabilizer and a \(G\)-equivariant presentation of \(X\) as a twist of \(G/H\). Equivalently, the quotient map over \(\bar K\),
\[
G_{\bar K}\to X_{\bar K},
\]
descends to a \(G\)-torsor
\[
P\to \operatorname{Spec} K
\]
whose contracted quotient gives the given homogeneous space \(X\). A \(K\)-point of \(P\) maps to a \(K\)-point of \(X\).

The obstruction to finding such a \(K\)-point on \(P\) is precisely the cohomology class
\[
[P]\in H^1(K,G).
\]
Here \(G=\mathrm{SL}_n\). By the standard theorem
\[
H^1(K,\mathrm{SL}_n)=1,
\]
for a number field \(K\), every \(\mathrm{SL}_n\)-torsor over \(K\) is trivial. Hence \(P(K)\neq \varnothing\). Choosing \(p\in P(K)\), its image in \(X\) is a \(K\)-rational point. Therefore:

\[
\mathcal G_X(K)\text{ neutral} \implies X(K)\neq \varnothing.
\]

This proves the assigned S1 claim.
