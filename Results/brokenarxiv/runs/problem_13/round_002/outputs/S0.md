1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Main theorem.

target_type:
Local-global existence theorem for rational points on homogeneous spaces.

main_goal:
Prove that the stated Brauer-theoretic hypothesis forces the descent/cohomological obstruction to a global rational point to vanish.

variables_and_parameters:
K: number field.  
G = SL_n over K.  
X: homogeneous space of G over K.  
\bar H: finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer of X over \bar K.  
Br_nr,alg(X) := ker(Br_nr(X) -> Br_nr(X_{\bar K})).  
(x_v): adelic family of local points in X(K_v).  
H_i, A_i: terms and central abelian quotients in a nilpotent stabilizer filtration.  
Delta_i: central lifting obstruction classes in Sha^2(K,A_i).  
chi: element of Sha^1(K,A_i^D), where A_i^D is Cartier dual.

conclusion_to_prove:
X(K) is nonempty.

2. Available tools

tool:
Local point gives local neutralization of the Springer/stabilizer gerbe.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a homogeneous space X of a connected linear algebraic group G with finite geometric stabilizer \bar H, X(K_v) gives a neutralization of the local gerbe/stabilizer class attached to X over K_v.

intended_role_in_proof:
Translate the hypothesis X(K_v) nonempty for all v into local triviality of the global obstruction class.

tool:
Nilpotent central filtration.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
A finite nilpotent group admits a finite filtration by normal subgroups whose successive quotients are finite central abelian groups in the corresponding quotient.

intended_role_in_proof:
Reduce the global neutralization problem for the finite nilpotent stabilizer to successive central finite abelian lifting problems.

tool:
Nonabelian obstruction sequence for a central extension.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a central extension 1 -> A -> H -> \bar H -> 1 with A finite abelian, a locally liftable \bar H-object has a lifting obstruction Delta(xi) in Sha^2(K,A); the obstruction vanishes exactly when the object lifts globally.

intended_role_in_proof:
Identify the obstruction at each central filtration step.

tool:
Poitou-Tate duality for finite Galois modules.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For finite commutative K-group A, the pairing Sha^2(K,A) x Sha^1(K,A^D) -> Q/Z is perfect.

intended_role_in_proof:
Show that a central lifting obstruction Delta vanishes once all pairings with chi in Sha^1(K,A^D) vanish.

tool:
Central abelian obstruction/Brauer-Manin compatibility.

source_status:
proved inside the current proof.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For each central finite abelian lifting step 1 -> A -> H -> \bar H -> 1 and each chi in Sha^1(K,A^D), construct an algebraic unramified Brauer class B_chi on X such that for any adelic local neutralization (x_v) inducing local data xi_v,
sum_v inv_v(B_chi(x_v)) = <Delta(xi), chi>_PT.

intended_role_in_proof:
Convert the hypothesis Br_nr,alg(X) = im Br(K) into vanishing of all Poitou-Tate pairings.

tool:
Evaluation of constant Brauer classes on adelic points.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
If B comes from Br(K), then for any adelic point (x_v), sum_v inv_v(B(x_v)) = 0 by global reciprocity.

intended_role_in_proof:
Since every B_chi is assumed constant, its Brauer-Manin sum vanishes.

tool:
Strong approximation/trivial torsor obstruction for SL_n.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
SL_n is connected, simply connected, special, and has H^1(F,SL_n)=1 for fields F; once the stabilizer gerbe of X is globally neutral, the corresponding homogeneous space has a K-point.

intended_role_in_proof:
Convert global neutralization of the stabilizer data into an actual rational point on X.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC4.

hardest_step_description:
The proof must construct the Brauer class B_chi for a central finite abelian lifting step, prove it is algebraic and unramified on X, and identify its local evaluation sum with the Poitou-Tate pairing against the obstruction Delta_i.

risk_if_wrong:
If B_chi is not genuinely unramified, or if the sign/functoriality in the pairing is wrong, the Brauer hypothesis cannot be applied and the proof collapses.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
Treat SC4 as a standalone proposition inside the proof. Define B_chi from the cup product/torsor-gerbe data, check independence of choices, verify unramifiedness by residue calculations or extension over codimension-one local rings, then compare local invariants with the standard Poitou-Tate pairing formula, fixing conventions explicitly.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not use the target theorem or any equivalent “Brauer obstruction is the only obstruction for nilpotent stabilizers” statement. The proof must derive the result through central abelian steps.

full_theorem_check:
The blueprint proves existence of a K-point, not merely Brauer-Manin nonemptiness, by converting vanishing obstructions into global neutrality and then into a rational point.

source_check:
No external theorem specific to this target is allowed. Deep but standard background must be named precisely: nonabelian cohomology for central extensions, Poitou-Tate duality, and the elementary specialness of SL_n.

hypothesis_check:
Use local solvability to place each obstruction in Sha^2. Use nilpotence only to get central abelian filtration. Use the Brauer hypothesis exactly for classes in Br_nr,alg(X).

notation_check:
Clearly distinguish geometric stabilizer \bar H from quotient stabilizers H_i. Define A_i^D as Cartier dual. Define Br_nr,alg(X) as the kernel appearing in the theorem.

standard_background_check:
Poitou-Tate is used only for finite commutative K-groups. The SL_n fact is used only after global neutrality, via H^1(K,SL_n)=1/specialness.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Set up the stabilizer gerbe/Springer class for X under SL_n. Prove local points give local neutralizations and global neutrality gives X(K), using H^1(K,SL_n)=1.

S2:
Construct a central filtration of the finite nilpotent geometric stabilizer compatible with the K-kernel action. Show induction through this filtration reduces the theorem to central finite abelian lifting steps.

S3:
For one central extension 1 -> A -> H -> \bar H -> 1, define the obstruction Delta(xi) in Sha^2(K,A). Prove vanishing is equivalent to existence of a global lift.

S4:
Prove the central abelian obstruction/Brauer-Manin compatibility required by the guidance: construct B_chi, prove B_chi lies in Br_nr,alg(X), and prove the invariant-sum formula with the Poitou-Tate pairing.

S5:
Use the theorem’s assumption that Br_nr,alg(X) is constant, global reciprocity, and Poitou-Tate perfectness to kill each Delta. Then assemble the induction and conclude X(K) nonempty.
