1. Assignment restatement

S-ID: S2

assigned subclaim(s): SC2

what must be proved: For an arbitrary admissible lift \(\mathbf X\), define the level-2 discrepancy against the canonical lift by
\[
A^{ij}_{s,t}:=\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}-\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij}.
\]
Then \(A\) is local, square-integrable, \(2\eta\)-Hölder in interval increments, additive in the Chen sense, and antisymmetric: \(A^{ij}_{s,t}=-A^{ji}_{s,t}\), hence \(A^{ii}_{s,t}=0\).

declared prerequisite subclaims: SC1, specifically that \(\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can}}\) is also a local, square-integrable, \(\eta\)-Hölder rough path lift with first level \(X\).

2. Subproof or failure

Let \(N=\lfloor 1/H\rfloor\). Since \(H\in(1/4,1/2]\), we have \(N\ge 2\), so level-2 coordinates are defined.

Work on the common full-measure event on which both \(\mathbf X\) and \(\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can}}\) are \(G^N(\mathbb R^d)\)-valued \(\eta\)-Hölder rough paths and both have first level \(X\). For \(i,j\in\{1,\dots,d\}\), set
\[
A^{ij}_{s,t}:=\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}-\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij},
\qquad 0\le s\le t\le 1.
\]

Locality: Let \(\mathcal F^{ij}_{s,t}\) be the sigma-algebra generated by the increments
\[
u\mapsto X_u^i-X_s^i,\qquad u\mapsto X_u^j-X_s^j,\qquad u\in[s,t].
\]
By admissibility of \(\mathbf X\), \(\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}\) is \(\mathcal F^{ij}_{s,t}\)-measurable. By SC1, \(\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij}\) is also \(\mathcal F^{ij}_{s,t}\)-measurable. Hence their difference \(A^{ij}_{s,t}\) is \(\mathcal F^{ij}_{s,t}\)-measurable.

Square integrability: By the square-integrability assumption on \(\mathbf X\), \(\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}\in L^2(\Omega)\). By SC1, \(\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij}\in L^2(\Omega)\). Since \(L^2(\Omega)\) is a vector space, \(A^{ij}_{s,t}\in L^2(\Omega)\).

\(\eta\)-regularity: Since both \(\mathbf X\) and \(\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can}}\) are \(\eta\)-Hölder rough paths, their level-2 components satisfy pathwise estimates
\[
|\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}|\le C(\omega)|t-s|^{2\eta},
\qquad
|\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij}|\le C_{\mathrm{can}}(\omega)|t-s|^{2\eta}.
\]
Therefore
\[
|A^{ij}_{s,t}|
\le \bigl(C(\omega)+C_{\mathrm{can}}(\omega)\bigr)|t-s|^{2\eta}.
\]
Thus \(A^{ij}\) inherits \(2\eta\)-Hölder increment regularity.

Chen additivity: For \(0\le s\le u\le t\le 1\), the level-2 Chen relation gives
\[
\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}
=
\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,u}
+\mathbf X^{ij}_{u,t}
+X^i_{s,u}X^j_{u,t},
\]
because the first level of \(\mathbf X\) is \(X\). The same identity holds for \(\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can}}\):
\[
\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij}
=
\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,u}^{ij}
+\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},u,t}^{ij}
+X^i_{s,u}X^j_{u,t}.
\]
Subtracting the two identities cancels the cross term and yields
\[
A^{ij}_{s,t}=A^{ij}_{s,u}+A^{ij}_{u,t}.
\]

Antisymmetry: Since \(\mathbf X_{s,t}\in G^N(\mathbb R^d)\), the level-2 shuffle identity gives
\[
\mathbf X^{ij}_{s,t}+\mathbf X^{ji}_{s,t}
=
\mathbf X^i_{s,t}\mathbf X^j_{s,t}
=
X^i_{s,t}X^j_{s,t}.
\]
The same identity holds for the canonical lift:
\[
\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ij}
+\mathbf X_{\mathrm{can},s,t}^{ji}
=
X^i_{s,t}X^j_{s,t}.
\]
Subtracting gives
\[
A^{ij}_{s,t}+A^{ji}_{s,t}=0.
\]
Hence \(A^{ij}_{s,t}=-A^{ji}_{s,t}\). In particular, taking \(i=j\) gives \(2A^{ii}_{s,t}=0\), so \(A^{ii}_{s,t}=0\).

Thus the level-2 discrepancy is local, square-integrable, \(2\eta\)-Hölder, Chen-additive, and antisymmetric.
