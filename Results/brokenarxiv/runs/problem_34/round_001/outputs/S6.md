FINAL PROOF NOT COMPLETED. The blocking point is that the target theorem is false as stated.

1. Take \(d=2\) and \(H=1/3\). Then
\[
N=\lfloor 1/H\rfloor=3.
\]
Let \(\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}\) be the canonical step-3 lift.

2. Let \(e_1,e_2\) be the standard basis of \(\mathbb R^2\). In the homogeneous degree-3 part of the step-3 free Lie algebra define
\[
C=[e_1,[e_1,e_2]],\qquad D=[e_2,[e_1,e_2]],\qquad L=C-D.
\]
Every homogeneous degree-3 Lie element is central in the step-3 nilpotent Lie algebra.

3. [KEY STEP] Let \(\tau\) be the coordinate transposition exchanging \(e_1\) and \(e_2\). Then
\[
\tau_*C=[e_2,[e_2,e_1]]=-[e_2,[e_1,e_2]]=-D,
\]
and
\[
\tau_*D=[e_1,[e_2,e_1]]=-[e_1,[e_1,e_2]]=-C.
\]
Therefore
\[
\tau_*L=\tau_*(C-D)=(-D)-(-C)=C-D=L.
\]
Thus \(L\) is invariant under the nontrivial coordinate permutation of \(\mathbb R^2\).

4. Fix any \(c\ne 0\). Define modified increments by
\[
\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{s,t}
=
\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}_{s,t}\exp(c(t-s)L),
\qquad 0\le s\le t\le 1.
\]
Since \(L\) is central and homogeneous of degree \(3\), this modification leaves the first and second levels unchanged and changes only the third level by the deterministic additive correction \(c(t-s)L\).

5. Chen multiplicativity is preserved. Indeed, using centrality of \(L\) and Chen’s relation for \(\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}\),
\[
\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{s,u}\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{u,t}
=
\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}_{s,u}\exp(c(u-s)L)
\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}_{u,t}\exp(c(t-u)L)
=
\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}_{s,t}\exp(c(t-s)L)
=
\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{s,t}.
\]

6. The modified lift has first level \(X\), because the correction is purely degree \(3\). Its components are local: the only new term is deterministic and hence measurable with respect to every interval-increment sigma-algebra. It is square-integrable for the same reason, together with square-integrability of the canonical lift.

7. The added third-level term is compatible with \(\eta\)-Hölder rough path regularity for every \(\eta<1/3\), since
\[
|c(t-s)L| \lesssim |t-s| = |t-s|^{3(1/3)}
\]
and \(3\eta<1\). The target hypotheses impose \(\eta\in(0,H)\), so here \(\eta<1/3\).

8. Translation invariance in law is preserved because the correction depends only on \(t-s\), and the canonical lift is translation invariant in law.

9. Scaling invariance in law is preserved at level \(3\), because when \(H=1/3\),
\[
(t-s)^{3H}=t-s.
\]
The first and second levels are unchanged, and the canonical lift already has the required scaling.

10. Coordinate-permutation invariance is preserved for \(d=2\), because the only nontrivial permutation is \(\tau\), and Step 3 proved \(\tau_*L=L\). The canonical lift is coordinate-permutation invariant in law, and the deterministic correction is fixed by the permutation.

11. Therefore \(\widetilde{\mathbf X}\) is a local, square-integrable \(\eta\)-Hölder rough path lift satisfying the stated translation, scaling, and coordinate-permutation invariance properties, but
\[
\widetilde{\mathbf X}_{s,t}^{(3)}
\ne
\mathbf X^{\mathrm{can}}_{s,t}{}^{(3)}
\]
whenever \(c\ne0\) and \(s<t\). Hence the canonical lift is not unique under the stated assumptions.

Thus the target theorem cannot be proved from the supplied assumptions, because it is false.
