## 1 Assignment Restatement

Prove the reverse implication of the target theorem using the prerequisites:

- **SC3:** IDF + almost atomic implies atomic.
- **SC4:** IDF + atomic implies finite factorizations.

Goal: If `R` has the IDF property and is almost atomic, then `R` is an FFD.

## 2 Subproof or Failure

Assume `R` has the IDF property and is almost atomic.

By **SC3**, `R` is atomic. Hence every nonzero element of `R` is atomic. In particular, every nonzero nonunit of `R` has at least one factorization into finitely many atoms, since it cannot satisfy the “unit” alternative in the definition of atomic.

Now `R` is both IDF and atomic. By **SC4**, every nonzero nonunit has at most finitely many factorizations into atoms, up to order and associates.

Therefore every nonzero nonunit of `R` has at least one and at most finitely many factorizations into atoms, up to order and associates. This is exactly the definition of an FFD.

So IDF + almost atomic implies `R` is an FFD.
