1. Assignment Restatement

Prove SC2 and SC3 for `N=3, j=2`: for a fixed coupon type with probability `a` and the other probabilities `b,c`, derive the probability that collector 2 is missing that type at collector 1’s completion time, then sum cyclically over `(x,y,z)` to obtain

```text
F(x,y,z)=3+x^2+y^2+z^2
 - (x^2+y^2)/(x+y)^2
 - (x^2+z^2)/(x+z)^2
 - (y^2+z^2)/(y+z)^2.
```

2. Subproof Or Failure

Let the distinguished type be `A`, with probability `a`, and let the other two types be `B,C`, with probabilities `b,c`. Let `T` be the first time by which all three types have appeared.

Collector 2 is missing type `A` at time `T` exactly when no duplicate `A` has appeared before stopping. Since collector 1 keeps the first `A` and passes every later `A` to collector 2, this is equivalent to saying that `A` appears exactly once among the draws up to and including time `T`.

So we compute

```text
P(A appears exactly once before completion).
```

The event fails if, before the first appearance of one of `B,C`, there are already at least two `A`s in the relevant two-type race. Use inclusion-exclusion over the two possible missing other types.

Let `E_B` be the event that, before the first `B`, at least two `A`s appear while only `A` and `C` may have appeared. Equivalently, in the sequence restricted to `{A,B}`, the first two draws from `{A,B}` are both `A`. Thus

```text
P(E_B) = (a/(a+b))^2 = a^2/(a+b)^2.
```

Similarly,

```text
P(E_C) = a^2/(a+c)^2.
```

Both `E_B` and `E_C` occur exactly when the first two relevant races against both `B` and `C` are won by `A`; in the original process this means the first two actual draws are both `A`. Hence

```text
P(E_B ∩ E_C) = a^2.
```

Therefore the probability that `A` is a singleton by time `T`, equivalently that collector 2 is missing type `A`, is

```text
1 - P(E_B) - P(E_C) + P(E_B ∩ E_C)
= 1 - a^2/(a+b)^2 - a^2/(a+c)^2 + a^2.
```

This proves SC2.

Now set the three probabilities to `(x,y,z)`. By linearity of expectation,

```text
E[U_2^3(x,y,z)]
= P(x-type missing) + P(y-type missing) + P(z-type missing).
```

Applying the singleton formula cyclically gives

```text
F(x,y,z)
= [1 - x^2/(x+y)^2 - x^2/(x+z)^2 + x^2]
+ [1 - y^2/(x+y)^2 - y^2/(y+z)^2 + y^2]
+ [1 - z^2/(x+z)^2 - z^2/(y+z)^2 + z^2].
```

Collecting terms,

```text
F(x,y,z)
= 3 + x^2+y^2+z^2
  - (x^2+y^2)/(x+y)^2
  - (x^2+z^2)/(x+z)^2
  - (y^2+z^2)/(y+z)^2.
```

This proves SC3.
