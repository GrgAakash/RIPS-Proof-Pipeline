2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED: SC5 as stated is false.

Take \(N=3\), \(j=2\), and write \(p=(x,y,z)\). In the Poisson embedding, collector 2 is missing type \(i\) iff the second arrival time of type \(i\) is later than the first arrival times of the other two types. Thus, for \(\{i,k,\ell\}=\{1,2,3\}\),
\[
\mathbb P(i\text{ missing})
=\int_0^\infty p_i^2t e^{-p_i t}(1-e^{-p_k t})(1-e^{-p_\ell t})\,dt
\]
so
\[
F(p)=3-\sum_{r<s}\frac{p_r^2+p_s^2}{(p_r+p_s)^2}
+\frac{x^2+y^2+z^2}{(x+y+z)^2}.
\]

For \(\phi(u,v)=(u^2+v^2)/(u+v)^2\),
\[
\partial_u\phi(u,v)=\frac{2v(u-v)}{(u+v)^3}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\partial_xF-\partial_yF
=
-\frac{2(x-y)}{(x+y)^2}
-\frac{2z(x-z)}{(x+z)^3}
+\frac{2z(y-z)}{(y+z)^3}
+\frac{2(x-y)}{(x+y+z)^2}.
\]

At the interior simplex point
\[
(x,y,z)=\left(\frac12,\frac7{16},\frac1{16}\right),
\]
this gives
\[
\partial_xF-\partial_yF
=
-\frac{32}{225}-\frac{224}{729}+\frac38+\frac18
=
\frac{1841}{36450}>0.
\]
Since \(x-y=1/16>0\),
\[
(x-y)(\partial_xF-\partial_yF)>0,
\]
which contradicts the required Schur derivative inequality.
