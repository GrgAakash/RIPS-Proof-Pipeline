1. Assignment Restatement

S-ID: S2

assigned subclaim(s): SC3 and SC4

what must be proved: Starting from the Poisson-process model, derive an explicit formula for  
\(\mathbb E[U_j^N(p)]\), preferably by conditioning and inclusion-exclusion, and show that the formula is symmetric in \(p\) and continuous on the closed simplex.

declared prerequisite subclaims: SC1 and SC2, specifically: in the Poisson embedding with independent rate-\(p_i\) processes, type \(i\) is missing from collector \(j\) at stopping time iff process \(i\) has fewer than \(j\) arrivals by \(M=\max_k Y_k\), where \(Y_k\) is the first arrival time of process \(k\).

2. Subproof or Failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED: SC3 is proved on the open simplex, and symmetry plus interior continuity are proved, but SC4 as stated is false: the formula does not extend continuously to the full closed simplex.

Assume \(p_i>0\) for all \(i\). Let \(N_i(t)\) be the independent Poisson process of rate \(p_i\), let \(Y_i=Y_{i,1}\) be its first arrival time, and let \(Y_{i,j}\) be its \(j\)-th arrival time. By the declared SC2 prerequisite, type \(i\) is missing from collector \(j\) iff \(N_i(M)<j\), where \(M=\max_k Y_k\). Since \(j\ge 2\), \(Y_i<Y_{i,j}\) almost surely, so
\[
N_i(M)<j
\iff Y_{i,j}>M
\iff Y_k<Y_{i,j}\text{ for every }k\ne i.
\]

The random variable \(Y_{i,j}\) has gamma density
\[
f_{i,j}(t)=\frac{p_i^j t^{j-1}e^{-p_i t}}{(j-1)!},\qquad t>0.
\]
It is independent of \(\{Y_k:k\ne i\}\), and
\[
\mathbb P(Y_k<t)=1-e^{-p_k t}.
\]
Therefore
\[
\mathbb P(N_i(M)<j)
=
\int_0^\infty
\frac{p_i^j t^{j-1}e^{-p_i t}}{(j-1)!}
\prod_{k\ne i}(1-e^{-p_k t})\,dt.
\]
By linearity of expectation,
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E[U_j^N(p)]
=
\sum_{i=1}^N
\int_0^\infty
\frac{p_i^j t^{j-1}e^{-p_i t}}{(j-1)!}
\prod_{k\ne i}(1-e^{-p_k t})\,dt
}
\]
for \(p\) in the open simplex.

Expanding the product by inclusion-exclusion gives, with \(p_A=\sum_{k\in A}p_k\),
\[
\prod_{k\ne i}(1-e^{-p_k t})
=
\sum_{A\subseteq [N]\setminus\{i\}}(-1)^{|A|}e^{-p_A t}.
\]
Hence
\[
\mathbb P(N_i(M)<j)
=
\sum_{A\subseteq [N]\setminus\{i\}}
(-1)^{|A|}
\left(\frac{p_i}{p_i+p_A}\right)^j,
\]
and therefore
\[
\boxed{
\mathbb E[U_j^N(p)]
=
\sum_{i=1}^N
\sum_{A\subseteq [N]\setminus\{i\}}
(-1)^{|A|}
\left(\frac{p_i}{p_i+p_A}\right)^j
}
\]
on the open simplex.

This formula is symmetric: any permutation of the coordinates sends each pair \((i,A)\) bijectively to \((\sigma(i),\sigma(A))\), preserving \(|A|\) and replacing \(p_i+p_A\) with the corresponding permuted sum. It is also smooth, hence continuous, on the open simplex because every denominator \(p_i+p_A\) is positive there.

However, it does not extend continuously to the full closed simplex. Take \(N=3\), \(j\ge2\), and approach \((0,0,1)\) through
\[
p^{a,b}_\varepsilon=(a\varepsilon,b\varepsilon,1-(a+b)\varepsilon),
\qquad a,b>0.
\]
Using the integral formula, the third type contributes \(0\) in the limit, while the first two types give
\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\mathbb P(N_1(M)<j)
=
1-\left(\frac{a}{a+b}\right)^j,
\]
\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\mathbb P(N_2(M)<j)
=
1-\left(\frac{b}{a+b}\right)^j.
\]
Thus
\[
\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\mathbb E[U_j^3(p^{a,b}_\varepsilon)]
=
2-\frac{a^j+b^j}{(a+b)^j}.
\]
For example, with \(a=b=1\) this limit is \(2-2^{1-j}\), while with \(a=1,b=2\) it is
\[
2-\frac{1+2^j}{3^j}.
\]
These are different for every \(j\ge2\). Hence the interior formula has no unique limit at \((0,0,1)\), so SC4’s closed-simplex continuity assertion is false.
