1. Target Decomposition

target_label:  
Problem 36

target_type:  
Schur-concavity theorem for an expectation in a generalized coupon collector process.

main_goal:  
Show that for every fixed `N >= 2` and `j >= 2`, the function  
`p -> E[U_j^N(p)]` is Schur-concave on the probability simplex.

variables_and_parameters:  
`N`: number of coupon types, integer `N >= 2`.  
`j`: collector index, integer `j >= 2`.  
`p = (p_1, ..., p_N)`: probability vector on the `N`-simplex.  
`T`: stopping time when collector 1 has all `N` types.  
`U_j^N(p)`: number of types missing from collector `j` at time `T`.

conclusion_to_prove:  
If `p` is majorized by `q`, then  
`E[U_j^N(p)] >= E[U_j^N(q)]`.  
Equivalently, the expected deficit is Schur-concave, hence it is maximized at the uniform vector `(1/N, ..., 1/N)`.

2. Available Tools

tool: Count interpretation of the siblings process  
source_status: provided definition / notation / assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: Collector `j` has type `i` at stopping time iff coupon type `i` has appeared at least `j` times by the time every type has appeared once. Thus type `i` is missing from collector `j` iff its total count at stopping time is `< j`.  
intended_role_in_proof: Converts the process into probabilities involving coupon counts at the first-completion time.

tool: Exponential/Poisson embedding  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: IID coupon draws with probabilities `p_i` may be embedded into independent Poisson processes of rates `p_i`; arrival order has the same distribution as the discrete coupon sequence.  
intended_role_in_proof: Gives independent first-arrival times and simplifies the event that one type has fewer than `j` arrivals before all types have appeared.

tool: Inclusion-exclusion for the maximum of independent exponential times  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: If `Y_i ~ Exp(p_i)` independently, then probabilities involving `max_i Y_i` may be expanded using products such as `prod_{k != i}(1 - e^{-p_k t})`, then inclusion-exclusion over subsets.  
intended_role_in_proof: Produces an explicit symmetric formula for `E[U_j^N(p)]`.

tool: Symmetry criterion for Schur-concavity  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: A continuously differentiable symmetric function `F` on the positive simplex is Schur-concave iff `(p_a - p_b)(partial_a F - partial_b F) <= 0` for all pairs `a,b`; boundary cases follow by continuity.  
intended_role_in_proof: Reduces Schur-concavity to a pairwise derivative inequality.

tool: Integral representation of the expected deficit  
source_status: must be proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact: For `j >= 2`,  
`E[U_j^N(p)]` can be written as a symmetric sum/integral over types involving the probability that type `i` has fewer than `j` arrivals by the time all other types have arrived.  
intended_role_in_proof: Provides a workable analytic expression whose Schur-concavity can be checked.

tool: Pairwise averaging/T-transform characterization of majorization  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: A symmetric function is Schur-concave iff it does not decrease under replacing two unequal coordinates by a value closer to their average while keeping all other coordinates fixed.  
intended_role_in_proof: Alternative route to proving Schur-concavity if the derivative criterion is technically cumbersome.

tool: Uniform vector is majorized by every probability vector  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: For every probability vector `p`, `(1/N, ..., 1/N)` is majorized by `p`.  
intended_role_in_proof: Once Schur-concavity is known, immediately yields the maximum at the uniform distribution.

3. Subclaim Support Graph

id: SC1  
statement: At the stopping time, `U_j^N(p) = sum_{i=1}^N 1{C_i(T) < j}`, where `C_i(T)` is the total number of coupons of type `i` drawn by time `T`.  
uses_prior_subclaims: none  
purpose: Converts the theorem to a sum of missing-type probabilities.  
status: must be proved in final proof.  
suggested_solver: S1.

id: SC2  
statement: Under the independent Poisson-process embedding with rates `p_i`, the law of `U_j^N(p)` is unchanged, and type `i` is missing from collector `j` iff the process of type `i` has fewer than `j` arrivals by time `M = max_k Y_k`, where `Y_k` is the first arrival time of type `k`.  
uses_prior_subclaims: SC1  
purpose: Replaces the discrete stopping rule by independent exponential first-arrival times.  
status: standard background plus must be proved in final proof.  
suggested_solver: S1.

id: SC3  
statement: For each `i`, an explicit expression for `P(C_i(M) < j)` is obtained by conditioning on the first arrival time or on the time of the `(j-1)`st arrival of type `i`, and expanding `prod_{k != i}(1 - e^{-p_k t})` by inclusion-exclusion.  
uses_prior_subclaims: SC2  
purpose: Produces an analytic formula for `E[U_j^N(p)] = sum_i P(C_i(M) < j)`.  
status: must be proved in final proof.  
suggested_solver: S2.

id: SC4  
statement: The formula from SC3 is symmetric in the coordinates of `p` and extends continuously to the closed simplex.  
uses_prior_subclaims: SC3  
purpose: Allows use of standard Schur-concavity criteria on the positive simplex and then extension to boundary points.  
status: must be proved in final proof.  
suggested_solver: S2.

id: SC5  
statement: On the interior of the simplex, the explicit formula satisfies the Schur derivative inequality  
`(p_a - p_b)(partial_a E[U_j^N(p)] - partial_b E[U_j^N(p)]) <= 0`  
for all `a,b`.  
uses_prior_subclaims: SC3, SC4  
purpose: Core analytic proof of Schur-concavity.  
status: must be proved in final proof.  
suggested_solver: S3.

id: SC6  
statement: By the symmetry and derivative criterion, SC5 implies that `E[U_j^N(p)]` is Schur-concave on the interior simplex, and by continuity on the whole simplex.  
uses_prior_subclaims: SC4, SC5  
purpose: Turns the analytic inequality into the theorem’s Schur-concavity conclusion.  
status: standard background plus must be proved in final proof.  
suggested_solver: S4.

id: SC7  
statement: Since the uniform vector is majorized by every probability vector, Schur-concavity implies  
`E[U_j^N(p)] <= E[U_j^N(1/N, ..., 1/N)]`.  
uses_prior_subclaims: SC6  
purpose: Establishes the stated maximum at the uniform distribution.  
status: standard background.  
suggested_solver: S5.

4. Hardest Step Prediction

hardest_step_id:  
SC5

hardest_step_description:  
The main difficulty is proving the pairwise derivative inequality from the explicit expectation formula without accidentally assuming the desired Schur-concavity. The proof must isolate two coordinates `p_a,p_b`, keep the remaining coordinates fixed, and show that moving mass from the larger coordinate to the smaller one cannot decrease the expected deficit.

risk_if_wrong:  
If the derivative inequality is only checked termwise where terms are not individually Schur-concave, the proof may fail because cancellations between inclusion-exclusion terms matter. This is the most likely source of an invalid proof.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:  
The final proof should either derive a clean pairwise expression whose sign is manifest, or use a two-coordinate averaging argument directly at the probabilistic/integral level. It should explicitly treat the fixed remaining coordinates and verify the sign for arbitrary `p_a,p_b > 0`.

5. Failure-Mode Checks

circularity_check:  
Do not use the theorem itself or any known result specific to the siblings coupon collector problem as a supporting lemma. Schur-concavity must be proved from the derived formula or direct pairwise averaging.

full_theorem_check:  
The proof must cover all integers `N >= 2` and `j >= 2`, all probability vectors on the closed simplex, and both conclusions: Schur-concavity and maximality at the uniform distribution.

source_check:  
Only the target statement, standard probability facts, standard majorization facts, and lemmas proved inside the current proof are available.

hypothesis_check:  
The argument must not silently assume all `p_i` are distinct or positive without later extending to zero coordinates by continuity or approximation.

notation_check:  
Clearly distinguish the stopping time `T` in discrete draws from the continuous Poisson stopping time `M`; define coupon counts `C_i` before using them.

standard_background_check:  
Poisson embedding, inclusion-exclusion, Schur derivative criterion, and majorization by the uniform vector are acceptable standard background, but any coupon-collector-specific formula must be derived.

6. Subproblem Assignment Table

S1:  
Prove SC1 and SC2. Formalize the relationship between the sibling passing rule and global coupon counts, then justify the independent Poisson-process embedding and the equivalence of the stopping rule.

S2:  
Prove SC3 and SC4. Starting from the Poisson model, derive an explicit symmetric integral or finite-sum formula for `E[U_j^N(p)]`, and show it is continuous on the closed simplex.

S3:  
Prove SC5. Use the formula from S2 to establish the pairwise Schur derivative inequality for two arbitrary coordinates in the interior simplex.

S4:  
Prove SC6. State and apply the standard Schur-concavity derivative criterion, then extend the result from strictly positive probabilities to the full closed simplex.

S5:  
Prove SC7 and assemble the theorem-level conclusion. Use the fact that the uniform vector is majorized by every probability vector and conclude the expected deficit is maximized at uniform.
