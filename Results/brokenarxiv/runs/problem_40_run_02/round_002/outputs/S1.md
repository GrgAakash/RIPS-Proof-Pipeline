2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED.

The obstacle is that the prompt does not supply a precise formal statement of the finite binary block coding lemma: no exact quantifiers, no relation between the dyadic interval and the allowed exponent interval, no definition of “scale,” no required endpoint behavior, and no permitted slack/buffer region are specified.

Under the natural interpretation suggested by the S0 blueprint, one would need something like:

For every sufficiently large \(M\) and sufficiently long interval \([M,N)\), there is a finite set \(B\) of distinct exponents, locally lying near \([M,N)\), such that
\[
\sum_{b\in B} b2^{-b}=\sum_{j=M}^{N-1}2^{-j}
\]
and
\[
|B|=O((N-M)/\log M).
\]

But this natural version has immediate endpoint constraints if \(B\subseteq [M,N-1]\). For example, multiplying by \(2^{N-1}\), the coefficient of the least significant binary place \(2^{-(N-1)}\) can only be affected by the exponent \(b=N-1\), and only when \(N-1\) is odd. Thus arbitrary endpoints cannot be handled without either choosing endpoints carefully or allowing exponents beyond the target interval. Similar low-bit congruence constraints appear at the last several binary places. Therefore an exact proof depends essentially on the missing formal interface of the lemma.
