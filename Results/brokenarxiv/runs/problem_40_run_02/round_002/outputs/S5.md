1. Assignment Restatement

Assume there is an infinite set \(A\subset \mathbb Z_{>0}\) such that
\[
A(N):=\#\{a\in A:a\le N\}=O(N/\log N)
\]
and
\[
\sum_{a\in A} a2^{-a}=r
\]
for some rational \(r>0\). Enumerate \(A\) increasingly as
\[
a_1<a_2<\cdots,
\]
prove \(a_n/n\to\infty\), prove
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n2^{-a_n}=r,
\]
and state the conditional assembly of the target theorem.

2. Subproof or Failure

Let \(A\subset \mathbb Z_{>0}\) be infinite. Since every nonempty subset of \(\mathbb Z_{>0}\) has a least element, \(A\) has a unique increasing enumeration
\[
a_1<a_2<\cdots
\]
with \(\{a_n:n\ge 1\}=A\). Also \(a_n\ge n\), so \(a_n\to\infty\).

By the hypothesis \(A(N)=O(N/\log N)\), there are constants \(C>0\) and \(N_0\ge 2\) such that for every integer \(N\ge N_0\),
\[
A(N)\le C\frac{N}{\log N}.
\]
For \(n\) large enough, \(a_n\ge N_0\). Since \(a_n\) is the \(n\)-th smallest element of \(A\), exactly \(n\) elements of \(A\) are \(\le a_n\), hence
\[
A(a_n)=n.
\]
Therefore, for all sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
n\le C\frac{a_n}{\log a_n}.
\]
Rearranging gives
\[
\frac{a_n}{n}\ge \frac{1}{C}\log a_n.
\]
Because \(a_n\to\infty\), we have \(\log a_n\to\infty\), and hence
\[
\frac{a_n}{n}\to\infty.
\]

It remains to compare the sums. For \(N\ge 1\),
\[
\sum_{\substack{a\in A\\ a\le N}} a2^{-a}
=
\sum_{\substack{n\ge 1\\ a_n\le N}} a_n2^{-a_n},
\]
because \(a_1,a_2,\dots\) is precisely the increasing enumeration of \(A\). Letting \(N\to\infty\), and using the assumed convergence
\[
\sum_{a\in A}a2^{-a}=r,
\]
we obtain
\[
\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n2^{-a_n}=r.
\]

Thus, conditionally on the existence of such an infinite set \(A\) and rational \(r>0\), the increasing enumeration of \(A\) satisfies all remaining requirements of the target theorem.
