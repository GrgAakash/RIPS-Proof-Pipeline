## 1. Subproblem

Prove the S4 step: for any fixed basepoint `x0 ∈ M`, Bishop-Gromov comparison gives

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))\le \omega_n R^n \qquad \forall R>0,
\]

and combining this with S3’s lower bound

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))\ge \omega_n R^n \qquad \forall R>0
\]

forces equality for every radius.

## 2. Inputs Used

Assume:

\[
(M,g)
\]

is complete, noncompact, `dim M = n`, and

\[
\operatorname{Ric}_g \ge 0.
\]

From S3, use the previously established lower volume bound:

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))\ge \omega_n R^n
\]

for every `R > 0`.

Here \(\omega_n\) denotes the Euclidean volume of the unit ball in \(\mathbb R^n\).

## 3. Bishop-Gromov Upper Bound

By the standard Bishop-Gromov volume comparison theorem, on a complete Riemannian manifold with \(\operatorname{Ric}_g\ge 0\), the function

\[
R \mapsto \frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))}{R^n}
\]

is nonincreasing on \((0,\infty)\).

Moreover, by the smooth local Euclidean asymptotic of geodesic balls,

\[
\lim_{R\downarrow 0}\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))}{R^n}=\omega_n.
\]

Since the volume ratio is nonincreasing as \(R\) increases, for every \(R>0\),

\[
\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))}{R^n}
\le
\lim_{r\downarrow 0}\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_r(x_0))}{r^n}
=
\omega_n.
\]

Therefore,

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))\le \omega_n R^n
\qquad \forall R>0.
\]

## 4. Combination With S3

S3 gives the reverse inequality:

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))\ge \omega_n R^n
\qquad \forall R>0.
\]

Together with Bishop-Gromov,

\[
\omega_n R^n
\le
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))
\le
\omega_n R^n.
\]

Hence

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))=\omega_n R^n
\qquad \forall R>0.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\frac{\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))}{\omega_n R^n}=1
\qquad \forall R>0.
\]

## 5. Output Claim

For the basepoint \(x_0\) used in S3,

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))=\omega_n R^n \quad \text{for every } R>0.}
\]

Thus the Bishop-Gromov volume ratio is identically equal to its Euclidean value.

## 6. Integration Note

This proves SC5 and SC6. The next step, assigned to S5, is to apply the equality case of Bishop-Gromov: equality

\[
\operatorname{Vol}(B_R(x_0))=\omega_n R^n
\quad \forall R>0
\]

forces \((M,g)\) to be globally isometric to \(\mathbb R^n\).
