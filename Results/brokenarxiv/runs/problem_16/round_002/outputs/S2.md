## 1. Assignment Restatement

Prove only S2: in the finite local splitting setup with \(L=K_w\), \(D=\mathrm{Gal}(K_w/\mathbb Q_p)\), and \(X=X^*(T_K)\), under the standard identification
\[
T(\mathbb Q_p)=\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,L^*),
\]
the subgroup \(T(\mathbb Z_p)\), defined as the maximal compact subgroup of \(T(\mathbb Q_p)\), equals
\[
\ker\bigl(v_w:\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,L^*)\to \mathrm{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z)\bigr).
\]

## 2. Subproof or Failure

Let \(L=K_w\). Write \(\mathcal O_L\) for its valuation ring and \(\mathcal O_L^*\) for its unit group. The valuation \(v_w:L^*\to \mathbb Z\) is \(D\)-invariant, so it induces a homomorphism
\[
v_w:\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,L^*)\to \mathrm{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z),
\qquad f\mapsto v_w\circ f.
\]

Its kernel is exactly
\[
\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,\mathcal O_L^*),
\]
because \(v_w(f(x))=0\) for every \(x\in X\) if and only if \(f(x)\in \mathcal O_L^*\) for every \(x\).

Now choose a \(\mathbb Z\)-basis of the finite free lattice \(X\). Then \(\mathrm{Hom}(X,\mathcal O_L^*)\cong(\mathcal O_L^*)^r\), where \(r=\mathrm{rank}_{\mathbb Z}X\). Since \(\mathcal O_L^*\) is compact in the \(p\)-adic topology, \((\mathcal O_L^*)^r\) is compact. The \(D\)-equivariant subgroup is the fixed-point subgroup under a finite group action, hence closed. Therefore
\[
\ker(v_w)=\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,\mathcal O_L^*)
\]
is compact.

Conversely, let \(C\subseteq \mathrm{Hom}_D(X,L^*)\) be any compact subgroup. The valuation map is continuous, and \(\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z)\) is a subgroup of the finite free abelian group \(\mathrm{Hom}(X,\mathbb Z)\), hence is discrete and torsion-free. Thus \(v_w(C)\) is a compact subgroup of a discrete torsion-free abelian group. A compact subset of a discrete space is finite, and a finite subgroup of a torsion-free abelian group is zero. Hence \(v_w(C)=0\), so \(C\subseteq \ker(v_w)\).

Thus \(\ker(v_w)\) is compact and contains every compact subgroup of \(\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,L^*)\). It is therefore the maximal compact subgroup. Since \(T(\mathbb Z_p)\) is defined here as the maximal compact subgroup of \(T(\mathbb Q_p)=\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,L^*)\), we get
\[
T(\mathbb Z_p)=\ker\bigl(v_w:\mathrm{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*)\to \mathrm{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z)\bigr).
\]
