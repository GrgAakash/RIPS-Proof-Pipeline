## 2. Subproof or failure

Fix an algebraic torus \(T/\mathbb Q\) and a prime \(p\). Assume the following prerequisites have been proved for this \(T,p\): choose a finite Galois splitting field \(K/\mathbb Q\), a place \(w\mid p\), let \(G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)\), \(D=\operatorname{Gal}(K_w/\mathbb Q_p)\), and \(X=X^*(T_K)\). Under the standard identifications,
\[
T(\mathbb Q)=\operatorname{Hom}_G(X,K^*),\qquad
T(\mathbb Q_p)=\operatorname{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*),
\]
the localization map \(T(\mathbb Q)\to T(\mathbb Q_p)\) is induced by \(K^*\hookrightarrow K_w^*\). Also assume
\[
T(\mathbb Z_p)=\ker\bigl(v_w:\operatorname{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*)\to \operatorname{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z)\bigr),
\]
and the image equality
\[
\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_G(X,K^*)\to \operatorname{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z)\bigr)
=
\operatorname{im}\bigl(\operatorname{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*)\to \operatorname{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z)\bigr).
\]

First, \(T(\mathbb Z_p)T(\mathbb Q)\subseteq T(\mathbb Q_p)\): by definition \(T(\mathbb Z_p)\subseteq T(\mathbb Q_p)\), and \(T(\mathbb Q)\) maps into \(T(\mathbb Q_p)\).

Conversely, take \(t\in T(\mathbb Q_p)\), viewed as \(f\in\operatorname{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*)\). Let
\[
\alpha=v_w\circ f\in \operatorname{Hom}_D(X,\mathbb Z).
\]
By the image equality, there exists \(q\in\operatorname{Hom}_G(X,K^*)=T(\mathbb Q)\) whose localized valuation is also \(\alpha\). Then in \(\operatorname{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*)\),
\[
v_w(fq^{-1})=v_w(f)-v_w(q)=\alpha-\alpha=0.
\]
Hence \(fq^{-1}\in T(\mathbb Z_p)\). Therefore
\[
t=(fq^{-1})q\in T(\mathbb Z_p)T(\mathbb Q).
\]
So \(T(\mathbb Q_p)\subseteq T(\mathbb Z_p)T(\mathbb Q)\), and the equality follows.
