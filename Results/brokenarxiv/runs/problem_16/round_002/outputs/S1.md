2. Subproof or Failure

By definition of algebraic torus, there is a finite extension \(L/\mathbb Q\) over which \(T\) becomes split. Replacing \(L\) by its Galois closure \(K\) over \(\mathbb Q\), the torus remains split after base change to \(K\). Thus \(K/\mathbb Q\) is finite Galois and \(T_K\) is a split torus. Put
\[
G=\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q),\qquad X=X^*(T_K).
\]
Since \(T_K\) is split, \(X\) is a finite free abelian group, and \(G\) acts naturally on \(X\) through descent.

Choose a place \(w\) of \(K\) above \(p\). Since \(K/\mathbb Q\) is finite Galois, the completion \(K_w/\mathbb Q_p\) is finite Galois, with Galois group identified with the decomposition group at \(w\). Denote this local group by
\[
D=\operatorname{Gal}(K_w/\mathbb Q_p).
\]
Because \(T_K\) is split, base change along \(K\hookrightarrow K_w\) gives that \(T_{K_w}\) is a split torus over \(K_w\). The character group \(X^*(T_{K_w})\) is canonically identified with \(X=X^*(T_K)\), and the \(D\)-action is the restriction of the Galois action through the chosen place \(w\).

Now use the standard description of points of a split torus. If \(E/F\) is a finite Galois extension splitting a torus \(S/F\), with \(\Gamma=\operatorname{Gal}(E/F)\) and \(Y=X^*(S_E)\), then evaluation of characters gives a canonical isomorphism
\[
S(E)\cong \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(Y,E^*).
\]
The Galois action on the right is
\[
(\gamma f)(y)=\gamma\bigl(f(\gamma^{-1}y)\bigr).
\]
Therefore the invariant homomorphisms are precisely those satisfying
\[
f(\gamma y)=\gamma f(y)
\]
for all \(\gamma\in\Gamma\), \(y\in Y\). By Galois descent,
\[
S(F)=S(E)^\Gamma.
\]
Hence
\[
S(F)\cong \operatorname{Hom}_\Gamma(Y,E^*).
\]

Apply this first with \(F=\mathbb Q\), \(E=K\), \(S=T\), \(\Gamma=G\), and \(Y=X\). This gives
\[
T(\mathbb Q)\cong \operatorname{Hom}_G(X,K^*).
\]

Apply the same argument with \(F=\mathbb Q_p\), \(E=K_w\), \(S=T_{\mathbb Q_p}\), \(\Gamma=D\), and \(Y=X^*(T_{K_w})\cong X\). This gives
\[
T(\mathbb Q_p)\cong \operatorname{Hom}_D(X,K_w^*).
\]

Thus the required finite Galois splitting setup and both identifications are established.
