2. **Subproof Or Failure**

I can prove the opposite containment, but not the requested reverse inclusion from only the allowed tools.

For every subgroup \(H\le D\), including nonnormal \(H\), \(L/L^H\) is Galois with group \(H\). If \(y\in Y^H\) and \(\pi_H\) is a uniformizer of \(L^H\), then
\[
y\otimes \pi_H\in (Y\otimes L^\times)^H.
\]
Corestricting to \(D\),
\[
N_{D/H}(y\otimes \pi_H)=\sum_{\sigma\in D/H}\sigma(y\otimes \pi_H)\in (Y\otimes L^\times)^D.
\]
Applying \(\nu_L\),
\[
\nu_L\bigl(N_{D/H}(y\otimes \pi_H)\bigr)
=
\sum_{\sigma\in D/H}\sigma y\cdot \operatorname{ord}_L(\pi_H)
=
e(L/L^H)N_{D/H}(y).
\]
Thus
\[
\sum_{H\le D} e(L/L^H)N_{D/H}(Y^H)
\subset \operatorname{im}(\nu_L).
\]

For the desired reverse inclusion, the valuation sequence gives
\[
1\to \mathcal O_L^\times\to L^\times\xrightarrow{\operatorname{ord}_L}\mathbb Z\to 0,
\]
hence
\[
0\to Y\otimes \mathcal O_L^\times
\to Y\otimes L^\times
\to Y\to 0.
\]
Taking \(D\)-cohomology, the image of \(\nu_L\) is
\[
\ker\bigl(Y^D\to H^1(D,Y\otimes \mathcal O_L^\times)\bigr).
\]
Hilbert 90 proves \(H^1(H,L^\times)=0\), and for \(Y=\mathbb Z\) it gives
\[
H^1(H,\mathcal O_L^\times)\cong \mathbb Z/e(L/L^H)\mathbb Z.
\]
But this does **not** characterize
\[
\ker\bigl(Y^D\to H^1(D,Y\otimes \mathcal O_L^\times)\bigr)
\]
for an arbitrary \(D\)-lattice \(Y\). Passing from the scalar case \(Y=\mathbb Z\) to general \(Y\) is exactly the missing transfer-generation step: one needs to prove that this kernel is generated by the elements
\[
e(L/L^H)N_{D/H}(Y^H).
\]
That is essentially the Nakayama valuation image theorem, or an equivalent transfer-generation theorem. I have not proved that theorem inside this artifact, so the assigned reverse inclusion is not established.
