**2 Subproof Or Failure**

Let \(X\subset \mathcal A=\mathbb R^n/\mathbb Z^n\) be the given pure \(d\)-dimensional weighted rational polyhedral complex.

Choose a finite rational polyhedral structure on \(X\). Since \(X\) is a compact rational polyhedral complex in the torus, after passing to a sufficiently fine rational subdivision, every cell is a rational polytope and the attaching maps are affine with integral slopes. Subdivision preserves the underlying topological space, pure dimension, rationality, weights on top-dimensional cells, and balancing.

A suitable PL Morse function can be obtained by choosing a rational PL function \(h\colon X\to \mathbb R\) that is affine-linear on every cell of the chosen subdivision and is generic in the following finite sense:

1. \(h\) takes distinct values on distinct vertices.
2. \(h\) is nonconstant on every positive-dimensional cell.
3. For every vertex \(p\), the restriction of \(h-h(p)\) to each ray of the local star \(\operatorname{Star}_p(X)\) has nonzero slope.
4. The covector defining the affine-linear part of \(h\) on each incident cell avoids the finitely many hyperplanes where degeneracies occur.

Such an \(h\) exists because the forbidden conditions are a finite union of proper rational affine hyperplanes in the finite-dimensional space of rational PL functions on a fixed rational subdivision. Rational points are dense, so a rational choice remains after avoiding those hyperplanes.

If necessary, refine the subdivision further by inserting all domains of linearity of \(h\). Then \(h\) is affine-linear on every cell, has no critical points in positive-dimensional cell interiors, and every possible PL critical point is a vertex.

For a vertex \(p\) of the subdivision, define the local star fan
\[
\operatorname{Star}_p(X)
\]
as the rational weighted fan in the tangent space \(T_p\mathcal A\simeq \mathbb R^n\) obtained by taking tangent cones at \(p\) to all cells of \(X\) containing \(p\), with inherited weights on \(d\)-dimensional cones.

Define the link
\[
\operatorname{Lk}(p)
\]
as the intersection of \(\operatorname{Star}_p(X)\) with a sufficiently small sphere centered at the origin in \(T_p\mathcal A\), equivalently the usual polyhedral link of \(p\) in the subdivision.

The descending star and descending link are
\[
\operatorname{Star}^-(p)=\{v\in \operatorname{Star}_p(X): dh_p(v)<0\},
\]
and
\[
\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)=\operatorname{Lk}(p)\cap \{v: dh_p(v)<0\},
\]
where \(dh_p\) denotes the linear part of \(h-h(p)\) on each cone of the local star. Genericity ensures no ray of the local star lies in \(dh_p=0\), so this descending link is a well-defined subcomplex after the chosen subdivision.

The local PL Morse index contribution at \(p\) is
\[
\iota(p)=1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)).
\]
For the signed form used later in the target theorem, define
\[
\iota_d(p)=(-1)^d\bigl(1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p))\bigr).
\]

This completes S1: the required generic rational PL Morse subdivision exists, the local star fan is defined, and the descending link and local index are defined at every vertex.
