**1 Assignment Restatement**

S4 was assigned only the local comparison lemma:

\[
(-1)^d(1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)))=m_p
\]

for a weighted balanced rational local star fan \(\Sigma=\operatorname{Star}_p(X)\), where \(m_p\) is supposed to be the local stable intersection multiplicity with a complementary affine space determined by the generic Morse covector and the positive definite form.

**2 Subproof Or Failure**

SETUP FAILURE.

The lemma cannot be proved from the packet as stated. There are two obstructions.

First, the data do not determine \(m_p\) canonically. A single generic covector \(\lambda\) and a positive definite form determine a hyperplane \(\ker \lambda\) and a gradient direction, but not in general an \((n-d)\)-dimensional complementary affine space when \(d\neq 1\). Additional choices, such as a full flag or \(d\) independent covectors, would be needed.

Second, under the standard tropical stable-intersection normalization, the stated equality is false because the left side is unweighted topological data, while \(m_p\) depends on weights and lattice indices.

Concrete obstruction: take \(d=0\). A local star is a single weighted point of weight \(w>1\), balanced vacuously. Then

\[
\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)=\varnothing,\qquad \chi(\varnothing)=0,
\]

so

\[
(-1)^0(1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)))=1.
\]

But the standard local stable intersection multiplicity of a weighted point with the ambient complementary space is \(m_p=w\). Thus the claimed equality gives \(1=w\), false for \(w>1\). The packet allows weighted tropical subvarieties, so this is not an excluded case.

Even in weight-one positive dimension, slope/lattice-index dependence is not reflected by the unweighted lower-link Euler characteristic. For example, in \(\mathbb R^2\), the balanced tropical line fan with rays

\[
(1,0),\quad (0,1),\quad (-1,-1)
\]

and covector \(\lambda(x,y)=x+2y\) has lower link consisting of one point, so the signed local Morse term for \(d=1\) is

\[
- (1-1)=0.
\]

Using the standard affine hyperplane \(\lambda=-\varepsilon\), the lower ray \((-1,-1)\) contributes lattice index

\[
|\lambda(-1,-1)|=3,
\]

so the stable-intersection multiplicity is positive, not \(0\).

Therefore the local comparison lemma, with the stated normalization, is unavailable and appears false.
