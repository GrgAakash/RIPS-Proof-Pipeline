**1 Assignment Restatement**

S3 assignment: for a vertex \(p\) in a generic PL Morse subdivision with local star fan \(\Sigma=\operatorname{Star}_p(X)\subset V=\mathbb R^n\), define the complementary affine space \(L_p\) using the Morse covector \(\xi\in V^*\) and the positive definite form \(Q\). Then define the local stable intersection multiplicity \(m_p\) by lattice indices and prove \(m_p\ge 0\).

**2 Subproof Or Failure**

Let \(V=\mathbb R^n\), \(N=\mathbb Z^n\), and let \(Q\) be the given positive definite quadratic form. Write \(B_Q\) for its associated positive definite symmetric bilinear form. Given a Morse covector \(\xi\in V^*\), define the \(Q\)-dual vector \(u_\xi\in V\) by
\[
B_Q(u_\xi,v)=\xi(v)\quad\text{for all }v\in V.
\]

Choose, generically and rationally, an \((n-d)\)-dimensional linear subspace \(W_p\subset V\) such that:

\[
V=\operatorname{span}(\sigma)+W_p
\]
for every \(d\)-dimensional cone \(\sigma\) of \(\Sigma\) that can contribute to the local stable intersection, and such that the small translate in the \(Q\)-dual Morse direction meets only expected-dimensional strata. The complementary affine space is then

\[
L_p := \varepsilon u_\xi + W_p
\]
for sufficiently small \(\varepsilon>0\). Equivalently, \(L_p\) is a small generic affine translate of the rational complementary space \(W_p\), oriented/selected on the Morse side determined by \(\xi\) via the vector \(u_\xi\).

For each top-dimensional cone \(\sigma\in\Sigma^{(d)}\) with weight \(w_\sigma\), define its lattice
\[
N_\sigma := N\cap \operatorname{span}(\sigma),
\]
and define
\[
N_{W_p}:=N\cap W_p.
\]
If \(\sigma\) contributes to the stable intersection with \(L_p\), meaning the translated affine space meets \(\sigma\) after the standard small perturbation and
\[
\operatorname{span}(\sigma)+W_p=V,
\]
then its local lattice-index contribution is
\[
w_\sigma\,[N:N_\sigma+N_{W_p}].
\]

Thus the local stable intersection multiplicity is

\[
m_p :=
\sum_{\substack{\sigma\in\Sigma^{(d)}\\ \sigma\cap L_p\neq\varnothing\ \text{stably}}}
w_\sigma\,[N:N_\sigma+N_{W_p}].
\]

Here \([N:N_\sigma+N_{W_p}]\) is a finite positive integer whenever the spanning condition holds. Tropical weights \(w_\sigma\) are nonnegative by the usual convention for weighted tropical varieties. Therefore each summand is a product of a nonnegative integer weight and a positive lattice index. Hence

\[
m_p\ge 0.
\]

This proves the S3 nonnegativity claim, conditional only on the standard setup that the Morse covector and complementary rational space have been chosen generically so that the stable intersection is zero-dimensional.
