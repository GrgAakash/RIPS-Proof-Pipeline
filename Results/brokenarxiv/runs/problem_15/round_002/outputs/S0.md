1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 15.

target_type:
Nonnegativity theorem for signed Euler characteristic of a tropical subvariety of a tropical abelian variety.

main_goal:
Prove that every pure \(d\)-dimensional tropical subvariety \(X\subset \mathcal A=\mathbb R^n/\mathbb Z^n\) satisfies
\[
(-1)^d\chi(X)\ge 0.
\]

variables_and_parameters:
\(\mathcal A=\mathbb R^n/\mathbb Z^n\); a positive definite quadratic form \(Q\) on \(\mathbb R^n\); a pure \(d\)-dimensional weighted rational balanced polyhedral complex \(X\subset \mathcal A\); top-dimensional weights; local stars \(\operatorname{Star}_p(X)\); local links and descending links for a generic PL Morse function.

conclusion_to_prove:
The signed topological Euler characteristic of the underlying space of \(X\) is nonnegative.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
C5.

hardest_step_description:
The difficult point is proving the exact equality between the signed local PL Morse index and the lattice-index stable intersection multiplicity of the local star fan with the complementary affine space determined by the Morse covector and \(Q\).

risk_if_wrong:
If the multiplicity depends on weights or lattice slopes in a way the topological Morse index does not, the equality can fail as stated. The final proof must not assume this comparison without proving the normalization carefully.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The proof must define the affine complement and multiplicity precisely, then prove the comparison locally for an arbitrary balanced rational fan. If that local statement cannot be proved from standard tropical intersection theory and PL Morse theory, the proof should report this as the obstruction rather than silently using it.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not use the target theorem, or any global Euler characteristic positivity theorem, to prove the local comparison lemma.

full_theorem_check:
The argument must prove nonnegativity for arbitrary pure-dimensional tropical subvarieties in the stated torus, not only smooth, hypersurface, or connected cases.

source_check:
No supporting theorem beyond standard PL Morse theory, standard local tropical balancing, and stable intersection definitions may be assumed. The local comparison must be proved inside the proof.

hypothesis_check:
The proof must explicitly use that tropical subvariety weights are positive integer weights. If the term “weighted” were interpreted to allow negative or arbitrary weights, the stable-intersection positivity step would be invalid.

notation_check:
Clearly distinguish \(X\), its underlying topological space, the local star fan \(\Sigma_p\), the link \(\operatorname{Lk}(p)\), the descending link \(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)\), and the complementary affine space \(L_p\).

standard_background_check:
Acceptable standard background: subdivision invariance of Euler characteristic, PL Morse formula for finite polyhedral complexes, local fan construction, balancing inherited by stars, and nonnegativity of positive weights times lattice indices. Not acceptable as standard without proof: the local Morse-index/stable-intersection equality.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Prove existence of a suitable generic rational PL Morse subdivision of \(X\). Define the local descending link and local index at each vertex.

S2:
Prove the PL Morse Euler formula for this finite polyhedral complex and verify that local stars of \(X\) are balanced weighted rational fans.

S3:
Define the complementary affine space \(L_p\) from the Morse covector and the positive definite form \(Q\). Define the lattice-index stable intersection multiplicity \(m_p\) and prove \(m_p\ge 0\).

S4:
Prove the local comparison lemma
\[
(-1)^d(1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)))=m_p.
\]
This is the central subproblem and must include all normalization details.

S5:
Assemble the proof:
\[
(-1)^d\chi(X)
=
\sum_p (-1)^d(1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^-(p)))
=
\sum_p m_p
\ge 0.
\]
Also check compactness, subdivision invariance, and edge cases \(d=0\) and \(X=\varnothing\), if allowed.
