1. Assignment restatement

S-ID: S1

assigned subclaim(s): Polyhedral Morse formula for \(X\).

what must be proved: After passing to a finite subdivision/triangulation and choosing a generic PL function \(f\), the Euler characteristic satisfies
\[
\chi(X)=\sum_{p}\operatorname{ind}_p(f),
\]
where the sum is over the finitely many vertices/critical points and the local index may be taken as
\[
\operatorname{ind}_p(f)=1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^{-}(p)).
\]
The formula is finite and independent of the chosen subdivision.

declared prerequisite subclaims: None.

2. Subproof or failure

Let \(K\) be a finite polyhedral complex whose support is \(X\). If necessary, replace \(K\) by a finite triangulation \(T\). This does not change the underlying topological space \(X\), hence does not change \(\chi(X)\).

Choose a generic PL function \(f:X\to \mathbb R\) which is affine on every simplex of \(T\) and takes distinct values on the vertices of \(T\). For example, after assigning distinct real values to the finitely many vertices in general position, extend linearly on each simplex. With this choice, no critical changes occur except when the sublevel set passes a vertex.

For a vertex \(v\in T\), define the lower link
\[
\operatorname{Lk}^{-}(v)
=
\{\sigma\in \operatorname{Lk}_T(v): f(w)<f(v)\text{ for every vertex }w\text{ of }\sigma\}.
\]
Define the local Morse index
\[
\operatorname{ind}_v(f)=1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^{-}(v)).
\]

Order the vertices \(v_1,\dots,v_N\) so that
\[
f(v_1)<f(v_2)<\cdots<f(v_N).
\]
Let \(T_i\) be the subcomplex spanned by vertices \(v_1,\dots,v_i\), and let \(|T_i|\) be its support. Since \(f\) is affine on each simplex and vertex values are distinct, the topology of the sublevel sets changes at \(f(v_i)\) exactly by adding the open star of \(v_i\) relative to \(T_i\). More precisely, \(T_i\) is obtained from \(T_{i-1}\) by adjoining the cone
\[
v_i * \operatorname{Lk}^{-}(v_i)
\]
together with its cone point \(v_i\). The intersection of this cone with \(T_{i-1}\) is precisely \(\operatorname{Lk}^{-}(v_i)\).

Euler characteristic is additive for finite CW complexes under union along a subcomplex:
\[
\chi(A\cup B)=\chi(A)+\chi(B)-\chi(A\cap B).
\]
Applying this with
\[
A=T_{i-1},\qquad B=v_i * \operatorname{Lk}^{-}(v_i),
\]
and using that every cone has Euler characteristic \(1\), we obtain
\[
\chi(T_i)-\chi(T_{i-1})
=
1-\chi(\operatorname{Lk}^{-}(v_i))
=
\operatorname{ind}_{v_i}(f).
\]
Summing over all vertices gives a telescoping identity:
\[
\chi(X)=\chi(T_N)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\bigl(\chi(T_i)-\chi(T_{i-1}\bigr)
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\operatorname{ind}_{v_i}(f),
\]
where \(T_0=\varnothing\) and \(\chi(T_0)=0\).

The sum is finite because \(T\) has finitely many vertices. Subdivision-invariance follows because the argument computes the ordinary topological Euler characteristic of the fixed space \(X\). Any finite subdivision \(T'\) gives another finite vertex sum, but the same telescoping argument gives
\[
\sum_{v\in T'}\operatorname{ind}_v(f')=\chi(X),
\]
so the total sum is independent of the chosen subdivision and generic PL function.

Thus the assigned S1 subclaim is proved.
