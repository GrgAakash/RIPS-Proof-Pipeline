1. Target decomposition

target_label: Problem 15

target_type: nonnegativity theorem for tropical subvarieties of tropical abelian varieties

main_goal: prove that every pure \(d\)-dimensional balanced weighted rational polyhedral complex \(X\subset \mathcal A\) has Euler characteristic with sign \((-1)^d\) nonnegative.

variables_and_parameters: tropical abelian variety \(\mathcal A=\mathbb R^n/\mathbb Z^n\); positive definite quadratic form \(Q\) on \(\mathbb R^n\); pure \(d\)-dimensional tropical subvariety \(X\subset \mathcal A\); topological Euler characteristic \(\chi(X)\).

conclusion_to_prove: \[
(-1)^d\chi(X)\ge 0.
\]

source_status: standard background fact

intended_role_in_proof: allows replacement of \(X\) by convenient refinements without changing \(\chi(X)\).

source_status: standard background fact / proved inside the current proof

intended_role_in_proof: reduce the Euler characteristic statement to a sum of local contributions.

tool: balancing condition  
source_status: provided assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: \(X\) is a weighted embedded rational polyhedral \(d\)-complex with integral slopes satisfying balancing along codimension-one faces.  
intended_role_in_proof: ensures local tropical cycles have well-defined stable intersections and local degrees.

source_status: provided assumption

intended_role_in_proof: convert signed local Morse indices into nonnegative local intersection numbers.

tool: local normal fan/link description  
source_status: standard background fact / proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact: the local behavior of \(X\) near a cell is encoded by the balanced fan given by the star or link of that cell.

source_status: standard background fact from the torus/polyhedral setup

intended_role_in_proof: justifies summing local contributions.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id: SC2

hardest_step_description: The delicate point is proving that the sign-corrected local Morse index equals a local stable intersection multiplicity. This requires matching topological data from the link of a critical point with tropical intersection data from the balanced star.

risk_if_wrong: If the identification has the wrong sign, misses boundary/link contributions, or uses a non-generic local model, the final nonnegativity conclusion may fail or become circular.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it: The proof should work locally in the tangent fan at a critical cell, explicitly define the complementary \(Q\)-dual affine space, compute the Morse index through the lower link, and then show that the same combinatorial determinant formula gives the stable intersection multiplicity.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check: Do not assume the target theorem or any general positivity theorem equivalent to it. Positivity of local multiplicities must be proved locally from balancing and positive definiteness.

full_theorem_check: The argument must handle arbitrary pure-dimensional weighted balanced rational polyhedral complexes in \(\mathcal A\), not only smooth or transverse examples. Subdivision and perturbation steps must preserve \(\chi(X)\) and the relevant tropical cycle.

source_check: No external theorems beyond genuinely standard background should be invoked without proof. Since no supporting statements are supplied, all tropical Morse/intersection identifications must be proved inside the current proof if they are not treated as elementary background.

hypothesis_check: The proof must use all key hypotheses: compact real torus, rational polyhedral embedded complex, pure dimension \(d\), weights, balancing, and positive definite quadratic form.

notation_check: Distinguish the ambient torus \(\mathcal A\), its universal cover \(\mathbb R^n\), the complex \(X\), local stars/links, and local multiplicities \(m_p\).

standard_background_check: Cellular Euler characteristic, subdivision invariance, finite polyhedral Morse theory, and basic determinant formulas for lattice indices may be treated as standard, but any tropical-specific positivity assertion should be proved or clearly reduced to local lattice linear algebra.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1: Prove the polyhedral Morse formula for \(X\): after subdivision and a generic PL function, \(\chi(X)\) is the sum of local Morse indices, with all sums finite and subdivision-invariant.

S2: For a local critical model, prove the equality between \((-1)^d\) times the local Morse index and the stable intersection multiplicity with the \(Q\)-dual complementary affine space.

S3: Prove that the balancing condition makes each local star a balanced fan and that the local stable intersection multiplicity used by S2 is well-defined and perturbation-invariant.

S4: Prove nonnegativity of the local intersection multiplicities using the positive definite quadratic form and lattice determinant/index formulas.

S5: Assemble S1-S4 into the global proof: sum local nonnegative multiplicities and conclude \((-1)^d\chi(X)\ge 0\).
