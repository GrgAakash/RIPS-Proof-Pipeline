2. Subproof or failure

Let \(N=\mathbb Z^n\) and \(N_{\mathbb R}=\mathbb R^n\). Let \(Q\) be the positive definite quadratic form, and let
\[
B_Q(u,v)=\frac12(Q(u+v)-Q(u)-Q(v))
\]
be its associated symmetric bilinear form. Positive definiteness means \(B_Q(v,v)>0\) for every nonzero \(v\in N_{\mathbb R}\).

For a real subspace \(V\subset N_{\mathbb R}\), define its \(Q\)-orthogonal complement by
\[
V^{\perp_Q}=\{y\in N_{\mathbb R}:B_Q(y,v)=0\text{ for all }v\in V\}.
\]
Since \(B_Q\) is positive definite, \(V\cap V^{\perp_Q}=0\): if \(v\in V\cap V^{\perp_Q}\), then \(B_Q(v,v)=0\), so \(v=0\). Also nondegeneracy gives
\[
\dim V^{\perp_Q}=n-\dim V,
\]
hence
\[
N_{\mathbb R}=V\oplus V^{\perp_Q}.
\]
Thus the \(Q\)-dual complementary space used locally is genuinely complementary; in determinant language, the relevant full-rank determinant is nonzero.

Now consider one local stable intersection term supplied by SC2. By the standard tropical stable intersection formula, after a generic perturbation a local contribution has the form
\[
m_{\sigma,L}=w_\sigma [N:N_\sigma+N_L],
\]
where:

- \(\sigma\) is a \(d\)-dimensional cone/facet of the local star of \(X\);
- \(w_\sigma\in \mathbb Z_{\ge 0}\) is its tropical weight;
- \(L\) is the complementary \(Q\)-dual rational linear space of dimension \(n-d\), with weight \(1\);
- \(N_\sigma=N\cap \operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}(\sigma)\);
- \(N_L=N\cap L\);
- transversality gives \((N_\sigma+N_L)\otimes_{\mathbb Z}\mathbb R=N_{\mathbb R}\).

Because \(N_\sigma+N_L\) has full rank in \(N\), the quotient \(N/(N_\sigma+N_L)\) is finite. Therefore
\[
[N:N_\sigma+N_L]\in \mathbb Z_{>0}.
\]
Since \(w_\sigma\in\mathbb Z_{\ge 0}\), it follows that
\[
m_{\sigma,L}=w_\sigma [N:N_\sigma+N_L]\in \mathbb Z_{\ge 0}.
\]

Equivalently, if this local term is written using bases as a determinant, it is the absolute lattice determinant
\[
w_\sigma\left|\det(u_1,\dots,u_d,\ell_1,\dots,\ell_{n-d})\right|
\]
up to the fixed covolume normalization of \(N\), where \(u_i\) span \(N_\sigma\) and \(\ell_j\) span \(N_L\). This expression is again nonnegative. If the SC2 formula records the same term through a \(Q\)-Gram determinant, then for independent vectors \(u_1,\dots,u_d\), the matrix
\[
(B_Q(u_i,u_j))_{i,j}
\]
is positive definite, hence has positive determinant. Thus the positive definite form introduces no negative local sign.

The full local multiplicity is a finite sum of such local terms:
\[
m_p=\sum_{\sigma} m_{\sigma,L}.
\]
Each summand is a nonnegative integer, so
\[
m_p\in\mathbb Z_{\ge 0}.
\]
This proves the assigned S4 subclaim, conditional only on the SC2/SC3 setup of the local stable intersection multiplicity.
