2. Subproof or failure

Assume S1-S4.

By S1, after passing to a subdivision compatible with a generic PL Morse function, the Euler characteristic of \(X\) is computed by finitely many local Morse contributions:
\[
\chi(X)=\sum_{p\in \operatorname{Crit}} \operatorname{ind}_p,
\]
where \(\operatorname{Crit}\) is finite. Multiplying by \((-1)^d\) gives
\[
(-1)^d\chi(X)=\sum_{p\in \operatorname{Crit}} (-1)^d\operatorname{ind}_p.
\]

By S2, each summand equals the corresponding local stable tropical intersection multiplicity:
\[
(-1)^d\operatorname{ind}_p=m_p.
\]
Therefore
\[
(-1)^d\chi(X)=\sum_{p\in \operatorname{Crit}} m_p.
\]

By S3, these \(m_p\) are well-defined local quantities for the chosen generic perturbation, and the value of the sum is independent of the harmless choices made in the local stable intersection construction. By S4, every \(m_p\) is a nonnegative integer. Since \(\operatorname{Crit}\) is finite, the finite sum of nonnegative integers is nonnegative. Hence
\[
(-1)^d\chi(X)=\sum_{p\in \operatorname{Crit}}m_p\ge 0.
\]

This proves the assigned assembly step, conditional on S1-S4.
