2. Subproof or failure.

Assuming S1 proves the standard obstruction:

For every self-map `f: M -> M` such that `f_*: pi_1(M) -> pi_1(M)` is an automorphism, one must have `L(f)=0`.

Reason: if `L(f) != 0`, Jiang-type gives `N(f)=R(f)`. Since `pi_1(M)` has property `R_infty` and `f_*` is an automorphism, `R(f)=infty`; but `N(f)` is finite for a self-map of a compact manifold. Contradiction.

Then applying this to `f = id_M`, whose induced map on `pi_1` is the identity automorphism and whose Lefschetz number is `L(id_M)=chi(M)`, S2 reduces the theorem to the case

```text
chi(M) = 0.
```

Thus the precise reduced target is:

```text
Reduced target.
Let M be a closed Jiang-type manifold whose fundamental group pi_1(M)
has property R_infty, and suppose chi(M)=0. Then M has no nonzero higher
homotopy groups; equivalently, pi_k(M)=0 for every k >= 2.
```

Equivalently, to finish the theorem by contradiction, it remains to rule out:

```text
M closed Jiang-type,
pi_1(M) has R_infty,
chi(M)=0,
and M is not aspherical.
```

The additional argument needed cannot merely exhibit `id_M` or a constant map, because `L(id_M)=chi(M)=0`, and a constant map does not generally induce a `pi_1`-automorphism. It also cannot assume the unavailable and false general claim that every closed non-aspherical `chi=0` manifold admits a self-map inducing a `pi_1`-automorphism with nonzero Lefschetz number.

So the missing argument must use the Jiang-type hypothesis more directly in the `chi(M)=0` case. In particular, it would need to derive a contradiction from the interaction of Nielsen, Lefschetz, and Reidemeister data for some carefully chosen self-map or class of self-maps, without relying on a general nonzero-Lefschetz automorphic self-map existence theorem.
