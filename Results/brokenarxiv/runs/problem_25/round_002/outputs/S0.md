1. Target decomposition

target_label: Problem 25

target_type: implication theorem, best proved by contradiction

main_goal: show that a closed Jiang-type manifold whose fundamental group has property `R_infty` cannot have nontrivial higher homotopy.

variables_and_parameters: `M` closed connected manifold; `G = pi_1(M)`; `f: M -> M`; `f_*` induced map on `G`; `N(f)`, `L(f)`, `R(f)` as in the statement.

conclusion_to_prove: `M` is aspherical, equivalently its universal cover is contractible, or equivalently `pi_n(M)=0` for all `n >= 2`.

2. Available tools

tool: Jiang-type dichotomy  
source_status: provided definition / notation / assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: for every self-map `f`, if `L(f)=0` then `N(f)=0`, and if `L(f) != 0` then `N(f)=R(f)`.  
intended_role_in_proof: converts any self-map with nonzero Lefschetz number into an equality between Nielsen and Reidemeister numbers.

tool: property `R_infty`  
source_status: provided definition / notation / assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: for every `phi in Aut(G)`, the number of `phi`-twisted conjugacy classes is infinite.  
intended_role_in_proof: if a self-map induces a group automorphism, its Reidemeister number is infinite.

tool: Reidemeister number of a map  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: for a connected space, `R(f)` is the number of twisted conjugacy classes of the induced homomorphism on `pi_1`, up to the usual basepoint/inner-automorphism ambiguity, which does not change the count.  
intended_role_in_proof: identifies `R(f)` with the `R_infty` condition when `f_*` is an automorphism.

tool: Nielsen number finiteness  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: for a self-map of a compact manifold, equivalently a finite CW complex, `N(f)` is a finite nonnegative integer.  
intended_role_in_proof: makes `N(f)=R(f)=infinity` impossible.

tool: Lefschetz number of the identity  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: `L(id_M)=chi(M)`.  
intended_role_in_proof: handles the easy case `chi(M) != 0`.

tool: closed manifolds as finite CW complexes  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: every closed manifold has the homotopy type of a finite CW complex.  
intended_role_in_proof: justifies using finite Nielsen theory and homological Lefschetz theory.

tool: asphericity criterion  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: a connected CW complex is aspherical iff its universal cover is contractible iff `pi_n=0` for all `n >= 2`.  
intended_role_in_proof: translates the contradiction from “not non-aspherical” to “aspherical.”

tool: remaining-case map-production lemma  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact: in the remaining case of a connected closed non-aspherical manifold with `chi(M)=0` and nontrivial `pi_1(M)`, construct a self-map `f: M -> M` such that `f_*` is an automorphism of `pi_1(M)` and `L(f) != 0`.  
intended_role_in_proof: supplies the contradiction when the identity and constant maps do not work.

tool: guidance item 1  
source_status: additional guidance item  
exact_statement_or_fact: do not rely only on `id_M` or on a constant map; the hard case is connected, closed, non-aspherical, `chi(M)=0`, nontrivial `pi_1(M)`.  
intended_role_in_proof: forces the proof to include the remaining-case map-production lemma.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id: C4

hardest_step_description: construct, in the `chi(M)=0`, nontrivial-`pi_1`, non-aspherical case, a self-map inducing a `pi_1`-automorphism and having nonzero Lefschetz number.

risk_if_wrong: the proof collapses into the invalid easy argument warned against in the guidance. The identity has Lefschetz number zero in this case, and a constant map has nonzero Lefschetz number but does not induce a `pi_1`-automorphism.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it: give an explicit, self-contained construction or lemma proof. It must verify both `f_* in Aut(pi_1(M))` and `L(f) != 0`, and it must not assume Jiang-type plus `R_infty` already imply asphericity.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check: C4 must be proved independently of the target conclusion; it cannot assume that all such Jiang-type manifolds are aspherical or use an equivalent theorem.

full_theorem_check: the proof must cover the hard case `chi(M)=0`, nontrivial `pi_1(M)`, non-aspherical `M`; identity and constant maps alone are insufficient.

source_check: no external paper results, hidden lemmas, internet lookups, or answer-key facts are allowed.

hypothesis_check: use `R_infty` only when the induced map on `pi_1` is an automorphism; use Jiang-type only for self-maps of `M`.

notation_check: distinguish `R(f)` from arbitrary twisted conjugacy counts, and note that basepoint choices change `f_*` only by inner automorphism.

standard_background_check: standard facts are limited to finite CW type of closed manifolds, finiteness of Nielsen number on compact manifolds, `L(id)=chi`, and the usual asphericity criterion.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1: Prove C1. Show that if `f_*` is an automorphism and `L(f) != 0`, then property `R_infty` gives `R(f)=infinity`, Jiang-type gives `N(f)=R(f)`, but Nielsen finiteness gives finite `N(f)`, contradiction.

S2: Prove C2 and C3. Use `L(id_M)=chi(M)` and C1 to force `chi(M)=0`. Also record that trivial `pi_1` cannot have property `R_infty`, and that constant maps are unusable in the nontrivial `pi_1` case because they do not induce automorphisms.

S3: Prove C4. In the reduced non-aspherical case, construct a self-map with induced `pi_1`-automorphism and nonzero Lefschetz number. This is the main technical subproblem and must be fully justified inside the proof.

S4: Prove C5 and C6. Assemble the contradiction: non-asphericity plus reductions gives the S3 map, while S1 forbids it. Conclude asphericity using the standard universal-cover/higher-homotopy criterion.

S5: Audit the final proof for circularity, especially around C4; check that no unstated supporting theorem equivalent to the target has been imported.
