1. Assignment restatement.

Prove C4: if `M` is connected, closed, non-aspherical, `chi(M)=0`, and `pi_1(M)` is nontrivial, then there is a self-map `f: M -> M` such that `f_*` is an automorphism of `pi_1(M)` and `L(f) != 0`.

2. Subproof or failure.

I cannot prove C4 as stated, because it is false in this level of generality.

Obstruction/counterexample pattern: let `B` be a closed orientable aspherical rational homology 3-sphere with no orientation-reversing self-homotopy equivalence and with `Out(pi_1 B)` trivial. Standard hyperbolic 3-manifold theory supplies such examples: closed asymmetric chiral hyperbolic rational homology 3-spheres; by Mostow-Prasad rigidity, automorphisms of `pi_1(B)` are realized by isometries, so trivial isometry group gives trivial outer automorphism group.

Set

`M = S^2 x B`.

Then:

- `M` is connected and closed.
- `pi_1(M) = pi_1(B)` is nontrivial.
- `M` is non-aspherical, since its universal cover is `S^2 x \widetilde B`, homotopy equivalent to `S^2`.
- `chi(M)=chi(S^2) chi(B)=2*0=0`, since every closed odd-dimensional manifold has Euler characteristic `0`.

Now let `f: M -> M` be any self-map such that `f_*` is an automorphism of `pi_1(M)=pi_1(B)`. Let `p: M -> B` be projection. Since `B` is aspherical, maps into `B` are determined up to free homotopy by the induced homomorphism on fundamental groups. Because every automorphism of `pi_1(B)` is inner, `p o f` is freely homotopic to `p`.

With rational cohomology, write

`H^*(M;Q) = Q[1,u,v,uv]`

where `|u|=2`, `|v|=3`, `u` is the `S^2` class and `v=p^*[B]^*`. Since `p o f ≃ p`, we have `f^*(v)=v`. Also `H^2(M;Q)=Q u`, so `f^*(u)=a u` for some `a in Q`. Hence `f^*(uv)=a uv`.

The Lefschetz number is therefore

`L(f)=tr H^0(f) + tr H^2(f) - tr H^3(f) - tr H^5(f)`
`= 1 + a - 1 - a = 0`.

Thus every self-map inducing a `pi_1`-automorphism has Lefschetz number zero. This contradicts C4.
