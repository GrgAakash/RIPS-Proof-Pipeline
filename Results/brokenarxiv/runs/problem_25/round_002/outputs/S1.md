1. Assignment Restatement

Prove C1: Under the theorem hypotheses, if a self-map \(f:M\to M\) induces an automorphism \(f_* \in \operatorname{Aut}(\pi_1(M))\), then \(L(f)=0\).

2. Subproof Or Failure

Assume, for contradiction, that \(f_* \in \operatorname{Aut}(\pi_1(M))\) and \(L(f)\neq 0\).

By property \(R_\infty\), every automorphism of \(\pi_1(M)\) has infinitely many twisted conjugacy classes. Since \(f_*\) is an automorphism, the Reidemeister number \(R(f)\), defined as the number of \(f_*\)-twisted conjugacy classes, is therefore infinite.

Because \(M\) is of Jiang-type and \(L(f)\neq 0\), condition (ii) in the definition gives
\[
N(f)=R(f).
\]
Thus \(N(f)\) would be infinite.

But \(M\) is a closed manifold, hence compact and triangulable as a finite polyhedron. A standard Nielsen fixed point theory fact is that for a self-map of a compact polyhedron, the Nielsen number is finite: it counts the essential fixed point classes among finitely many fixed point classes. Therefore \(N(f)<\infty\), contradicting \(N(f)=R(f)=\infty\).

Hence no such \(f\) can have \(L(f)\neq 0\). Therefore every self-map \(f:M\to M\) with \(f_*\in\operatorname{Aut}(\pi_1(M))\) satisfies
\[
L(f)=0.
\]
