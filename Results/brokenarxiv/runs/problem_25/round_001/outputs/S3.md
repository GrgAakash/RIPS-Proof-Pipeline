what must be proved:
If there exists a continuous self-map \(f:M\to M\) such that \(f_*\in \operatorname{Aut}(\pi_1(M))\) and \(L(f)\ne 0\), then the hypotheses that \(M\) is Jiang-type and \(\pi_1(M)\) has property \(R_\infty\) are inconsistent.

declared prerequisite subclaims:
SC1: If \(f:M\to M\) induces an automorphism of \(\pi_1(M)\), then \(R(f)=\infty\).

SC2: For every continuous self-map \(f\) of a closed manifold, \(N(f)<\infty\).

2. Subproof or failure

Assume \(M\) is a closed Jiang-type manifold and \(\pi_1(M)\) has property \(R_\infty\). Suppose there exists a continuous self-map \(f:M\to M\) such that \(f_*\in \operatorname{Aut}(\pi_1(M))\) and \(L(f)\ne 0\).

Since \(f_*\) is an automorphism of \(\pi_1(M)\), prerequisite SC1 gives
\[
R(f)=\infty.
\]

Because \(M\) is Jiang-type and \(L(f)\ne 0\), the defining Jiang-type dichotomy gives
\[
N(f)=R(f).
\]
Therefore
\[
N(f)=\infty.
\]

On the other hand, since \(M\) is closed and \(f\) is a continuous self-map of \(M\), prerequisite SC2 gives
\[
N(f)<\infty.
\]

This contradicts \(N(f)=\infty\). Hence the simultaneous assumptions are inconsistent. This proves SC3, conditional on SC1 and SC2.
