2. Subproof or failure

Assume \(G=\pi_1(M)\) has property \(R_\infty\), and let \(f:M\to M\) be a continuous self-map whose induced homomorphism on fundamental groups is an automorphism
\[
f_*=\phi\in \operatorname{Aut}(G).
\]

By the standard Reidemeister-class interpretation in Nielsen fixed point theory, the Reidemeister number \(R(f)\) is the number of equivalence classes in \(G\) under the \(\phi\)-twisted conjugacy relation
\[
\alpha \sim \sigma \alpha \phi(\sigma)^{-1},
\qquad \alpha,\sigma\in G.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
R(f)=R(\phi),
\]
where \(R(\phi)\) denotes the number of \(\phi\)-twisted conjugacy classes of \(G\).

Since \(G\) has property \(R_\infty\), every automorphism of \(G\) has infinitely many twisted conjugacy classes. Applying this to the automorphism \(\phi=f_*\), we get
\[
R(\phi)=\infty.
\]
Therefore
\[
R(f)=\infty.
\]

This proves SC1.
