assigned subclaim(s): SC4

what must be proved: If `M` is not aspherical, construct a continuous self-map `f:M -> M` such that `f_*` is an automorphism of `pi_1(M)` and `L(f) != 0`.

declared prerequisite subclaims: none

2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED.

The elementary cases can be handled.

If `chi(M) != 0`, take `f = id_M`. Then `f_* = id` on `pi_1(M)`, hence is an automorphism, and
`L(f) = L(id_M) = chi(M) != 0`.

If `pi_1(M)` is trivial, take `f` to be a constant map. The induced homomorphism on the trivial group is the identity automorphism of the trivial group. Since `M` is connected, the constant map induces the identity on `H_0(M;Q)` and the zero map on positive-dimensional rational homology, so `L(f)=1`.

The remaining case is:

`M` closed, non-aspherical, `chi(M)=0`, and `pi_1(M)` nontrivial.

In this case the identity map has Lefschetz number zero, and the constant map has nonzero Lefschetz number but does not induce an automorphism of `pi_1(M)`. The supplied packet contains no obstruction-theoretic, Postnikov, or homological realization statement implying that non-asphericity alone produces a self-map with prescribed automorphic `pi_1` action and nonzero Lefschetz number.
