2. Subproof or failure

Assume \(M\) is a closed Jiang-type manifold and \(\pi_1(M)\) has property \(R_\infty\). We prove that \(M\) is aspherical.

Suppose, for contradiction, that \(M\) is not aspherical. By the declared prerequisite SC4, there exists a continuous map
\[
f:M\to M
\]
such that
\[
f_*:\pi_1(M)\to \pi_1(M)
\]
is an automorphism and
\[
L(f)\ne 0.
\]

But then the declared prerequisite SC3 applies to this same map \(f\). Since \(f_*\in \operatorname{Aut}(\pi_1(M))\) and \(L(f)\ne 0\), SC3 says that the hypotheses of the theorem are inconsistent. Those hypotheses are exactly the assumptions currently in force: \(M\) is closed and Jiang-type, and \(\pi_1(M)\) has property \(R_\infty\). This contradiction shows that the supposition that \(M\) is not aspherical is impossible.

Therefore \(M\) is aspherical.
