1. Target decomposition

target_label: ample_generics_for_two_sorted_ultrametric_dc_fraisse_limit

target_type: topological-dynamical theorem about automorphism group of a Fraisse limit

main_goal: prove that `Aut(U)` has ample generics.

variables_and_parameters:
`U` is the Fraisse limit of finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces with dc-embeddings.  
`n >= 1` is arbitrary.  
`K` denotes the finite two-sorted ultrametric dc-embedding class.  
`K_p^n` denotes finite `K`-structures equipped with `n` partial automorphisms.

conclusion_to_prove: for every integer `n >= 1`, the diagonal conjugation action of `Aut(U)` on `Aut(U)^n` has a comeager orbit.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id: C5

hardest_step_description: proving WAP directly when partial automorphisms move distance-sort elements. The amalgam must identify only the prescribed original `A` data, preserve the order structure on distances, preserve all distances under every partial automorphism, and avoid accidentally forcing incompatible distance-sort identifications.

risk_if_wrong: the proof could silently assume finite totalization or ordinary free amalgamation of point ultrametrics, neither of which handles distance-sort motion. That would leave the main KR hypothesis unproved.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it: define the WAP witness `B` explicitly, track domains/ranges of every partial automorphism on both sorts, and prove a compatibility lemma for the distance-sort amalgam before defining point cross-distances. The proof should verify each partial automorphism relation after amalgamation, not merely state that the construction is “free.”

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check: Do not use ample generics, generic tuples, or the KR conclusion as an input. Only use KR as a standard background criterion after JEP and WAP are proved.

full_theorem_check: The proof must cover every `n >= 1`, not just `n = 1`.

source_check: No web sources, hidden lemmas, previous proofs, or answer keys may be used.

hypothesis_check: The proof must use dc-embeddings, not ordinary isometric embeddings with fixed distance set.

notation_check: Distinguish point-sort maps from distance-sort maps throughout; partial automorphisms may be partially defined on both sorts.

standard_background_check: The only substantial external theorem should be the standard Kechris-Rosendal JEP/WAP criterion for ample generics of Fraisse-limit automorphism groups.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1: Prove the base amalgamation facts for finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces with dc-embeddings, including linear amalgamation of finite distance orders over a common suborder and a cross-distance definition satisfying the ultrametric inequality. Also formalize `K_p^n` objects and morphisms.

S2: Using S1, prove JEP for `K_p^n`: given two finite systems with `n` partial automorphisms, embed them into one finite system where the partial automorphisms remain partial automorphisms and no unintended identifications are introduced.

S3: For an arbitrary object `A` of `K_p^n`, construct a finite extension `B` intended as a WAP witness. The construction must explicitly handle all point-sort and distance-sort elements appearing in domains/ranges of the partial automorphisms and their finite interaction data.

S4: Prove that the `B` from S3 satisfies WAP. Given two extensions of `B`, build a common extension over the original `A`; verify order preservation, distance preservation, ultrametricity, and compatibility of all `n` partial automorphisms, especially on moved distance-sort elements.

S5: State the standard Kechris-Rosendal criterion precisely enough for this setting, verify that S2 and S4 supply its hypotheses for every `n >= 1`, and conclude that `Aut(U)^n` has a comeager conjugacy class for every `n`.
