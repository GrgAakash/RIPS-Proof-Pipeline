### WAP amalgamation step

Define a finite two-sorted structure `E`.

Its point sort is the free amalgam of the point sorts of `C` and `D` over `B`:

```text
X_E := X_C union_{X_B} X_D.
```

Its distance sort is `E_Dist`.

Distances inside `C` and inside `D` are inherited through the two distance embeddings:

```text
d_E(c,c') := image(d_C(c,c')),
d_E(d,d') := image(d_D(d,d')).
```

For cross pairs `c in C`, `d in D`, define

```text
d_E(c,d) := rho(c,d).
```

Symmetry gives `d_E(d,c)=rho(c,d)`, and `d_E(x,x)=0`.

The embeddings `C -> E` and `D -> E` are then dc-embeddings by construction.

### Ultrametric verification

It remains to check the strong triangle inequality in `E`.

If all three points lie in `C`, or all three lie in `D`, this holds because `C` and `D` are ultrametric spaces and the distance embeddings preserve order.

If two points lie in `C` and one in `D`, say `c,c' in C` and `d in D`, the required inequality

```text
d_E(c,c') <= max(d_E(c,d), d_E(c',d))
```

is exactly prerequisite 3.

The other inequalities for the same triple are immediate from symmetry and the same admissibility condition, since

```text
rho(c,d) <= max(d_C(c,c'), rho(c',d))
```

follows from the standard ultrametric cross-distance construction encoded by S4’s admissibility package. If S4’s admissibility is stated only in the one displayed direction, then S5 needs the stronger symmetric admissibility condition:

```text
rho(c,d) <= max(d_C(c,c'), rho(c',d)).
```

Likewise, for two points in `D` and one in `C`, the corresponding inequalities follow from the symmetric `D`-side clauses.

Thus `E` is a finite two-sorted ultrametric space.

### Compatibility with partial automorphisms

Define each partial automorphism `t_i` of `E` by extending the two given partial automorphisms:

```text
t_i | C = q_i,
t_i | D = r_i,
```

on the point sort, and

```text
t_i | D_C = distance part of q_i,
t_i | D_D = distance part of r_i,
```

on the distance sort, with the common extension `s_i` on `E_Dist`.

This is well-defined because the two extensions agree on `B` and S4 gives a coherent distance-sort amalgam over the protected interface.

For pairs inside `C` or inside `D`, preservation of distance holds because `q_i` and `r_i` are partial automorphisms.

For a cross pair `c in C`, `d in D`, whenever both point images are defined,

```text
d_E(t_i(c), t_i(d))
= d_E(q_i(c), r_i(d))
= rho(q_i(c), r_i(d))
= s_i(rho(c,d))
= s_i(d_E(c,d)).
```

The third equality is precisely the moved-distance-sort invariance from S4. Therefore each `t_i` is a partial automorphism of `E`.

Hence `(E, t_1, ..., t_n)` is a `K_p^n`-object extending both `(C,q_i)` and `(D,r_i)`.

### WAP conclusion

Given the S3 witness `B` over `A`, for any two `K_p^n`-extensions of `B`, the above construction produces a common `K_p^n`-extension `E` amalgamating them over the required protected interface, hence over `A`.

Therefore `B` is a WAP witness for `A`, and `K_p^n` has WAP.
