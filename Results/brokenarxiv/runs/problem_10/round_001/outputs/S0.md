ample-generics theorem for an automorphism group of a Fraisse limit

main_goal:  
Prove that the automorphism group of the Fraisse limit `U` of finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces with dc-embeddings has ample generics.

variables_and_parameters:  
`U`: Fraisse limit of the stated class.  
`G = Aut(U)`.  
`n >= 1`: arbitrary integer.

For every `n >= 1`, the diagonal conjugation action of `G` on `G^n` has a comeager orbit.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:  
S3

hardest_step_description:  
The hardest part is proving the finite extension property for finitely many partial automorphisms in this two-sorted ultrametric setting. The proof must extend both the point sort and the ordered distance sort, and must preserve the exact dc-embedding relation between distances and point distances.

risk_if_wrong:  
If partial automorphisms cannot be totalized finitely, the planned proof of WAP collapses. A mistaken construction may also fail because new distance values are not linearly orderable in a way compatible with all automorphism orbits.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:  
The final proof should give an explicit orbit-completion construction: freely add formal images under the partial maps, close under the finite generated action, define distances between new orbit points by invariant ultrametric amalgamation rules, and add only finitely many distance values. It must separately verify symmetry, separation, ultrametric inequality, preservation of `0`, linear ordering of the distance sort, and invariance under the extended automorphisms.

Keep point-sort maps and distance-sort maps distinct. A partial automorphism in the two-sorted structure acts on both sorts and must preserve the distance relation.

standard_background_check:  
The only substantial named standard theorem should be the Kechris-Rosendal ample-generics criterion for Fraisse limits. Any class-specific amalgamation, EPPA-like, or totalization statement must be proved, not cited as known for this class.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:  
Prove strong amalgamation for finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces with dc-embeddings. Include ordered-distance-set amalgamation and verification that the cross-distance formula gives a finite two-sorted ultrametric amalgam.

S2:  
For fixed `n`, define `K_p^n` precisely and prove JEP using S1. Make sure embeddings preserve partial automorphism graphs on both sorts.

S3:  
Prove the finite extension property: every finite object of `K_p^n` embeds into one where the `n` partial automorphisms become total automorphisms. The construction must be finite and must preserve the two-sorted ultrametric structure.

S4:  
Prove that objects of `K_p^n` with total automorphisms are weak amalgamation bases. Given two extensions, build an equivariant amalgam over the base and verify compatibility with all partial automorphisms.

S5:  
Assemble the proof: derive WAP from S3 and S4, combine with JEP from S2, invoke the Kechris-Rosendal criterion, and conclude that `Aut(U)^n` has a comeager conjugacy class for every `n >= 1`.
