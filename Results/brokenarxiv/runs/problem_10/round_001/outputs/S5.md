We prove WAP for \(K_p^n\), conditional on S3 and S4.

Take any object \(A\in K_p^n\). By S3, there is an embedding
\[
e:A\to B
\]
in \(K_p^n\) such that all \(n\) partial automorphisms on \(B\) are total automorphisms of \(B\). By S4, this \(B\) is a weak amalgamation base. Therefore, for any two embeddings
\[
f:B\to C,\qquad g:B\to D
\]
in \(K_p^n\), there exist an object \(E\in K_p^n\) and embeddings
\[
r:C\to E,\qquad s:D\to E
\]
such that the two copies of the original \(A\) agree in \(E\):
\[
r\circ f\circ e = s\circ g\circ e.
\]
This is exactly the weak amalgamation property for \(A\). Since \(A\) was arbitrary, \(K_p^n\) has WAP.

Hence, for this fixed \(n\), \(\operatorname{Aut}(U)^n\) has a comeager conjugacy class. Since \(n\ge 1\) was arbitrary, \(\operatorname{Aut}(U)\) has ample generics.
