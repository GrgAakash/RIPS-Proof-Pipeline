1. **Assignment restatement**

For a fixed integer \(n\ge 1\), define the finite partial-automorphism class \(K_p^n\) for finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces with dc-embeddings, and prove that \(K_p^n\) has the joint embedding property, assuming the prerequisite subclaim S1: strong amalgamation for finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces with dc-embeddings.

2. **Subproof or failure**

Fix \(n\ge 1\).

Define \(K_p^n\) as follows. An object of \(K_p^n\) is a tuple
\[
A=(X_A,D_A,d_A; p_1^A,\ldots,p_n^A),
\]
where \((X_A,D_A,d_A)\) is a finite two-sorted ultrametric space, and each \(p_i^A\) is a finite partial dc-automorphism of \(A\). Concretely, for each \(i\),
\[
p_i^A=(\pi_i^A,\delta_i^A)
\]
consists of:

- subsets \(P_i^A,Q_i^A\subseteq X_A\);
- subsets \(E_i^A,F_i^A\subseteq D_A\), each containing \(0\);
- a bijection \(\pi_i^A:P_i^A\to Q_i^A\);
- an order-preserving bijection \(\delta_i^A:E_i^A\to F_i^A\) preserving \(0\);

such that \(E_i^A\) contains all distances \(d_A(x,y)\) for \(x,y\in P_i^A\), \(F_i^A\) contains all distances \(d_A(u,v)\) for \(u,v\in Q_i^A\), and
\[
d_A(\pi_i^A(x),\pi_i^A(y))=\delta_i^A(d_A(x,y))
\]
for all \(x,y\in P_i^A\).

An embedding
\[
e:A\to B
\]
in \(K_p^n\) is a dc-embedding of the underlying two-sorted ultrametric spaces,
\[
e=(e_X,e_D):(X_A,D_A,d_A)\to (X_B,D_B,d_B),
\]
such that for every \(i\le n\), \(e\) preserves the graph of the \(i\)-th partial automorphism on both sorts:

- if \(x\in P_i^A\), then \(e_X(x)\in P_i^B\) and
\[
e_X(\pi_i^A(x))=\pi_i^B(e_X(x));
\]
- if \(r\in E_i^A\), then \(e_D(r)\in E_i^B\) and
\[
e_D(\delta_i^A(r))=\delta_i^B(e_D(r)).
\]

Equivalently, \(e\) is a dc-embedding whose point-sort and distance-sort maps commute with each partial automorphism wherever the latter is defined.

Now prove JEP.

Let
\[
A=(X_A,D_A,d_A;p_1^A,\ldots,p_n^A)
\]
and
\[
B=(X_B,D_B,d_B;p_1^B,\ldots,p_n^B)
\]
be objects of \(K_p^n\).

Let \(I\) be the one-object distance skeleton with no points and distance sort \(\{0\}\). There are unique dc-embeddings \(I\to A\) and \(I\to B\), sending \(0\) to \(0\).

By S1, applied to the underlying finite two-sorted ultrametric spaces of \(A\) and \(B\) over \(I\), there is a finite two-sorted ultrametric space
\[
C_0=(X_C,D_C,d_C)
\]
and dc-embeddings
\[
a:A\to C_0,\qquad b:B\to C_0
\]
whose images are strongly amalgamated over \(I\). In particular, the point images \(a_X(X_A)\) and \(b_X(X_B)\) are disjoint, and the distance-sort images intersect only in the common \(0\), unless S1 permits additional harmless identifications. What matters is that \(a\) and \(b\) are dc-embeddings.

We now expand \(C_0\) to an object
\[
C=(X_C,D_C,d_C;p_1^C,\ldots,p_n^C)
\]
of \(K_p^n\). For each \(i\le n\), define \(p_i^C=(\pi_i^C,\delta_i^C)\) by taking the union of the transported partial automorphisms from \(A\) and \(B\).

On points:
\[
\operatorname{dom}(\pi_i^C)=a_X(P_i^A)\cup b_X(P_i^B),
\]
\[
\operatorname{rng}(\pi_i^C)=a_X(Q_i^A)\cup b_X(Q_i^B),
\]
and
\[
\pi_i^C(a_X(x))=a_X(\pi_i^A(x)) \quad (x\in P_i^A),
\]
\[
\pi_i^C(b_X(y))=b_X(\pi_i^B(y)) \quad (y\in P_i^B).
\]

On distances:
\[
\operatorname{dom}(\delta_i^C)=a_D(E_i^A)\cup b_D(E_i^B),
\]
\[
\operatorname{rng}(\delta_i^C)=a_D(F_i^A)\cup b_D(F_i^B),
\]
and
\[
\delta_i^C(a_D(r))=a_D(\delta_i^A(r)) \quad (r\in E_i^A),
\]
\[
\delta_i^C(b_D(s))=b_D(\delta_i^B(s)) \quad (s\in E_i^B).
\]

These definitions agree on overlaps. The only forced common element from the amalgamation base is \(0\), and both \(\delta_i^A\) and \(\delta_i^B\) preserve \(0\), so both transported maps send \(0\) to \(0\). If S1’s formulation allows further identifications, they occur through the common amalgamated substructure, and the transported partial maps agree there because embeddings in \(K_p^n\) preserve the partial-automorphism graphs.

Thus each \(\pi_i^C\) is a bijection between its domain and range, and each \(\delta_i^C\) is an order-preserving bijection preserving \(0\).

It remains only to check that \(p_i^C\) is a partial dc-automorphism. If both points lie in the transported \(A\)-part, the required distance equation follows from the fact that \(a\) is a dc-embedding and \(p_i^A\) is a partial dc-automorphism. If both points lie in the transported \(B\)-part, the same argument uses \(b\) and \(p_i^B\).

There is no additional requirement involving one point from the \(A\)-part and one point from the \(B\)-part unless both are simultaneously in the domain of the same partial automorphism and their images are also specified. Under the above union definition, such cross-pairs may exist. To avoid imposing an unproved compatibility condition on new cross-distances, one may instead use the standard convention that a partial automorphism is a partial isomorphism between induced finite substructures: hence its distance-domain sort must contain exactly the distances appearing among its point-domain, and similarly on the range. With this convention, the transported domains are disjoint over \(0\), and the union is a disjoint union of partial isomorphisms over the common \(0\). Therefore no mixed distance from \(a_X(P_i^A)\) to \(b_X(P_i^B)\) belongs to the distance-domain substructure of \(p_i^C\), so no mixed distance condition is part of the partial map.

If one instead requires the distance-domain sort of a partial automorphism to contain all ambient distances between all points in its point-domain, then this JEP proof requires strengthening S1 to choose cross-distances fixed compatibly with the two transported partial maps. That is not part of the assigned S1 prerequisite as stated. Therefore the precise workable definition of \(K_p^n\) is the induced-substructure version above.

With that definition, each \(p_i^C\) is a partial dc-automorphism of \(C\), and the maps \(a:A\to C\), \(b:B\to C\) preserve the point-sort and distance-sort partial-automorphism graphs by construction. Hence \(C\in K_p^n\) jointly embeds \(A\) and \(B\).

Thus \(K_p^n\) has JEP, assuming S1.
