2. Subproof or Failure

Let \(G\) be an \(n\)-vertex graph with
\[
e(G)=\binom n2-\lfloor n^{2-\epsilon}\rfloor,
\]
and let \(H=\overline G\). Then
\[
e(H)=m=\lfloor n^{2-\epsilon}\rfloor.
\]

Choose a threshold \(T=T(n)\) as follows:
\[
T=n^{1-\epsilon/2}\quad\text{if }0<\epsilon<2,
\]
and
\[
T=n^{1/2}\quad\text{if }\epsilon\ge 2.
\]
For \(0<\epsilon<2\), we have \(T=o(n)\), and
\[
\frac{e(H)}{T}\le \frac{n^{2-\epsilon}}{n^{1-\epsilon/2}}
=n^{1-\epsilon/2}=o(n).
\]
For \(\epsilon\ge 2\), \(T=n^{1/2}=o(n)\). Also \(e(H)=\lfloor n^{2-\epsilon}\rfloor\le 1\) for all large \(n\), and therefore
\[
\frac{e(H)}{T}\le n^{-1/2}=o(n).
\]
In both cases, \(T=o(n)\) and \(e(H)/T=o(n)\). Since \(T=o(n)\), for sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
2T<n-3.
\]

Define
\[
U=\{v\in V(G): d_H(v)\le T\}.
\]
Every vertex in \(V\setminus U\) has \(d_H(v)>T\). Hence, by the handshaking identity in \(H\),
\[
T|V\setminus U|
< \sum_{v\in V\setminus U} d_H(v)
\le \sum_{v\in V(H)} d_H(v)
=2e(H)=2m.
\]
Thus
\[
|V\setminus U|<\frac{2m}{T},
\]
and in particular
\[
|V\setminus U|\le \frac{2m}{T}=o(n)
\]
up to the harmless strict/non-strict inequality convention. Therefore \(|U|=n-o(n)\).

Now assume, toward a contradiction, that \(G[U]\) contains two adjacent edges \(ab,bc\) of the same color. Since \(ab,bc\in E(G[U])\), the vertices \(a,b,c\) all lie in \(U\). By the prerequisite S1, for every
\[
x\in V(G)\setminus\{a,b,c\},
\]
at least one of \(ax,cx\) is missing from \(G\), equivalently at least one of \(ax,cx\) is an edge of \(H\).

Restricting to \(x\in U\setminus\{a,b,c\}\), this gives at least one \(H\)-edge from the pair \(\{a,c\}\) to each such \(x\). Therefore the number of \(H\)-edges incident to \(a\) or \(c\) and going to \(U\setminus\{a,b,c\}\) is at least
\[
|U|-3.
\]
On the other hand, since \(a,c\in U\),
\[
d_H(a)\le T,\qquad d_H(c)\le T.
\]
Thus the total number of \(H\)-edges incident to \(a\) or \(c\) is at most
\[
d_H(a)+d_H(c)\le 2T.
\]
Consequently
\[
|U|-3\le 2T.
\]
But \(|U|=n-o(n)\), while \(2T=o(n)\). Hence for sufficiently large \(n\),
\[
|U|-3>2T,
\]
contradiction. Equivalently, using \(|V\setminus U|=o(n)\) and \(2T<n-3\) eventually, we have
\[
|U|-3=n-|V\setminus U|-3>2T
\]
for all sufficiently large \(n\).

Therefore, for sufficiently large \(n\), no two adjacent edges of \(G[U]\) have the same color.
