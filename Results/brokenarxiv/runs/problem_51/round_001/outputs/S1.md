1. Assignment Restatement

S-ID: S1

assigned subclaim(s): SC1 and SC2

what must be proved:
If an edge-coloring of \(G\) makes every copy of \(P_4\) rainbow, then:

SC1. If adjacent edges \(ab,bc\in E(G)\) have the same color, then for every \(x\notin\{a,b,c\}\), at least one of \(ax,cx\) is not in \(E(G)\).

SC2. If disjoint edges \(ab,cd\in E(G)\) have the same color, then at least one of \(ac,ad,bc,bd\) is not in \(E(G)\).

declared prerequisite subclaims: none

2. Subproof or Failure

Let \(G\) be an \(n\)-vertex graph with an edge-coloring such that every copy of \(P_4\) in \(G\) is rainbow.

Proof of SC1. Suppose \(ab,bc\in E(G)\) are adjacent same-colored edges. Let \(x\notin\{a,b,c\}\). Assume for contradiction that both \(ax\in E(G)\) and \(cx\in E(G)\).

The vertices \(x,a,b,c\) are distinct, and the edges
\[
xa,\quad ab,\quad bc
\]
are all present in \(G\). Therefore the ordered vertex sequence
\[
x-a-b-c
\]
forms a copy of \(P_4\) in \(G\). This copy contains the two edges \(ab\) and \(bc\), which have the same color. Hence this copy of \(P_4\) is not rainbow, contradicting the assumed coloring property. Therefore at least one of \(ax,cx\) is missing from \(G\). This proves SC1.

Proof of SC2. Suppose \(ab,cd\in E(G)\) are disjoint same-colored edges, so \(a,b,c,d\) are distinct. Assume for contradiction that all four cross-edges
\[
ac,\ ad,\ bc,\ bd
\]
belong to \(E(G)\).

Since \(bc\in E(G)\), the ordered vertex sequence
\[
a-b-c-d
\]
has distinct vertices and all three required edges
\[
ab,\quad bc,\quad cd
\]
present in \(G\). Thus it forms a copy of \(P_4\) in \(G\). This copy contains both \(ab\) and \(cd\), which have the same color. Hence this copy of \(P_4\) is not rainbow, contradicting the assumed coloring property. Therefore at least one of \(ac,ad,bc,bd\) is missing from \(G\). This proves SC2.
