2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED.

The missing obstacle is the central uniformity statement itself. From the allowed packet we may use that \(G\) has some non-elementary acylindrical action on a hyperbolic space and that property (T) forbids infinite virtually cyclic quotients, but these facts do not by themselves give a uniform word-length bound, independent of \(S\), for finding two independent loxodromics in a fixed action.

A plausible contradiction strategy would be: assume no such \(N\) exists, choose generating sets \(S_i\) such that \(B_{S_i}(i)\) contains no independent loxodromic pair, and then extract a limiting action or quotient that is elementary or virtually cyclic, contradicting property (T). However, this requires a nontrivial compactness/limit theorem connecting “large \(S_i\)-balls contain no independent loxodromics in the fixed action” to an infinite virtually cyclic quotient or another property (T)-forbidden degeneration. No such theorem is supplied, and it is not a genuinely elementary standard fact.

Thus SC5 cannot be proved from the permitted inputs without an additional uniform-dynamical lemma.
