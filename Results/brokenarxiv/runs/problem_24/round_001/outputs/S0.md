1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Kazhdan acylindrically hyperbolic groups have uniform exponential growth

target_type:
existence of a uniform positive lower bound on exponential growth rates over finite generating sets

main_goal:
Show that for every finite generating set \(S\) of \(G\), the exponential growth rate \(\omega(G,S)\) is bounded below by a constant \(>1\) independent of \(S\).

variables_and_parameters:
\(G\): an acylindrically hyperbolic group with Kazhdan’s property (T).  
\(S\): an arbitrary finite generating set of \(G\).  
\(\omega(G,S)\): exponential growth rate with respect to \(S\).

conclusion_to_prove:
\[
\omega(G):=\inf_S \omega(G,S)>1,
\]
where the infimum is over finite generating sets \(S\).

source_status:
standard background fact / must be proved inside current proof as needed

A property (T) group has finite abelianization and no infinite virtually cyclic quotient. In particular, a sequence of actions or generating sets cannot collapse \(G\) onto an infinite cyclic or dihedral limiting action.

intended_role_in_proof:
Used to ensure the acylindrical hyperbolic dynamics remain genuinely non-elementary uniformly over generating sets.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC5

hardest_step_description:
The proof must establish a uniform bound, independent of the generating set \(S\), for finding enough non-elementary hyperbolic dynamics inside \(B_S(N)\).

risk_if_wrong:
Without uniformity in \(S\), the argument proves only exponential growth for each generating set, not uniform exponential growth.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The final proof should isolate a precise uniform-dynamical lemma: property (T) plus acylindrical hyperbolicity forces uniformly short non-elementary behavior for every generating set. This lemma must be stated and proved carefully, with explicit explanation of where property (T) rules out limiting elementary actions or virtually cyclic quotients.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not assume uniform exponential growth in any form while proving the uniform short free-semigroup lemma.

full_theorem_check:
The argument must prove the infimum over all finite generating sets is \(>1\), not merely that one generating set has exponential growth.

source_check:
No external source, paper-specific theorem, or hidden lemma is available. Any nontrivial uniform acylindrical-action statement must be proved inside the current proof unless declared genuinely standard.

hypothesis_check:
Property (T) is essential for uniformity; acylindrical hyperbolicity alone supplies non-elementary dynamics but may not by itself provide the required uniform bound.

notation_check:
Define \(\omega(G,S)\), \(B_S(N)\), loxodromic elements, independent loxodromics, and free semigroup before use.

standard_background_check:
Safe standard facts include property (T) implies finite generation, property (T) forbids infinite cyclic quotients, ping-pong for independent loxodromics, and the growth lower bound from a free semigroup in a bounded word ball.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Prove the elementary growth reductions: property (T) implies finite generation, and if two elements in \(B_S(N)\) generate a free semigroup then \(\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/N}\).

S2:
Set up the acylindrically hyperbolic dynamics. Explain that \(G\) admits a non-elementary acylindrical action on a hyperbolic space and has independent loxodromic elements. Also prove property (T) rules out virtually cyclic or infinite cyclic quotient degeneration.

S3:
Prove the central uniformity lemma: there exists \(N=N(G)\) such that for every finite generating set \(S\), \(B_S(N)\) contains two elements acting non-elementarily/independently in the chosen hyperbolic action.

S4:
Use ping-pong for the two uniformly short independent loxodromic elements to show that suitable elements in a uniformly bounded \(S\)-ball generate a free semigroup.

S5:
Assemble the proof: fix arbitrary finite \(S\), apply the uniformity and ping-pong lemmas, obtain \(\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/N}\), then take the infimum over all finite generating sets.
