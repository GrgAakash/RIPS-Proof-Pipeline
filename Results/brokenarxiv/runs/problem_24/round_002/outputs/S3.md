## 1 Assignment restatement
Given a finite generating set `S` and elements `a,b ∈ B_S(L)` generating a free semigroup, prove that for each `n`, the `2^n` positive words of length `n` in the alphabet `{a,b}` are distinct elements of `G` and all lie in `B_S(nL)`.

## 2 Subproof or failure
Let `n ≥ 1`. There are exactly `2^n` formal positive words of length `n` in the two-letter alphabet `{a,b}`.

Since `a` and `b` generate a free semigroup, the evaluation map from the free semigroup on two generators to `G`, sending the two formal generators to `a` and `b`, is injective. Therefore distinct formal positive words of length `n` evaluate to distinct elements of `G`.

Now take any positive word
```text
w = x_1 x_2 ... x_n,   with each x_i ∈ {a,b}.
```
Because `a,b ∈ B_S(L)`, we have `|a|_S ≤ L` and `|b|_S ≤ L`. Hence each `|x_i|_S ≤ L`. By subadditivity of word length,
```text
|w|_S ≤ |x_1|_S + ... + |x_n|_S ≤ nL.
```
Thus `w ∈ B_S(nL)`.

Therefore the `2^n` positive words of length `n` in `a,b` are distinct and all lie in `B_S(nL)`.
