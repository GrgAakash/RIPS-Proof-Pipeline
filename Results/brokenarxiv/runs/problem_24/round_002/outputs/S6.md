## 2 Final Proof

<!-- BEGIN_FINAL_PROOF -->
Let `G` be an acylindrically hyperbolic group with Kazhdan’s property (T).

We use the standard fact that a discrete group with Kazhdan’s property (T) is finitely generated. Hence `G` has finite generating sets, and its uniform exponential growth rate is defined by
\[
\omega(G)=\inf_S \omega(G,S),
\]
where `S` ranges over finite generating sets and
\[
\omega(G,S)=\lim_{m\to\infty} |B_S(m)|^{1/m}
\]
equivalently may be read with `limsup`; the lower bound below applies in either convention.

Since `G` is acylindrically hyperbolic, there exists a Gromov-hyperbolic space `X` and a non-elementary acylindrical action
\[
G\curvearrowright X .
\]
Fix this action once and for all.

By the supplied standard uniform free-semigroup theorem for non-elementary acylindrical actions, there is a constant
\[
L=L(G\curvearrowright X)\in \mathbb N
\]
depending only on this fixed action such that, for every finite generating set `S` of `G`, the ball
\[
B_S(L)=\{g\in G: |g|_S\le L\}
\]
contains two elements `a_S,b_S` which generate a free semigroup. The important point is that `L` is fixed independently of `S`; only the pair `a_S,b_S` may vary with `S`.

Now fix an arbitrary finite generating set `S`. Choose `a,b\in B_S(L)` generating a free semigroup. For each `n\ge 1`, the `2^n` positive words of length `n` in the alphabet `{a,b}` evaluate to distinct elements of `G`, because `a,b` generate a free semigroup. Also, since `|a|_S,|b|_S\le L`, every such word has `S`-length at most `nL`. Therefore
\[
|B_S(nL)|\ge 2^n
\]
for every `n\ge 1`.

Let \(\beta_S(m)=|B_S(m)|\). Then
\[
\beta_S(nL)^{1/(nL)}\ge (2^n)^{1/(nL)}=2^{1/L}.
\]
Taking `n\to\infty` along the subsequence `m=nL` gives
\[
\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/L}.
\]
Since `S` was arbitrary and `L` is independent of `S`,
\[
\omega(G)=\inf_S \omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/L}>1.
\]
Thus `G` has uniform exponential growth.
