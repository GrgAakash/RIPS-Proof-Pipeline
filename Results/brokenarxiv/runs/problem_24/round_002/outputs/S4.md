1 Assignment Restatement

Show that the lower bound
\[
|B_S(nL)| \ge 2^n
\]
implies
\[
\omega(G,S) \ge 2^{1/L},
\]
using the standard exponential growth rate definition.

2 Subproof Or Failure

Let
\[
\beta_S(m)=|B_S(m)|
\]
be the growth function of \(G\) with respect to the finite generating set \(S\). The standard exponential growth rate is
\[
\omega(G,S)=\lim_{m\to\infty}\beta_S(m)^{1/m},
\]
equivalently the corresponding limsup if one uses that convention.

Assume that for every \(n\ge 1\),
\[
\beta_S(nL)=|B_S(nL)|\ge 2^n.
\]
Then
\[
\beta_S(nL)^{1/(nL)}\ge (2^n)^{1/(nL)}=2^{1/L}.
\]
Taking \(n\to\infty\) along the subsequence of radii \(m=nL\), and using the definition of \(\omega(G,S)\), gives
\[
\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/L}.
\]

If \(L\) is not an integer, replace \(L\) by \(\lceil L\rceil\); since \(B_S(L)\subseteq B_S(\lceil L\rceil)\), the same argument gives the slightly weaker but sufficient bound
\[
\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/\lceil L\rceil}>1.
\]
