1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Uniform exponential growth for property (T) acylindrically hyperbolic groups

target_type:
Implication theorem about group growth

main_goal:
Show that an acylindrically hyperbolic group \(G\) with Kazhdan's property (T) has a uniform lower bound \(>1\) on exponential growth rates over all finite generating sets.

variables_and_parameters:
\(G\): an acylindrically hyperbolic group.  
Property assumption: \(G\) has Kazhdan's property (T).  
Fixed action: a non-elementary acylindrical action of \(G\) on a Gromov-hyperbolic space.  
\(S\): an arbitrary finite generating set of \(G\).  
\(L\): the constant supplied by the uniform free-semigroup theorem for the fixed action.

conclusion_to_prove:
There exists \(\lambda>1\), independent of the finite generating set \(S\), such that the exponential growth rate of \(G\) with respect to \(S\) is at least \(\lambda\). Equivalently, \(G\) has uniform exponential growth.

source_status:

exact_statement_or_fact:
Every countable discrete group with Kazhdan's property (T) is finitely generated. In the present setting, this ensures \(G\) is finitely generated.

intended_role_in_proof:
Allows use of finite generating sets and of the stated uniform free-semigroup theorem, whose hypothesis assumes the acting group is finitely generated.

tool:
Uniform free-semigroup theorem for non-elementary acylindrical actions

source_status:
additional guidance item

exact_statement_or_fact:
If a finitely generated group acts non-elementarily and acylindrically on a Gromov-hyperbolic space, then there exists a constant \(L\), depending only on the action, such that for every finite generating set \(S\) of the group, the ball \(B_S(L)\) contains two elements that generate a free semigroup.

intended_role_in_proof:
Produces, uniformly over all finite generating sets \(S\), two short elements \(a,b\in B_S(L)\) generating a free semigroup.

tool:
Free semigroup gives exponential ball growth

source_status:
standard background fact / proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
If \(a,b\in B_S(L)\) generate a free semigroup, then the \(2^n\) positive words of length \(n\) in \(a,b\) are distinct and all lie in \(B_S(nL)\). Hence \(|B_S(nL)|\ge 2^n\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Converts uniformly short free semigroup generators into a uniform lower bound on growth.

tool:
Definition of uniform exponential growth

source_status:
provided definition / notation / assumption

exact_statement_or_fact:
A finitely generated group \(G\) has uniform exponential growth if
\[
\inf_S \omega(G,S)>1,
\]
where \(S\) ranges over finite generating sets and \(\omega(G,S)\) is the exponential growth rate with respect to \(S\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Identifies the exact conclusion once the lower bound \(\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/L}\) is proved for every \(S\).

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC3

hardest_step_description:
Applying the uniform free-semigroup theorem correctly: the constant \(L\) must depend only on the fixed acylindrical action, not on the generating set \(S\).

risk_if_wrong:
If \(L\) depended on \(S\), the argument would only prove exponential growth for each generating set, not uniform exponential growth.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
Fix one non-elementary acylindrical action from acylindrical hyperbolicity, then apply the guidance theorem once to that action. Emphasize that the resulting \(L\) is fixed before \(S\) is chosen and works for every finite generating set.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
The proof must not assume uniform exponential growth or any equivalent result. The only growth input should be the free-semigroup-to-growth counting argument.

full_theorem_check:
The final proof must cover all finite generating sets \(S\), not merely one chosen generating set.

source_check:
The only nontrivial external theorem used should be the supplied guidance theorem. Property (T) implies finite generation may be cited as standard background.

hypothesis_check:
Property (T) is used only to guarantee finite generation. Acylindrical hyperbolicity is used to obtain the fixed non-elementary acylindrical action.

notation_check:
Define \(B_S(r)\) as the ball of radius \(r\) in the word metric for \(S\). Clarify that \(a,b\in B_S(L)\) means each has \(S\)-word length at most \(L\).

standard_background_check:
The final proof may use standard facts about word metrics, exponential growth rates, and free semigroups, but should explicitly prove the short counting estimate \(|B_S(nL)|\ge 2^n\).

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Verify the setup facts: property (T) implies \(G\) is finitely generated, and acylindrical hyperbolicity gives a non-elementary acylindrical action of \(G\) on a Gromov-hyperbolic space.

S2:
Apply the supplied uniform free-semigroup theorem to the fixed action and state precisely the resulting constant \(L\), emphasizing that \(L\) is independent of the finite generating set \(S\).

S3:
Given a finite generating set \(S\) and elements \(a,b\in B_S(L)\) generating a free semigroup, prove that the \(2^n\) positive words of length \(n\) in \(a,b\) are distinct and lie in \(B_S(nL)\).

S4:
Translate \(|B_S(nL)|\ge 2^n\) into \(\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/L}\), using the standard definition of exponential growth rate.

S5:
Take the infimum over all finite generating sets \(S\) and conclude \(\inf_S\omega(G,S)\ge 2^{1/L}>1\), hence \(G\) has uniform exponential growth.
