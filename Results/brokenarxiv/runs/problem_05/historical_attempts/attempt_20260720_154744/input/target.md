Problem 05:

Let $N \ge 3$ be an integer. For any $N$-component hyperbolic link $L \subset \mathbb{S}^3$ with exterior $X_L = \mathbb{S}^3 \setminus \text{int}(N(L))$ (where $N(L)$ is a regular neighborhood of $L$), if $P \subset X_L$ is an incompressible spanning planar surface (i.e., a planar surface with exactly one boundary component on each boundary torus of $X_L$), then at least one boundary component of $P$ must have a slope $a/p$ (in standard meridian-longitude coordinates) that is either meridional or integral (i.e., $p \in \{0, 1\}$).
