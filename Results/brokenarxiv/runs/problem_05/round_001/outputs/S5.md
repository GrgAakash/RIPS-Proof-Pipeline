2. Subproof or failure

The assigned assembly is conditional on the prerequisite obstruction subclaims listed above.

Let the boundary components of `P` lie on the `N` boundary tori of `X_L`, one on each torus. For each boundary component, write its slope in standard meridian-longitude coordinates as a primitive fraction `a_i/p_i`, with the usual convention that the meridional slope has denominator `p_i = 0`.

Assume, for contradiction, that no boundary component of `P` has slope that is meridional or integral. Then no slope has `p_i = 0`, and no slope is integral. In primitive meridian-longitude coordinates, a nonmeridional slope `a_i/p_i` is integral exactly when `|p_i| = 1`; after choosing the conventional positive denominator normalization, this is exactly `p_i = 1`. Therefore every boundary slope has normalized denominator `p_i notin {0,1}`.

By the declared prerequisite SC2-SC4, this all-strictly-nonmeridional-nonintegral case produces the forbidden capped sphere/core configuration and hence a contradiction to the hypotheses that `L` is hyperbolic and `P` is incompressible planar spanning.

Thus the assumption that all boundary slopes have `p_i notin {0,1}` is impossible. Consequently at least one boundary component of `P` has normalized denominator `p_i in {0,1}`. Such a slope is meridional when `p_i = 0` and integral when `p_i = 1`. This proves the desired conclusion, conditional on the prerequisite obstruction subclaims.
