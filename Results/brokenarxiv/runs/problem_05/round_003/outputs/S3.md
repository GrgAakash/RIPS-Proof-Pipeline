**1. Claim Status**

`SETUP FAILURE` for proving essentiality from the supplied hypotheses alone.

The capped surface `\widehat P` need not be essential in `M(alpha)` merely because `P` is incompressible in `X_L`, nor merely because each filling core meets `\widehat P` exactly once.

**2. Analysis**

After `alpha`-filling, the planar surface `P` caps off to a sphere `\widehat P`. Since each component of `\partial P` is capped by a meridian disk of the corresponding filling solid torus, each `alpha`-core intersects `\widehat P` exactly once.

But incompressibility of `P` in the unfilled exterior only rules out compressing disks for `P` lying in `X_L`. It does not rule out the possibility that the capped sphere `\widehat P` bounds a 3-ball in the filled manifold `M(alpha)`.

If `\widehat P` bounds a ball `B`, the filling cores may pass through `B` as properly embedded arcs meeting `\widehat P` once. Removing neighborhoods of those cores from `B` gives a ball with drilled arcs. The remaining punctured sphere boundary can be incompressible in that drilled complement in many standard configurations. Thus incompressibility of `P` is compatible with `\widehat P` being inessential after filling.

The one-intersection condition also does not help: a sphere bounding a ball can meet a closed curve exactly once if the curve is not entirely contained in the ball after filling decomposition is taken into account. In the present Dehn filling picture, each core pierces the capped disk once, so this is exactly the warned configuration.

**3. Failed Proof Point**

The tempting argument would be:

1. Suppose `\widehat P` bounds a ball in `M(alpha)`.
2. Try to isotope that ball away from the filling solid tori.
3. Deduce a compression of `P` in `X_L`.

Step 2 fails. The filling cores meet `\widehat P` once each, so the ball cannot simply be pushed off the attached solid tori. The ball may contain essential-looking drilled arcs after removing the cores, and this does not automatically produce a compression disk for `P`.

**4. Extra Hypothesis Needed**

C5 needs, explicitly, one of the following as an input hypothesis/theorem:

`Essential capped sphere hypothesis:`  
In the `alpha`-filled manifold `M(alpha)`, the capped sphere `\widehat P` is essential; equivalently, it does not bound a 3-ball.

Or a stronger external theorem implying it, for example a theorem saying that under the given link-exterior hypotheses, an incompressible spanning planar surface with one boundary component on each cusp caps off after boundary-slope filling to an essential sphere.

Without such an additional result, C5 cannot assume the existence of an essential sphere in `M(alpha)`.
