2. Final proof

<!-- BEGIN_FINAL_PROOF -->
FINAL PROOF NOT COMPLETED.

1. Let \(N_F(G)\) denote the number of copies of \(F\) in \(G\).

2. The upper bound is immediate. Graphs counted in the definition of \(c(n,F)\) are a subclass of all \(n\)-vertex graphs with \(\operatorname{ex}(n,F)+1\) edges. Hence
\[
h_F(n,1)\le c(n,F).
\]

3. A minimizer for \(h_F(n,1)\) exists because, for fixed \(n\), there are only finitely many \(n\)-vertex graphs.

4. Suppose \(G\) is an \(n\)-vertex graph with \(\operatorname{ex}(n,F)+1\) edges and some edge \(e\in E(G)\) has \(G-e\) \(F\)-free. Then \(G-e\) has \(\operatorname{ex}(n,F)\) edges, so \(G-e\) is an extremal \(F\)-free graph. Therefore \(G\) is obtained by adding one edge to an extremal \(F\)-free graph, and consequently
\[
N_F(G)\ge c(n,F).
\]

5. [KEY STEP] The missing necessary step is the following exact local statement:
for every fixed graph \(F\) containing a cycle, there should exist \(n_0(F)\) such that whenever \(n\ge n_0(F)\), \(e(G)=\operatorname{ex}(n,F)+1\), and \(G-e\) contains a copy of \(F\) for every edge \(e\in E(G)\), then
\[
N_F(G)>c(n,F).
\]
This statement is not among the allowed supporting statements, was not proved by S1-S5, and is not a generic standard-background fact. It is precisely the missing lower-bound ingredient identified in the packet.

6. If the [KEY STEP] were proved, the lower bound would follow: take a minimizer \(G\). By Step 2, \(N_F(G)\le c(n,F)\). The [KEY STEP] would force the existence of an edge \(e\) such that \(G-e\) is \(F\)-free, and then Step 4 would give \(N_F(G)\ge c(n,F)\). Thus equality would follow.

7. Because the [KEY STEP] remains unproved from the supplied materials, the exact target theorem is not proved.
