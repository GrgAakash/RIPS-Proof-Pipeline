Problem 22:
Let $p > 0$ be a prime. Let $f: R \to S$ be a homomorphism of commutative rings of characteristic $p$. We say $f$ is b-nil formally unramified if for every ring $A$ of characteristic $p$ and ideal $I \subset A$ such that the ideal generated by $p$-th powers of elements of $I$ is zero, any $R$-algebra homomorphism $S \to A/I$ has at most one lift to an $R$-algebra homomorphism $S \to A$. The relative Frobenius homomorphism of $f$ is the map $F_{S/R}: F_*R \\otimes\_R S \\to F\_*S$ given by $r \otimes s \mapsto rs^p$, where $F_*R$ denotes $R$ viewed as an $R$-algebra via the absolute Frobenius $x \mapsto x^p$. Then $f$ is formally unramified if and only if $F_{S/R}$ is an epimorphism in the category of rings.
