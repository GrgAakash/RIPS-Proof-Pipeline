1. Target decomposition

target_label: b_nil_unramified_relative_Frobenius_epimorphism

target_type: equivalence theorem

main_goal: Show that the uniqueness of lifts across ideals with zero p-th-power ideal is equivalent to categorical epimorphy of the relative Frobenius map.

variables_and_parameters: p prime, p > 0; f: R -> S a homomorphism of commutative rings of characteristic p; F_{S/R}: F_*R tensor_R S -> F_*S, r tensor s |-> r s^p.

conclusion_to_prove: f is b-nil formally unramified if and only if F_{S/R} is an epimorphism in the category of rings.

2. Available tools

tool: Definition of b-nil formally unramified  
source_status: provided definition / notation / assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: For every characteristic p ring A and ideal I subset A with the ideal generated by p-th powers of elements of I equal to zero, every R-algebra map S -> A/I has at most one R-algebra lift S -> A.  
intended_role_in_proof: Used directly in the reverse implication after constructing a suitable ideal I from two maps out of F_*S.

tool: Definition of relative Frobenius  
source_status: provided definition / notation / assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: F_{S/R}: F_*R tensor_R S -> F_*S sends r tensor s to r s^p.  
intended_role_in_proof: Allows equality after precomposition with F_{S/R} to be translated into equality on f(R) and on p-th powers from S.

tool: Definition of categorical epimorphism  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: A ring map g: B -> C is an epimorphism iff for every ring T and every pair of ring maps u,v: C -> T, u g = v g implies u = v.  
intended_role_in_proof: This is the exact form needed for both implications.

tool: Underlying-ring interpretation of F_*S  
source_status: provided definition / notation / assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: F_*S has the same underlying ring as S, with altered source algebra structure; hence ring maps F_*S -> T may be treated as ordinary ring maps S -> T.  
intended_role_in_proof: Permits comparing maps S -> A or S -> T as maps out of F_*S.

tool: Freshman’s dream in characteristic p  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: In a commutative ring of characteristic p, (x+y)^p = x^p + y^p and (x-y)^p = x^p - y^p.  
intended_role_in_proof: Shows that congruent lifts modulo I have equal p-th powers, and that an ideal generated by elements with zero p-th powers also has zero p-th-power ideal.

tool: Targets of maps from characteristic p rings  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: If C has p = 0 and there is a unital ring map C -> T, then p = 0 in T; if the zero ring convention matters, the zero target is harmless because all maps to it are equal.  
intended_role_in_proof: Ensures that the target T in the epimorphism test is eligible for the b-nil lifting condition.

tool: Difference-generated ideal  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact: Given ring maps u,v: S -> T, the ideal I generated by all u(s)-v(s) makes the reductions of u and v to T/I equal. If additionally every u(s)-v(s) has p-th power zero in characteristic p, then I has zero p-th-power ideal.  
intended_role_in_proof: Supplies the universal b-nil thickening needed to prove epimorphy from formal unramifiedness.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id: C4

hardest_step_description: Showing that the ideal I generated by all differences u(s)-v(s) has zero p-th-power ideal, not merely that each generator has p-th power zero.

risk_if_wrong: The reverse implication would only show equality modulo an ideal whose generators are p-nilpotent, which is weaker than the required hypothesis that every element of I has p-th power zero.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it: Write an arbitrary element x of I as a finite sum x = sum a_i(u(s_i)-v(s_i)). In characteristic p, x^p = sum a_i^p(u(s_i)-v(s_i))^p. Since u and v agree after precomposition with F_{S/R}, they agree on s_i^p, so each difference has p-th power zero.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1: Prove C1. Start with alpha(s)-beta(s) in I, use the hypothesis on I and characteristic p to conclude alpha(s)^p=beta(s)^p.

S2: Prove C2. Given two R-algebra lifts alpha,beta: S -> A of the same map to A/I, use S1 to show alpha and beta agree after precomposition with F_{S/R}; then apply epimorphy.

S3: Prove C3. Given u,v: F_*S -> T with u F_{S/R}=v F_{S/R}, evaluate on r tensor 1 to show agreement on f(R), then define the common R-algebra structure.

S4: Prove C4. Evaluate agreement on 1 tensor s to get u(s^p)=v(s^p). Define I from all differences u(s)-v(s), prove reductions agree, and prove I has zero p-th-power ideal by expanding finite sums in characteristic p.

S5: Prove C5 and assemble the equivalence. For arbitrary u,v agreeing after F_{S/R}, use S3 and S4 to apply b-nil formal unramifiedness with A=T and the constructed ideal I, then conclude u=v.
