F_{S/R}:F_*R\otimes_R S\to F_*S,\qquad r\otimes s\mapsto r s^p

declared prerequisite subclaims: C1, used in the form: if \(A\) has characteristic \(p\), \(I\subset A\) has zero \(p\)-th-power ideal, and two \(R\)-algebra maps \(\alpha,\beta:S\to A\) have the same reduction modulo \(I\), then \(\alpha(s)^p=\beta(s)^p\) for all \(s\in S\).

2. Subproof or failure

Assume \(F_{S/R}\) is an epimorphism in the category of rings.

Let \(A\) be a ring of characteristic \(p\), and let \(I\subset A\) be an ideal such that the ideal generated by the \(p\)-th powers of elements of \(I\) is zero. Let
\[
\bar\varphi:S\to A/I
\]
be an \(R\)-algebra homomorphism, and suppose \(\alpha,\beta:S\to A\) are two \(R\)-algebra homomorphism lifts of \(\bar\varphi\). Thus the reductions of \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) modulo \(I\) are equal.

We prove \(\alpha=\beta\). By C1, for every \(s\in S\),
\[
\alpha(s)^p=\beta(s)^p.
\]

Regard \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) as ring homomorphisms
\[
F_*S\to A
\]
using the same underlying ring as \(S\). We claim that they agree after precomposition with \(F_{S/R}\). Indeed, for a pure tensor \(r\otimes s\in F_*R\otimes_R S\),
\[
(\alpha\circ F_{S/R})(r\otimes s)
=\alpha(r s^p)
=\alpha(f(r))\,\alpha(s)^p,
\]
while
\[
(\beta\circ F_{S/R})(r\otimes s)
=\beta(r s^p)
=\beta(f(r))\,\beta(s)^p.
\]
Since \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are \(R\)-algebra maps, \(\alpha(f(r))=\beta(f(r))\). Since \(\alpha(s)^p=\beta(s)^p\) by C1, the two displayed expressions are equal. Therefore
\[
\alpha\circ F_{S/R}=\beta\circ F_{S/R}.
\]

Because \(F_{S/R}\) is an epimorphism in the category of rings, equality after precomposition with \(F_{S/R}\) implies
\[
\alpha=\beta
\]
as ring homomorphisms \(F_*S\to A\), hence as \(R\)-algebra homomorphisms \(S\to A\).

Thus every \(R\)-algebra homomorphism \(S\to A/I\) has at most one \(R\)-algebra lift \(S\to A\). Therefore \(f\) is \(b\)-nil formally unramified.
