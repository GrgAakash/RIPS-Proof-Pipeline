Cleaned skeleton packet for this standalone problem:
Let p > 0 be a prime. Let f: R -> S be a homomorphism of commutative rings of characteristic p. We say f is b-nil formally unramified if for every ring A of characteristic p and ideal I subset A such that the ideal generated by p-th powers of elements of I is zero, any R-algebra homomorphism S -> A/I has at most one lift to an R-algebra homomorphism S -> A. The relative Frobenius homomorphism of f is the map F_{S/R}: F_*R \otimes_R S -> F_*S given by r \otimes s |-> r s^p, where F_*R denotes R viewed as an R-algebra via the absolute Frobenius x |-> x^p. Prove: f is b-nil formally unramified if and only if F_{S/R} is an epimorphism in the category of rings.
