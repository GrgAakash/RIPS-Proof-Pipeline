2. Subproof or failure

Let
\[
g(z)=(1+z)^2=z^2+2z+1.
\]
This is a real polynomial. Its only zero is \(z=-1\), with multiplicity \(2\). Hence all zeros of \(g\) are real.

By the standard polynomial characterization of the classical Laguerre--Polya class, a real polynomial belongs to \(L\text{-}P\) if and only if all of its zeros are real. Therefore
\[
g(z)=(1+z)^2 \in L\text{-}P.
\]
