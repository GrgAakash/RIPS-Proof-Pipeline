2. Subproof or failure

Fix `0<q<1`. By prerequisite SC1,  
`g(z)=(1+z)^2` belongs to `L-P`.

Assume, for contradiction, that the target theorem is true as written. Then `B_q` maps `L-P` surjectively onto itself. Since `g in L-P`, there must exist some `h in L-P` such that `B_q(h)=g`.

By the assigned prerequisite, the unique formal/polynomial preimage of `g` under `B_q` is

`f(z)=1+2z+((1+q)/(2q))z^2`.

Therefore `h=f`.

By prerequisite SC4, this quadratic has negative discriminant for `0<q<1`. By the standard quadratic discriminant criterion, `f` has no real zeros. By the standard polynomial characterization of the classical Laguerre--Polya class, a real polynomial belongs to `L-P` iff all of its zeros are real. Hence `f notin L-P`.

This contradicts `h in L-P` and `h=f`. Therefore `g` has no preimage in `L-P` under `B_q`, so `B_q|_{L-P}` is not surjective. Consequently the claimed bijection theorem is false as written and cannot be proved without changing the statement.
