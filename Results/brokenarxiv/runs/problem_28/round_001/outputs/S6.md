\[
\det(tI-A)=t^2-3t+1.
\]
Thus \(A\) has two distinct positive eigenvalues
\[
\mu_+=\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2}>1,\qquad
\mu_-=\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\in(0,1).
\]
Since the eigenvalues are distinct, \(A\) is diagonalizable over \(\mathbb C\). Therefore every solution of the recurrence has the form
\[
F_n=c_+\mu_+^n v_+ + c_-\mu_-^n v_-
\]
for eigenvectors \(v_+,v_-\) and constants \(c_+,c_-\in\mathbb C\).

3. [KEY STEP] If \(F=(F_n)_{n\in\mathbb Z}\in \ell^2(\mathbb Z;\mathbb C^2)\), then necessarily \(c_+=0\), because \(|\mu_+|>1\) makes the \(v_+\)-component grow exponentially as \(n\to+\infty\). \[
