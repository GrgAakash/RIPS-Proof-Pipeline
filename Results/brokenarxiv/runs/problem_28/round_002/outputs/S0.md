1. Target decomposition

target_label: existence_of_amenable_URN_with_noncompact_eigenfunctions

target_type: construction / existence theorem

main_goal: Construct a unimodular random rooted network `N` and a finite-range operator `D` with `lambda = 0`.

variables_and_parameters: Choose an integer `d >= 4` and Bernoulli bond percolation parameter `p < 1`, close enough to `1`, on the `d`-regular tree `T_d`. Let `C` be the root cluster conditioned to be infinite. Form the barycentric subdivision `B(C)` with old vertex-nodes and edge-nodes, and orient each original edge by an independent fair mark.

conclusion_to_prove: `B(C)` is amenable and unimodular with finite expected degree; `D` has finite range; `dim ker(D) > 0`; and almost surely every nonzero `0`-eigenfunction of `D` has infinite support.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id: C5

hardest_step_description: Proving the positive von Neumann dimension of the `l2` divergence-free flow space and correctly transferring that dimension to the barycentric network.

risk_if_wrong: If the dimension formula or root-change scaling is mishandled, the construction may only produce nonzero flows without positive root trace.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it: Prove the dimension identity directly: edge-space dimension is `E deg(o)/2`; the gradient image has dimension `1` because constants are not in `l2` on an infinite component; therefore `dim ker div = E deg(o)/2 - 1 > 0`. Then explicitly note that barycentric root scaling multiplies this by a positive finite factor.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check: Positive dimension is not inferred from the target theorem; it is computed from the divergence operator on the constructed tree.

full_theorem_check: The proof must verify all hypotheses: amenable, unimodular, finite expected degree, finite-range `D`, positive von Neumann dimension, and no finite-support nonzero eigenfunctions.

source_check: No supporting theorem from the skeleton is cited. All construction-specific claims are assigned as subclaims.

hypothesis_check: Choose `p < 1` for pendant Følner paths, but close enough to `1` so that conditional expected degree is greater than `2`.

notation_check: Interpret “eigenfunction” as nonzero eigenfunction; the zero function has empty support.

standard_background_check: Only basic mass transport, finite-dimensional Hilbert-space rank-nullity, and elementary facts about trees should be treated as standard. The percolation, amenability, and dimension computations must be written out.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1: Prove C1. Construct the infinite conditioned percolation cluster on `T_d`, prove unimodularity under conditioning, bounded degree, and choose `p < 1` close enough to `1` so `E deg(o) > 2`.

S2: Prove C2. Show that for `p < 1`, the infinite cluster almost surely contains arbitrarily long pendant paths and hence is amenable; then show barycentric subdivision preserves amenability and finite expected degree.

S3: Prove C3. Give the exact root law on `B(C)` as the old-vertex/edge-vertex Palm mixture and verify the mass transport principle.

S4: Prove C4 and C5. Define `D`, prove it has finite range, identify its `l2` kernel with divergence-free `l2` edge flows, and compute positive von Neumann dimension.

S5: Prove C6. For an arbitrary nonzero formal `0`-eigenfunction, show the old-vertex component either vanishes or has infinite support; if it vanishes, the edge component is a divergence-free flow, and a nonzero such flow on a tree cannot have finite support.
