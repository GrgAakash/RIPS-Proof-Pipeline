Problem 01:

Consider Bernoulli bond percolation with fixed retention parameter $p \in (0,1]$ on the random recursive tree, coupled through the natural growth process (where at each step $n \ge 1$, a new vertex $n$ attaches to a uniformly chosen existing vertex in $\{0, \dots, n-1\}$, and the connecting edge is retained with probability $p$). Let $P(p)$ be the probability that the root cluster remains a largest cluster at every time step $n$. There exists a critical probability $p_c \in (0, 1)$ such that $P(p) = 0$ for $p < p_c$ and $P(p) > 0$ for $p > p_c$.
