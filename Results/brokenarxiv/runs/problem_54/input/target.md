Problem 54:
Consider the critical Hénon equation $-\Delta u = |x|^\alpha u^{\frac{N+2+2\alpha}{N-2}}$ in $\mathbb{R}^N$, where $\alpha > 0$ and $N \ge 3$. The equation admits non-radial positive classical solutions satisfying the Newtonian-type decay condition $u(x) = O(|x|^{2-N})$ as $|x| \to \infty$ if and only if $\alpha = 2(k-1)$ for some integer $k \ge 1$.
