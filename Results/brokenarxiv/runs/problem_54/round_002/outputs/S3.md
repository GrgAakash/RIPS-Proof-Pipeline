From the allowed material I can derive only the cylinder formulation and the elementary spectral reduction for linearized or projected quantities. I cannot prove the requested global rigidity bridge.

The key obstruction is this: a positive homoclinic solution of the nonlinear cylinder equation may be expanded in spherical harmonics,
\[
v(t,\theta)=\sum_{\ell,m} a_{\ell m}(t)Y_{\ell m}(\theta),
\]
but the nonlinear term \(v^p\) couples all angular modes. Projecting gives
\[
-a_{\ell m}''+\left(\lambda_\ell+\frac{(N-2)^2}{4}\right)a_{\ell m}
=
\int_{\mathbb S^{N-1}} v^pY_{\ell m}\,d\theta,
\]
which does not imply any algebraic relation between \(\lambda_\ell\) and \(\kappa\). The desired resonance condition would require a strong classification, hidden symmetry, conformal pullback, moving-plane rigidity, or a nonlinear spectral identity showing that every nonzero angular mode must lie exactly at \(\lambda_\ell=\kappa(\kappa+N-2)\). None of those is included in the allowed support.

There is also a conceptual issue in the S0 blueprint as stated. The cylinder equation is autonomous and depends on \(\kappa\) only through the exponent \(p\), while the claimed resonance
\[
\lambda_\ell=\kappa(\kappa+N-2)
\]
is an exact algebraic spectral quantization. Such a conclusion is much stronger than what follows from decay, positivity, and standard spherical harmonic decomposition alone.

What can be proved after assuming the missing bridge is straightforward: since the spherical spectrum is
\[
\lambda_\ell=\ell(\ell+N-2),\qquad \ell=0,1,2,\dots,
\]
the relation
\[
\ell(\ell+N-2)=\kappa(\kappa+N-2)
\]
implies
\[
(\ell-\kappa)(\ell+\kappa+N-2)=0.
\]
Because \(\ell\ge0\), \(\kappa=(\alpha+2)/2>1\), and \(N\ge3\), the second factor is positive, so \(\kappa=\ell\in\mathbb N\). Therefore
\[
\alpha=2\kappa-2=2(\ell-1).
\]
For a genuinely nonradial solution, \(\ell\ge1\), and since \(\alpha>0\), actually \(\kappa>1\), so \(\ell\ge2\).
