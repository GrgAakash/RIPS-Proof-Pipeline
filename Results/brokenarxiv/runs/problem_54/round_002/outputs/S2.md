2. Subproof or Failure

Let
\[
\gamma=\frac{N-2}{2},\qquad p=1+\frac{2\kappa}{\gamma}.
\]
A positive `t`-only cylinder homoclinic solves
\[
-q''+\gamma^2q=q^p,\qquad q>0,\qquad q(t)\to0\quad(t\to\pm\infty).
\]

Multiplying by \(q'\) gives the conserved energy
\[
-\frac12(q')^2+\frac{\gamma^2}{2}q^2-\frac1{p+1}q^{p+1}=0.
\]
Hence
\[
(q')^2=\gamma^2q^2-\frac{2}{p+1}q^{p+1}.
\]
At the unique maximum \(t=\tau\),
\[
q(\tau)^{p-1}=\frac{p+1}{2}\gamma^2=\gamma(\gamma+\kappa).
\]
Solving by separation yields
\[
q(t)=Q_\kappa(t-\tau),
\]
where
\[
Q_\kappa(t)=\left(\gamma(\gamma+\kappa)\right)^{\gamma/(2\kappa)}
\sech^{\gamma/\kappa}(\kappa t).
\]
Thus all positive `t`-only homoclinics are translates of \(Q_\kappa\); in original \(x\)-variables these translations are exactly radial dilations/scalings.

Now linearize the cylinder equation
\[
-v_{tt}-\Delta_{\mathbb S^{N-1}}v+\gamma^2v=v^p
\]
about \(Q_\kappa(t-\tau)\). For a spherical harmonic \(Y_\ell\) with
\[
-\Delta_{\mathbb S^{N-1}}Y_\ell=\lambda_\ell Y_\ell,\qquad
\lambda_\ell=\ell(\ell+N-2),
\]
the degree-\(\ell\) linearized operator is
\[
L_{\ell,\tau}\phi
=
-\phi''+\bigl(\gamma^2+\lambda_\ell\bigr)\phi
-pQ_\kappa(t-\tau)^{p-1}\phi.
\]
Since
\[
Q_\kappa^{p-1}
=
\gamma(\gamma+\kappa)\sech^2(\kappa(t-\tau)),
\]
and \(p\gamma(\gamma+\kappa)=(\gamma+2\kappa)(\gamma+\kappa)\),
\[
L_{\ell,\tau}
=
-\frac{d^2}{dt^2}
+\gamma^2+\lambda_\ell
-(\gamma+\kappa)(\gamma+2\kappa)\sech^2(\kappa(t-\tau)).
\]

Translation in \(t\) is irrelevant for bounded kernel, so set \(\tau=0\). Put \(s=\kappa t\) and \(\beta=\gamma/\kappa\). The kernel equation becomes
\[
-\psi''+\left(\beta^2+\frac{\lambda_\ell}{\kappa^2}\right)\psi
-(\beta+1)(\beta+2)\sech^2s\,\psi=0.
\]
This is the Pöschl-Teller equation with parameter \(\nu=\beta+1\). Its bounded solutions occur exactly when
\[
\beta^2+\frac{\lambda_\ell}{\kappa^2}=(\nu-n)^2,
\qquad n=0,1,\dots,\ n<\nu.
\]
For \(n=1\), this gives \(\lambda_\ell=0\), the radial translation mode \(Q_\kappa'\). For \(n\ge2\), the right side is \(<\beta^2\), forcing \(\lambda_\ell<0\), impossible. For the only nonzero angular possibility \(n=0\),
\[
\beta^2+\frac{\lambda_\ell}{\kappa^2}=(\beta+1)^2,
\]
so
\[
\lambda_\ell=\kappa^2(2\beta+1)
=\kappa^2+2\gamma\kappa
=\kappa(\kappa+N-2).
\]
Therefore bounded kernel in a nonzero angular degree occurs exactly when
\[
\ell(\ell+N-2)=\kappa(\kappa+N-2).
\]
