## 1 Assignment restatement

For every integer `k = gamma >= 2`, construct a positive nonradial solution of

\[
-v_{tt}-\Delta_{\mathbb S^{N-1}}v+\beta^2v=v^p,\qquad
\beta=\frac{N-2}{2},\quad p=1+\frac{2k}{\beta},
\]

on \(\mathbb R\times \mathbb S^{N-1}\), with the two endpoint asymptotics corresponding to a positive classical original-variable solution with Newtonian decay. The construction must be nonlinear and pointwise verified, not merely a linear resonance.

## 2 Subproof or failure

I cannot complete the requested construction from the allowed material.

The natural explicit candidate is

\[
v(t,\theta)=A\bigl(\cosh(kt)+a q(\theta)\bigr)^{-\beta/k},
\qquad |a|<1,
\]

with \(q\) nonconstant on \(\mathbb S^{N-1}\). A direct pointwise substitution shows this solves the cylinder equation only if \(q\) satisfies both

\[
-\Delta_{\mathbb S^{N-1}}q=k(k+N-2)q
\]

and the nonlinear eikonal identity

\[
|\nabla_{\mathbb S^{N-1}}q|^2=k^2(1-q^2).
\]

Then one may choose

\[
A=\bigl[\beta(\beta+k)(1-a^2)\bigr]^{\beta/(2k)}.
\]

The radial case \(a=0\) works. The nonradial case requires a spherical harmonic of degree \(k\) satisfying the eikonal identity. That is a strong global isoparametric condition, not a consequence of linear resonance. It is not supplied by the allowed material, and it is not available for arbitrary \(N\ge 3\) and every integer \(k\ge2\) from standard background alone.

Using a degree-\(k\) spherical harmonic only verifies the linearized resonance. It does not verify the nonlinear PDE, because the leftover term involving \(|\nabla q|^2\) is angularly dependent unless the eikonal identity holds.

Therefore the requested “for every \(k\ge2\)” positive nonradial construction is not justified under the stated rules.
