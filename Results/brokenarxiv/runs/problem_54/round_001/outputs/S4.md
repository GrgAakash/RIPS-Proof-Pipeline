1. Assignment restatement

For \(\alpha=2(k-1)\), \(k\in\mathbb N\), construct positive classical solutions of

\[
-\Delta u=|x|^{2k-2}u^{(N+4k-2)/(N-2)}
\]

from the standard critical bubble or a degree-\(k\) mechanism, and verify the equation directly. The intended use is the existence/nonradial side only, not the full iff theorem.

2. Subproof or failure

I can verify the radial degree-\(k\) bubble, but I cannot complete the required nonradial construction from the allowed inputs.

Let

\[
\gamma=\frac{N-2}{2k},\qquad p=\frac{N+4k-2}{N-2},
\]

so that \(\gamma p=\gamma+2\). For

\[
u_0(x)=A(1+|x|^{2k})^{-\gamma},
\]

a direct radial Laplacian computation gives

\[
-\Delta u_0
=
A(N-2)(N+2k-2)|x|^{2k-2}(1+|x|^{2k})^{-\gamma-2}.
\]

Thus \(u_0\) solves the equation if

\[
A^{p-1}=(N-2)(N+2k-2).
\]

This gives only radial solutions.

A natural nonradial trial is

\[
u(x)=A\bigl(1+|x|^{2k}-2|x|^kY(x/|x|)\bigr)^{-\gamma},
\]

where \(Y\) is a degree-\(k\) spherical harmonic. Direct verification on the cylinder shows this works only if, in addition,

\[
-\Delta_{S^{N-1}}Y=k(k+N-2)Y,
\qquad
|\nabla_{S^{N-1}}Y|^2+k^2Y^2=\text{constant}.
\]

The first condition is standard for degree-\(k\) harmonics, but the second is an extra eikonal/isoparametric condition not supplied by the packet and not a consequence of ordinary spherical harmonic theory. For example, the standard candidate \(P_k=\operatorname{Re}(x_1+ix_2)^k\) has

\[
|\nabla P_k|^2=k^2(x_1^2+x_2^2)^{k-1},
\]

which is not proportional to \(|x|^{2k-2}\) in \(N\ge3\). So the naive degree-\(k\) bubble-pullback leaves an uncancelled angular residual.

Therefore this subproblem is not solved under the stated restrictions.
