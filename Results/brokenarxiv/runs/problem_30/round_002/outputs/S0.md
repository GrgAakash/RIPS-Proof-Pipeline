1. Target decomposition

target_label: Problem 30

target_type: if-and-only-if admissibility theorem for a generalized Bayes predictive density under KL loss

main_goal: Prove that the Jeffreys-prior Bayesian predictive density for predicting independent \(y\sim \mathrm{Ga}(T\alpha,\beta)\) from \(x\sim \mathrm{Ga}(\alpha,\beta)\), with \(\beta\) unknown, is admissible exactly when \(\alpha>1\).

variables_and_parameters:
\(\alpha>0\), \(T>0\) known; \(\beta>0\) unknown; \(x>0\), \(y>0\); set \(k=(T+1)\alpha\).

conclusion_to_prove:
Let \(q_J(y\mid x)\) be the Bayes predictive density under \(\pi(\beta)\propto 1/\beta\). Under KL loss,
\[
L(\beta,q)=\int p(y;\,T\alpha,\beta)\log\frac{p(y;\,T\alpha,\beta)}{q(y\mid x)}\,dy
\]
averaged over \(x\sim\mathrm{Ga}(\alpha,\beta)\), \(q_J\) is admissible iff \(\alpha>1\).

2. Available tools

tool: Gamma integral and beta-gamma algebra  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact:
\[
\int_0^\infty t^{a-1}e^{-ct}\,dt=\Gamma(a)c^{-a},
\]
and if \(X\sim\mathrm{Ga}(a,\beta)\), \(Y\sim\mathrm{Ga}(b,\beta)\) independent, then \(X+Y\sim\mathrm{Ga}(a+b,\beta)\) and \(X/(X+Y)\sim\mathrm{Beta}(a,b)\), independent of \(X+Y\).  
intended_role_in_proof: Compute \(q_J\), simplify risks, and factor joint gamma variables.

tool: Jeffreys predictive formula  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact:
\[
q_J(y\mid x)=
\frac{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(T\alpha)}
\frac{x^\alpha y^{T\alpha-1}}{(x+y)^{(T+1)\alpha}},
\qquad y>0.
\]
intended_role_in_proof: Identify the decision rule whose admissibility is being studied.

tool: KL Bayes rule identity  
source_status: standard background fact, with model-specific verification inside proof  
exact_statement_or_fact: For any proper prior \(\Pi\), the posterior predictive density minimizes integrated KL risk:
\[
q_\Pi(y\mid x)=
\frac{\int p(x;\alpha,\beta)p(y;T\alpha,\beta)\,d\Pi(\beta)}
{\int p(x;\alpha,\beta)\,d\Pi(\beta)}.
\]
intended_role_in_proof: Used for Blyth admissibility and for constructing candidate dominating predictive densities.

tool: Risk-difference identity  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact:
For any predictive density \(q\),
\[
R(\beta,q_J)-R(\beta,q)
=
E_\beta\!\left[\log\frac{q(Y\mid X)}{q_J(Y\mid X)}\right].
\]
intended_role_in_proof: Converts admissibility/dominance into a pointwise expected log-ratio inequality.

tool: Predictive perturbation family  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact:
Use \(\lambda=1/\beta\). For bounded \(h:(0,\infty)\to[-1,1]\) and \(0<\varepsilon<1\), define
\[
A_r^h(z)=
\frac{z^r}{\Gamma(r)}
\int_0^\infty \lambda^{r-1}e^{-z\lambda}h(\lambda)\,d\lambda.
\]
Then
\[
q_{\varepsilon,h}(y\mid x)
=
q_J(y\mid x)
\frac{1+\varepsilon A_k^h(x+y)}
{1+\varepsilon A_\alpha^h(x)}
\]
is a valid predictive density, namely the Bayes predictive density under
\[
\pi_{\varepsilon,h}(\lambda)\propto \lambda^{-1}(1+\varepsilon h(\lambda)).
\]
intended_role_in_proof: Exact normalized perturbation framework for the inadmissibility half.

tool: Inadmissibility perturbation lemma for \(0<\alpha\le1\)  
source_status: proved inside the current proof; load-bearing lemma required by guidance  
exact_statement_or_fact:
For \(0<\alpha\le1\), \(k=(T+1)\alpha\), there exist bounded measurable \(h\not\equiv\mathrm{constant}\) and \(0<\varepsilon<1\) such that, for every \(\beta>0\),
\[
E_\beta\!\left[
\log\frac{1+\varepsilon A_k^h(X+Y)}
{1+\varepsilon A_\alpha^h(X)}
\right]\ge0,
\]
with strict inequality for at least one \(\beta\).  
intended_role_in_proof: Gives an explicit normalized perturbation \(q_{\varepsilon,h}\) dominating \(q_J\), proving inadmissibility for \(0<\alpha\le1\).

tool: Blyth-type admissibility criterion for predictive KL loss  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact:
If finite proper priors \(\Pi_n\) approximate \(\pi_J(\beta)d\beta=d\beta/\beta\) on compact subsets, their Bayes predictive densities \(q_n\) satisfy vanishing integrated regret against \(q_J\), and risk differences are continuous in \(\beta\), then no predictive density can dominate \(q_J\).  
intended_role_in_proof: Decision-theoretic engine for the \(\alpha>1\) admissibility half.

tool: Permissibility/Blyth verification for \(\alpha>1\)  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact:
For \(\alpha>1\), construct smooth compactly supported truncations of \(d\beta/\beta\), e.g. in \(\mu=\log\beta\), and prove the Bayes regret
\[
\int \{R(\beta,q_J)-R(\beta,q_n)\}\,d\Pi_n(\beta)
\to0.
\]
intended_role_in_proof: Supplies the analytic condition needed by the Blyth criterion.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id: SC4

hardest_step_description: The difficult part is the \(0<\alpha\le1\) inadmissibility half. The proof must not merely cite a risk-improvement inequality. It must either prove the stated perturbation lemma from the gamma model or explicitly introduce it as the standalone load-bearing lemma to be proved.

risk_if_wrong: If SC4 is asserted without proving existence of \(h,\varepsilon\), the inadmissibility half is circular and the “only if” direction is unsupported.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it: The final proof should derive the perturbation density, prove normalization, compute the exact risk difference, and then prove the nonnegative risk-improvement inequality uniformly in \(\beta\), with strict improvement somewhere.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check: Do not use admissibility or inadmissibility of \(q_J\) as an input. Blyth and perturbation criteria must be proved or stated as internal subclaims, not assumed.

full_theorem_check: Both directions are required: admissible for \(\alpha>1\), inadmissible for \(0<\alpha\le1\). The boundary \(\alpha=1\) belongs to inadmissibility.

source_check: No outside paper, displayed inequality, or hidden theorem is available. Any decision-theoretic criterion used must be proved in the final proof or isolated as an internal lemma.

hypothesis_check: \(\alpha>0\), \(T>0\), \(\beta>0\); \(T\) affects \(k=(T+1)\alpha\) but the admissibility threshold is only in \(\alpha\).

notation_check: Distinguish scale \(\beta\) from rate \(\lambda=1/\beta\). The Jeffreys prior is \(d\beta/\beta=d\lambda/\lambda\).

standard_background_check: Gamma integrals, beta-gamma factorization, Jensen/Gibbs inequality, Fubini/Tonelli under verified integrability, and basic Bayes-risk optimality are acceptable standard tools.
