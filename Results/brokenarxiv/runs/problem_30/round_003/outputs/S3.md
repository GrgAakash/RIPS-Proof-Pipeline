Assume the rate convention
\[
p_b(t\mid \lambda)=\frac{\lambda^b}{\Gamma(b)}t^{b-1}e^{-\lambda t},\qquad t>0,
\]
with observed shape \(\alpha\), future shape \(k-\alpha\), so \(k\) is the joint shape for \(X+Y\). Define
\[
A_b^h(t)=
\frac{\int_0^\infty h(\lambda)\lambda^{b-1}e^{-t\lambda}\,d\lambda}
{\int_0^\infty \lambda^{b-1}e^{-t\lambda}\,d\lambda}.
\]
Thus \(A_b^h(t)=E[h(\Lambda)]\) for \(\Lambda\sim \mathrm{Gamma}(b,\text{rate }t)\).

For the Jeffreys prior \(\pi_J(\lambda)\propto \lambda^{-1}\) and perturbation
\[
\pi_{\epsilon,h}(\lambda)=(1+\epsilon h(\lambda))\pi_J(\lambda),
\]
the marginal factors satisfy
\[
m_\alpha^{\epsilon,h}(x)=m_\alpha^J(x)\{1+\epsilon A_\alpha^h(x)\},
\]
and
\[
m_k^{\epsilon,h}(x+y)=m_k^J(x+y)\{1+\epsilon A_k^h(x+y)\}.
\]
Therefore the predictive density is
\[
q_{\epsilon,h}(y\mid x)
=q_J(y\mid x)
\frac{1+\epsilon A_k^h(x+y)}
{1+\epsilon A_\alpha^h(x)}.
\]

With KL predictive risk
\[
R(\lambda,q)=E_\lambda\log\frac{p(Y\mid\lambda)}{q(Y\mid X)},
\]
we get
\[
R(\lambda,q_J)-R(\lambda,q_{\epsilon,h})
=
E_\lambda\log\frac{q_{\epsilon,h}(Y\mid X)}{q_J(Y\mid X)}
\]
and hence
\[
R(\lambda,q_J)-R(\lambda,q_{\epsilon,h})
=
E_\lambda\log
\frac{1+\epsilon A_k^h(X+Y)}
{1+\epsilon A_\alpha^h(X)}
=:\Delta(\lambda).
\]

For validity, it is enough that \(h\) is bounded measurable and \(|\epsilon|\|h\|_\infty<1\), which makes both factors strictly positive and keeps the logarithm finite. More generally, one needs \(q_{\epsilon,h}\ge 0\), normalized, and the displayed expectation well-defined.

Dominance conditions:

\[
q_{\epsilon,h}\text{ dominates }q_J
\iff
\Delta(\lambda)\ge 0\quad\text{for every }\lambda>0.
\]

Strict dominance means additionally
\[
\Delta(\lambda)>0
\]
for at least one \(\lambda>0\).

Scale/rate check: the displayed \(A_b^h(t)\) and the arguments \(X\), \(X+Y\) are correct only when \(\lambda\) is the gamma **rate**. If \(\lambda\) were a scale parameter, the conjugate kernel and \(A_b^h\) would not be \(\lambda^{b-1}e^{-t\lambda}\); the formula would need reparameterization.
