## 1 Assignment restatement

Derive the Jeffreys-prior Bayesian predictive density \(q_J(y\mid x)\) for

\[
x\sim \mathrm{Ga}(\alpha,\beta),\qquad y\sim \mathrm{Ga}(T\alpha,\beta),
\]

with known \(\alpha>0,T>0\), unknown \(\beta>0\), and prior \(\pi(\beta)\propto 1/\beta\). Verify that the resulting \(q_J(\cdot\mid x)\) integrates to one.

## 2 Subproof or failure

The sampling density of \(x\) is

\[
p(x\mid \beta)=\frac{x^{\alpha-1}}{\beta^\alpha \Gamma(\alpha)}\exp(-x/\beta),\qquad x>0.
\]

With \(\pi(\beta)\propto \beta^{-1}\),

\[
\pi(\beta\mid x)\propto \beta^{-\alpha-1}\exp(-x/\beta).
\]

Since

\[
\int_0^\infty \beta^{-\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta}\,d\beta
= \frac{\Gamma(\alpha)}{x^\alpha},
\]

the posterior is

\[
\pi(\beta\mid x)=\frac{x^\alpha}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\beta^{-\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta}.
\]

The predictive density is therefore

\[
q_J(y\mid x)
= \int_0^\infty
\frac{y^{T\alpha-1}}{\beta^{T\alpha}\Gamma(T\alpha)}e^{-y/\beta}
\frac{x^\alpha}{\Gamma(\alpha)}\beta^{-\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta}\,d\beta.
\]

Let \(k=(T+1)\alpha\). Then

\[
q_J(y\mid x)
=
\frac{x^\alpha y^{T\alpha-1}}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(T\alpha)}
\int_0^\infty \beta^{-k-1}e^{-(x+y)/\beta}\,d\beta.
\]

Using

\[
\int_0^\infty \beta^{-k-1}e^{-s/\beta}\,d\beta
= \frac{\Gamma(k)}{s^k},\qquad s>0,k>0,
\]

we get

\[
\boxed{
q_J(y\mid x)
=
\frac{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(T\alpha)}
\frac{x^\alpha y^{T\alpha-1}}{(x+y)^{(T+1)\alpha}},
\qquad y>0.
}
\]

Verification of normalization:

\[
\int_0^\infty q_J(y\mid x)\,dy
=
\frac{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(T\alpha)}
x^\alpha
\int_0^\infty \frac{y^{T\alpha-1}}{(x+y)^{(T+1)\alpha}}\,dy.
\]

Set \(t=y/x\). Then \(dy=x\,dt\), and

\[
x^\alpha\int_0^\infty \frac{y^{T\alpha-1}}{(x+y)^{(T+1)\alpha}}\,dy
=
\int_0^\infty \frac{t^{T\alpha-1}}{(1+t)^{(T+1)\alpha}}\,dt.
\]

This is the beta integral

\[
B(T\alpha,\alpha)
=
\frac{\Gamma(T\alpha)\Gamma(\alpha)}{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}.
\]

Hence

\[
\int_0^\infty q_J(y\mid x)\,dy
=
\frac{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(T\alpha)}
\frac{\Gamma(T\alpha)\Gamma(\alpha)}{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}
=1.
\]

Thus \(q_J(\cdot\mid x)\) is a valid density.
