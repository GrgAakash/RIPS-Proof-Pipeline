1. Target Decomposition

target_label:  
Problem 30

target_type:  
Admissibility iff theorem for a generalized Bayes predictive density under Kullback-Leibler loss.

main_goal:  
Prove that the Jeffreys-prior Bayesian predictive density for \(Y\sim \operatorname{Ga}(T\alpha,\beta)\), based on independent \(X\sim \operatorname{Ga}(\alpha,\beta)\), is admissible exactly when \(\alpha>1\).

variables_and_parameters:  
\(\alpha>0\) known, \(T>0\) known, \(\beta>0\) unknown scale parameter, \(X\sim \operatorname{Ga}(\alpha,\beta)\), \(Y\sim \operatorname{Ga}(T\alpha,\beta)\), \(X\perp Y\mid \beta\).

conclusion_to_prove:  
Let \(q_J(y\mid x)\) be the Bayesian predictive density under \(\pi(\beta)\propto 1/\beta\). Under KL loss,
\[
L(\beta,q)=\int p(y;\,T\alpha,\beta)\log \frac{p(y;\,T\alpha,\beta)}{q(y\mid X)}\,dy,
\]
\(q_J\) is admissible iff \(\alpha>1\); for \(0<\alpha\le 1\), there exists a predictive density strictly dominating it.

2. Available Tools

tool:  
Gamma integral and posterior calculation

source_status:  
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:  
For \(s,z>0\),
\[
\int_0^\infty \beta^{-s-1}e^{-z/\beta}\,d\beta=\Gamma(s)z^{-s}.
\]
With prior \(\pi(\beta)\propto 1/\beta\), the posterior is inverse-gamma:
\[
\pi(\beta\mid x)\propto \beta^{-\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta}.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:  
Compute the Jeffreys-prior predictive density explicitly.

tool:  
Explicit Jeffreys predictive density

source_status:  
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:  
\[
q_J(y\mid x)
=
\frac{\Gamma((T+1)\alpha)}{\Gamma(\alpha)\Gamma(T\alpha)}
\frac{x^\alpha y^{T\alpha-1}}{(x+y)^{(T+1)\alpha}},
\qquad x,y>0.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:  
This is the decision rule whose admissibility is being characterized.

tool:  
Beta-gamma algebra

source_status:  
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:  
If \(X\sim \operatorname{Ga}(\alpha,\beta)\) and \(Y\sim \operatorname{Ga}(T\alpha,\beta)\) independently, then
\[
X+Y\sim \operatorname{Ga}((T+1)\alpha,\beta),
\]
and
\[
\frac{Y}{X+Y}\sim \operatorname{Beta}(T\alpha,\alpha),
\]
independently of \(X+Y\).

intended_role_in_proof:  
Simplifies risk expressions and boundary estimates.

tool:  
KL risk-difference identity

source_status:  
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:  
For predictive densities \(q,q_J\),
\[
R(\beta,q_J)-R(\beta,q)
=
E_\beta\left[\log \frac{q(Y\mid X)}{q_J(Y\mid X)}\right].
\]

intended_role_in_proof:  
Fixes the sign convention for domination: \(q\) dominates \(q_J\) iff this quantity is nonnegative for all \(\beta\) and positive for some \(\beta\).

tool:  
Proper-prior Bayes optimality under log score

source_status:  
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:  
For a proper prior \(\Pi\), the posterior predictive \(q_\Pi\) uniquely minimizes integrated KL risk, and
\[
\int \{R(\beta,q)-R(\beta,q_\Pi)\}\,\Pi(d\beta)
=
\int m_\Pi(x)\operatorname{KL}\!\left(q_\Pi(\cdot\mid x),q(\cdot\mid x)\right)\,dx
\ge 0.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:  
Supplies the Bayes-risk comparison needed for Blyth’s method.

tool:  
Blyth admissibility criterion

source_status:  
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:  
If an improper generalized Bayes rule \(q_J\) is the limit of proper Bayes rules \(q_n\) in the sense that their integrated Bayes-risk gaps against \(q_J\) vanish over expanding proper priors, then \(q_J\) is admissible.

intended_role_in_proof:  
Proves the \(\alpha>1\) admissibility half once suitable approximating priors are constructed.

tool:  
Perturbed-prior operator

source_status:  
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:  
For bounded positive \(h\), define
\[
A_s^h(z)
=
\frac{z^s}{\Gamma(s)}
\int_0^\infty \beta^{-s-1}e^{-z/\beta}h(\beta)\,d\beta.
\]
The predictive density based on \(\pi_h(\beta)\propto h(\beta)/\beta\) satisfies
\[
\frac{q_h(y\mid x)}{q_J(y\mid x)}
=
\frac{A_{(T+1)\alpha}^h(x+y)}{A_\alpha^h(x)}.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:  
Gives a concrete way to build and verify a dominating predictive density when \(0<\alpha\le 1\).

tool:  
Model-specific boundary/capacity dichotomy

source_status:  
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:  
The Jeffreys predictive admits Blyth approximations with vanishing boundary risk exactly for \(\alpha>1\). For \(0<\alpha\le 1\), one can construct a bounded positive nonconstant \(h\) such that
\[
E_\beta\left[
\log \frac{A_{(T+1)\alpha}^h(X+Y)}{A_\alpha^h(X)}
\right]\ge 0
\]
for every \(\beta>0\), with strict inequality for at least one \(\beta\).

intended_role_in_proof:  
This is the main technical dichotomy producing admissibility for \(\alpha>1\) and inadmissibility for \(0<\alpha\le 1\).

4. Hardest Step Prediction

hardest_step_id:  
SC3/SC4 boundary dichotomy at \(\alpha=1\).

hardest_step_description:  
The core difficulty is proving the exact threshold: Blyth cutoff errors must vanish when \(\alpha>1\), while for \(0<\alpha\le 1\) a genuine dominating perturbation must be constructed. The final proof must compute the relevant gamma-integral boundary terms explicitly enough that the inequality direction cannot be ambiguous.

risk_if_wrong:  
The proof could accidentally prove only minimaxity or best equivariance, not admissibility. It could also reverse the threshold or omit the borderline \(\alpha=1\).

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:  
Isolate the boundary estimate as a named lemma, prove it from the displayed gamma integrals, and check the sign using the exact KL risk-difference convention.

5. Failure-Mode Checks

circularity_check:  
Do not assume any result already equivalent to “\(q_J\) is admissible iff \(\alpha>1\).” The Blyth criterion may be used only as a general decision-theoretic tool, and the gamma boundary estimates must be proved inside the proof.

full_theorem_check:  
Both directions are required: admissibility for every \(T>0\) when \(\alpha>1\), and strict domination for every \(T>0\) when \(0<\alpha\le 1\).

source_check:  
No external sources, internet, hidden lemmas, or prior results specific to this theorem.

hypothesis_check:  
Use only \(\alpha>0\), \(T>0\), \(\beta>0\), independence of \(X,Y\), and the stated gamma densities.

notation_check:  
Keep \(\beta\) as the scale parameter. Distinguish the Jeffreys prior \(\pi(\beta)\propto 1/\beta\) from proper approximating priors and perturbed priors.

standard_background_check:  
Gamma integrals, beta-gamma factorization, KL/log-score Bayes optimality, and Blyth’s criterion are acceptable standard tools; any model-specific recurrence, capacity, or perturbation lemma must be proved.
