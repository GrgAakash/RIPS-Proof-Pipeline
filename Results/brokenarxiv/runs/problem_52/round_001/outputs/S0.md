1. Target Decomposition

target_label: Virtual fibering criterion from residual torsion-free nilpotence and vanishing \(L^2\)-Betti numbers.

target_type: existence theorem / virtual fibering theorem.

main_goal: Show that \(M\) has a finite cover \(M'\to M\) admitting a fibre bundle map \(M'\to S^1\).

variables_and_parameters:
\(M\): closed aspherical manifold.  
\(d\): dimension of \(M\), with \(d\ge 3\).  
\(G=\pi_1(M)\).  
Hypotheses: \(G\) is residually torsion-free nilpotent, and all \(L^2\)-Betti numbers of \(G\), over every field, vanish.

conclusion_to_prove:
There exists a finite-index subgroup \(G'\le G\) realized by a finite cover \(M'\to M\), and a fibre bundle projection \(M'\to S^1\).

2. Available Tools

tool: finite covers and finite-index subgroups  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: Connected finite covers of a connected manifold \(M\) correspond to finite-index subgroups of \(\pi_1(M)\).  
intended_role_in_proof: Translate virtual fibering into a group-theoretic finite-index statement.

tool: fibering over \(S^1\) and epimorphisms to \(\mathbb Z\)  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: A fibration \(M'\to S^1\) induces a surjection \(\pi_1(M')\to \mathbb Z\), whose kernel is the fundamental group of the fibre when the fibre is connected.  
intended_role_in_proof: Identify the algebraic form of the desired conclusion.

tool: algebraic fibering criterion  
source_status: proved inside the current proof  
exact_statement_or_fact: For the relevant finite cover \(M'\), if there is an epimorphism \(\phi:\pi_1(M')\to\mathbb Z\) whose kernel has the required finiteness property compatible with the dimension/topological category, then \(M'\) fibres over \(S^1\).  
intended_role_in_proof: Convert a group-theoretic construction into the required topological fibration.

tool: residual torsion-free nilpotence  
source_status: assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: For every nontrivial \(g\in G\), there is a homomorphism from \(G\) to a torsion-free nilpotent group in which the image of \(g\) is nontrivial.  
intended_role_in_proof: Supply many torsion-free nilpotent quotients and separate elements; expected to produce finite-index or quotient data leading to an epimorphism to \(\mathbb Z\).

tool: vanishing \(L^2\)-Betti numbers over every field  
source_status: assumption  
exact_statement_or_fact: All \(L^2\)-Betti numbers of \(G\), with coefficients over every field in the intended sense, are zero.  
intended_role_in_proof: Provide homological constraints strong enough to force an algebraic fibering structure.

tool: asphericity  
source_status: assumption / standard background  
exact_statement_or_fact: Since \(M\) is aspherical, \(M\) is a \(K(G,1)\), so topological finiteness information about \(M\) is reflected in group-theoretic finiteness information about \(G\).  
intended_role_in_proof: Allow the proof to move between manifold topology and group finiteness properties.

tool: finite-index stability  
source_status: standard background fact  
exact_statement_or_fact: Asphericity, closedness of covers, residual torsion-free nilpotence, and vanishing \(L^2\)-Betti numbers are stable under passing to finite-index subgroups, in the forms needed by the argument.  
intended_role_in_proof: Ensure the constructed finite cover still satisfies the hypotheses needed for later steps.

4. Hardest Step Prediction

hardest_step_id: C4

hardest_step_description:
The difficult point is showing that the \(L^2\)-Betti vanishing hypothesis over every field controls the kernel of an epimorphism to \(\mathbb Z\) strongly enough to imply the finiteness condition needed for fibering.

risk_if_wrong:
If C4 only proves existence of a map to \(\mathbb Z\), or only proves weak homological vanishing, the argument will not yield a fibre bundle. The theorem could become circular if C4 secretly imports a virtual fibering theorem.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The final proof must state the precise finiteness property required, prove it directly from the residual torsion-free nilpotent and \(L^2\)-Betti assumptions, and avoid using any theorem equivalent to the target virtual fibering statement.

6. Subproblem Assignment Table

S1:
Prove the reduction from virtual fibering of \(M\) to finding a finite-index subgroup \(G'\le\pi_1(M)\) with an epimorphism \(G'\to\mathbb Z\) satisfying the exact kernel finiteness condition required by the chosen fibering criterion. State that criterion precisely and identify its dimensional/topological assumptions.

S2:
Verify which hypotheses pass to finite-index subgroups: closed aspherical finite covers, residual torsion-free nilpotence, and vanishing \(L^2\)-Betti numbers over every field. Give exact statements and proofs or standard citations allowed as background.

S3:
Using only residual torsion-free nilpotence and standard group theory, construct or isolate a finite-index subgroup \(G'\) admitting an epimorphism to \(\mathbb Z\). Make clear whether this requires positive first Betti number, a nilpotent quotient with infinite abelianization, or another argument.

S4:
Prove the key homological implication: from the vanishing \(L^2\)-Betti numbers over every field, together with the residual torsion-free nilpotent structure, show that the kernel of the epimorphism \(G'\to\mathbb Z\) has the finiteness property needed by S1.

S5:
Complete the topological conversion. Given \(M'\) closed aspherical of dimension \(d\ge3\) and \(\phi:\pi_1(M')\to\mathbb Z\) with the finiteness condition from S4, prove that \(M'\) fibres over \(S^1\), then conclude that \(M\) virtually fibres over \(S^1\).
