2. Subproof or failure

Let \(p:M'\to M\) be a finite covering of a closed aspherical manifold \(M\) of dimension \(d\).

First, \(M'\) is a closed \(d\)-manifold. Covering maps are local homeomorphisms, so \(M'\) is a \(d\)-manifold. Since \(p\) is finite-sheeted and \(M\) is compact, \(M'\) is compact. Since \(M\) has no boundary, local charts lift to boundaryless local charts, so \(M'\) is closed.

Second, \(M'\) is aspherical. Let \(\widetilde M\to M\) be the universal cover of \(M\). Since \(M'\to M\) is a covering, the universal cover of \(M'\) is canonically also \(\widetilde M\), up to covering equivalence. Because \(M\) is aspherical, \(\widetilde M\) is contractible. Hence the universal cover of \(M'\) is contractible, so \(M'\) is aspherical.

Now let \(G\) be residually torsion-free nilpotent and let \(H\leq G\) be finite index. To prove \(H\) is residually torsion-free nilpotent, take any nontrivial \(h\in H\). Since \(G\) is residually torsion-free nilpotent, there exists a homomorphism
\[
\phi:G\to N
\]
to a torsion-free nilpotent group \(N\) such that \(\phi(h)\neq 1\). The restriction \(\phi|_H:H\to N\) still detects \(h\). Therefore every nontrivial element of \(H\) survives in some torsion-free nilpotent quotient, so \(H\) is residually torsion-free nilpotent. The finite-index assumption is not needed for this part; residual torsion-free nilpotence passes to all subgroups by restriction.

Finally, assume all \(L^2\)-Betti numbers of \(G\) over every field vanish. Let \(H\leq G\) have finite index \(m=[G:H]\). Use the standard finite-index scaling formula for \(L^2\)-Betti numbers over a field \(k\):
\[
b_i^{(2),k}(H)=m\, b_i^{(2),k}(G)
\]
for every \(i\geq 0\). Since \(b_i^{(2),k}(G)=0\) for every \(i\) and every field \(k\), this gives
\[
b_i^{(2),k}(H)=m\cdot 0=0
\]
for every \(i\) and every field \(k\). Thus vanishing over every field passes to finite-index subgroups.

This proves the assigned finite-index stability statements.
