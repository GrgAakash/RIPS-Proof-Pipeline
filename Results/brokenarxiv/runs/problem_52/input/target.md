Problem 52:
A closed manifold virtually fibres over the circle if it admits a finite cover $M' \to M$ such that $M'$ fibres over the circle. Let $M$ be an aspherical closed manifold of dimension $d \ge 3$. If the fundamental group of $M$ is residually (torsion-free and nilpotent) and all its $L^2$-Betti numbers vanish over every field, then $M$ virtually fibres over the circle.
