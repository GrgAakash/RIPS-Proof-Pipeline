2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED.

The assigned subclaim is not a precise mathematical statement as written, and in its natural universal interpretation it is false from the supplied hypotheses.

Indeed, take `X` to be a one-point coarse space. Then `l^p(X)` is one-dimensional for every `p in (1,infty)`, and every controlled propagation operator is just multiplication by a scalar. Hence

`C_u[X,E] = C`

and its norm completion is also

`B^p_u(X,E) = C`

for every `p`.

The closed two-sided ideals of `C` are exactly `{0}` and `C`. Both are geometric: `{0} ∩ C_u[X,E] = {0}` is dense in `{0}`, and `C ∩ C_u[X,E] = C` is dense in `C`. Therefore the geometric-ideal lattice is the same two-element lattice for every exponent `p`.

Consequently this lattice cannot recover any exponent-sensitive `l^p` operator-ideal structure in general. For example, for `p=2` and `q=3`, the geometric-ideal lattices are isomorphic, but the exponents are neither equal nor conjugate, since `2 != 3` and `1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6 != 1`.

Thus SC4 cannot be proved from the allowed inputs without an additional nontrivial hypothesis or an additional rigidity theorem.
