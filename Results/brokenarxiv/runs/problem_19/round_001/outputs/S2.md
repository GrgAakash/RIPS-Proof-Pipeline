2. Subproof Or Failure

Assume \(\lambda_2(G)\le 0\) and assume that
\[
\sqrt{m-\frac n2-\frac12}
\]
is real. For the real square root to be real, its radicand is nonnegative:
\[
m-\frac n2-\frac12\ge 0.
\]
Therefore
\[
\sqrt{m-\frac n2-\frac12}\ge 0.
\]
Combining this with the hypothesis \(\lambda_2(G)\le 0\), we get
\[
\lambda_2(G)\le 0\le \sqrt{m-\frac n2-\frac12}.
\]
Hence
\[
\lambda_2(G)\le \sqrt{m-\frac n2-\frac12}.
\]
This proves the assigned sign-case subclaim.
