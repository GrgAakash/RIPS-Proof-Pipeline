**1. Assignment Restatement**

Subclaim A: For each `x0 in X` and each open neighbourhood `V` of `x0`, find a clopen set `U` with `x0 in U subset V`. Also verify that restricting a W*-bundle `E` to such a clopen `U` has the expected fibres and traces, and that proving triviality on such neighbourhoods proves local triviality.

**2. Subproof Or Failure**

Solved.

Since `X` is compact Hausdorff, it is normal. Because `dim(X)=0`, the standard zero-dimensional normal-space separation theorem applies:

For every closed set `F subset X` and open set `O superset F`, there is a clopen set `C` such that  
`F subset C subset O`.

Apply this with `F={x0}` and `O=V`. Then there is a clopen neighbourhood `U` of `x0` with

`x0 in U subset V`.

Thus every point has arbitrarily small clopen neighbourhoods.

Now let `U subset X` be clopen. The restriction `E|_U` is the W*-bundle over `U` obtained by restricting the central `C(X)`-structure to `C(U)`. Equivalently, since `U` is clopen, the characteristic function `1_U` is a projection in `C(X)`, so the restricted bundle is represented by the corner/direct summand

`E|_U = 1_U E`

with base algebra `C(U)=1_U C(X)`.

For each `x in U`, the fibre of the restricted bundle is unchanged:

`(E|_U)_x = E_x`.

Indeed, evaluation at `x` only depends on the germ over `x`, and passing from `X` to the clopen subspace `U` does not change the fibre ideal at `x`.

The trace also restricts as expected. If the W*-bundle has fibre traces `tau_x` on `E_x`, then the restricted W*-bundle has the same traces indexed by `x in U`:

`tau_x^{E|_U}(a)=tau_x^E(a)` for `a in E|_U`.

Finally, local triviality over clopen neighbourhoods implies local triviality over `X`: local triviality means that for each `x0 in X`, there exists an open neighbourhood `W` of `x0` such that `E|_W` is isomorphic to the trivial bundle over `W`. Since every neighbourhood of `x0` contains a clopen neighbourhood `U`, if one proves triviality on such a `U`, then `U` is already an open neighbourhood of `x0`. Hence `E` is locally trivial.
