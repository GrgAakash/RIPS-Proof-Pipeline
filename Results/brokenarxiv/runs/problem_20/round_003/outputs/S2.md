**1 Assignment Restatement**

For fixed `x0`, finite `F ⊂ E`, finite `G ⊂ M`, and `epsilon > 0`, prove a finite-stage local statement: after shrinking to a clopen neighbourhood `U` of `x0`, the fibre isomorphism `theta: E_x0 -> M` and finite lift choices give maps between `E|_U` and the trivial bundle `C_sigma(U,M)` whose multiplication, trace, and `*`/linearity defects on `F` and `G` are uniformly `< epsilon` in every fibre over `U`.

**2 Subproof Or Failure**

Let `pi_x: E -> E_x` be the fibre quotient and `tau_x` the fibre trace. Since `M` is a II_1 factor, a unital normal isomorphism `theta: E_x0 -> M` preserves the normalized trace.

Fixed-neighbourhood lemma: if `D ⊂ E` is finite and `||pi_x0(d)||_{2,x0}=0` for all `d ∈ D`, then for every `eta > 0` there is a clopen `U` with `x0 ∈ U` and
`sup_{u∈U} ||pi_u(d)||_{2,u} < eta` for all `d ∈ D`.

Proof: for each `d`, the function `u ↦ ||pi_u(d)||_{2,u}` is continuous by the W*-bundle definition, since it is `tau_u(pi_u(d*d))^{1/2}`. The common set where all these functions are `< eta` is an open neighbourhood of `x0`. Since compact Hausdorff dimension zero gives a clopen neighbourhood base, shrink to a clopen `U` inside it. The same argument applies to finitely many scalar trace defects in `C(X)`.

Now choose a finite-dimensional `*`-closed subspace `V ⊂ M` containing `1`, `G`, all products `gh` with `g,h ∈ G`, and all elements `theta(pi_x0(f))` for `f ∈ F`. Choose a unital `*`-linear lift
`L: V -> E`
such that `pi_x0(L(v)) = theta^{-1}(v)`. This is standard: lift a real basis of `V_sa`, symmetrize the lifts, send `1` to `1`, and extend complex-linearly.

For a clopen `U`, define the finite-stage maps
`Phi_U: E|_U -> C_sigma(U,M)` by
`Phi_U(a|_U)(u) = theta(pi_x0(a))`,
and
`Psi_U: V -> E|_U` by
`Psi_U(v) = L(v)|_U`,
identifying `V` with constant sections of `C_sigma(U,M)`.

`Phi_U` is an exact unital `*`-homomorphism. Thus its multiplicativity, linearity, and `*`-defects are zero. Its trace defect on `F` is
`|tau_M(theta(pi_x0(f))) - tau_u(pi_u(f))|`,
which is zero at `u=x0` and continuous in `u`.

For `Psi_U`, the only nontrivial algebra defect on `G` is
`d_{g,h} = L(gh) - L(g)L(h)`.
At `x0`,
`pi_x0(d_{g,h}) = theta^{-1}(gh) - theta^{-1}(g)theta^{-1}(h) = 0`.
The trace defect
`u ↦ tau_u(pi_u(L(g))) - tau_M(g)`
also vanishes at `x0`. Since `L` is `*`-linear, the linearity and `*`-defects are exactly zero. If noncoherent prechosen lifts are used instead, include the finitely many linearity and `*` defect sections in the same finite set; they also vanish at `x0`.

Apply the fixed-neighbourhood lemma to all multiplication defects `d_{g,h}`, all inverse defects `L(theta(pi_x0(f))) - f`, and the finitely many trace defect functions. After shrinking to a clopen `U ∋ x0`, for every `u ∈ U`:
`||Psi_U(gh)_u - Psi_U(g)_u Psi_U(h)_u||_2 < epsilon`,
`|tau_u(Psi_U(g)_u) - tau_M(g)| < epsilon`,
`|tau_M(Phi_U(f)(u)) - tau_u(f_u)| < epsilon`,
and
`||Psi_U(Phi_U(f))_u - f_u||_2 < epsilon`.
Also `Phi_U(Psi_U(g)) = g` exactly as a constant section.

This proves Subclaim B for each fixed finite stage.

This argument cannot be upgraded to one clopen `U` for all finite stages. It only handles finitely many continuous defect functions at a time. A countable intersection of clopen neighbourhoods of `x0` need not contain any neighbourhood of `x0`, even in a compact zero-dimensional space.
