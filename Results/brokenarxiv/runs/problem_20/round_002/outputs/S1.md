## 1 Assignment Restatement

Prove only SC1 and SC7: for an arbitrary point `x0 in X`, it is enough to find one clopen neighbourhood `U` of `x0` and a W*-bundle isomorphism

```text
E|_U ≅ C_sigma(U, M),
```

where `M` is the fixed II_1 factor isomorphic to every fibre. Then `E` is locally trivial.

## 2 Subproof

**Standard background used.**  
Zero-dimensional compact Hausdorff clopen-base theorem: if `X` is compact Hausdorff and `dim(X)=0`, then every point has a neighbourhood basis of clopen sets. Thus for every `x0 in X` and every open neighbourhood `O` of `x0`, there is a clopen `U` with

```text
x0 in U subset O.
```

This is exactly the topological input needed.

**SC1: clopen restriction reduction.**  
Fix `x0 in X`. Since `dim(X)=0`, we may restrict attention to clopen neighbourhoods `U` of `x0`. For such `U`, the restriction `E|_U` is again a W*-bundle over the compact Hausdorff space `U`, with fibre at `u in U` equal to the original fibre `E_u`. Because all fibres of `E` are isomorphic to `M`, all fibres of `E|_U` are also isomorphic to `M`.

Therefore, to prove local triviality at `x0`, it suffices to construct a W*-bundle isomorphism

```text
Phi_U : E|_U -> C_sigma(U, M)
```

for some clopen neighbourhood `U` of `x0`.

**SC7: conclusion from the local model.**  
Assume SC6 supplies, for the fixed clopen neighbourhood `U` from the finite-stage construction, an exact W*-bundle isomorphism

```text
Phi_U : E|_U ≅ C_sigma(U, M).
```

Since `U` is clopen, in particular `U` is open in `X`. Thus `U` is an open neighbourhood of `x0` on which `E` is isomorphic to the trivial W*-bundle with fibre `M`.

Because `x0` was arbitrary, every point of `X` has an open neighbourhood over which `E` is trivial. Hence `E` is locally trivial.

This proves SC1 and SC7, conditional only on the existence of the exact isomorphism over the fixed clopen neighbourhood supplied by the other subproblems.
