**2 Subproof**

Let `A` and `B` be W*-bundles over a compact Hausdorff space `U`, with central `C(U)`-structure and conditional expectations

```text
E_A : A -> C(U),      E_B : B -> C(U).
```

Write

```text
||a||_{2,U} = sup_{u in U} E_A(a*a)(u)^{1/2}
```

and similarly on `B`. A W*-bundle isomorphism means a unital `*`-isomorphism `Phi : A -> B` which is `C(U)`-linear and satisfies `E_B(Phi(a)) = E_A(a)`.

Standard background used:

1. The closed unit ball of a W*-bundle is complete for the uniform `2`-norm.
2. Multiplication is jointly uniformly continuous on norm-bounded sets for the uniform `2`-norm:
   ```text
   ||xy - x'y'||_2 <= ||x - x'||_2 ||y|| + ||x'|| ||y - y'||_2.
   ```
3. A unital `2`-norm continuous `*`-homomorphism between W*-bundles which is `C(U)`-linear and trace-preserving is a W*-bundle morphism.

Here is the useful form of SC6.

**Approximate-Intertwining Lemma.**  
Let `A` and `B` be W*-bundles over compact `U`. Let

```text
F_1 subset F_2 subset ... subset A_{<=1},
G_1 subset G_2 subset ... subset B_{<=1}
```

be countable, `*`-closed, unital, `2`-norm dense subsets of the unit balls, stable under multiplication up to later stages in the following sense: for every `x,y` in the union of the `F_n`, the product `xy` belongs to the `2`-norm closure of the union, and similarly for `G_n`.

Suppose there are unital maps on finite stages

```text
phi_n : F_n -> B_{<=1},
psi_n : G_n -> A_{<=1}
```

and numbers `epsilon_n -> 0`, such that after passing to larger stages whenever necessary:

1. `phi_n` and `psi_n` are asymptotically `C(U)`-linear:
   ```text
   ||phi_n(fx) - f phi_n(x)||_{2,U} <= epsilon_n,
   ||psi_n(fy) - f psi_n(y)||_{2,U} <= epsilon_n
   ```
   for the finitely many `f in C(U)` and stage elements under consideration.

2. They are asymptotically `*`-preserving and multiplicative:
   ```text
   ||phi_n(x*) - phi_n(x)*||_{2,U} <= epsilon_n,
   ||phi_n(xy) - phi_n(x)phi_n(y)||_{2,U} <= epsilon_n,
   ```
   and similarly for `psi_n`.

3. They are asymptotically trace-preserving:
   ```text
   ||E_B(phi_n(x)) - E_A(x)||_{C(U)} <= epsilon_n,
   ||E_A(psi_n(y)) - E_B(y)||_{C(U)} <= epsilon_n.
   ```

4. They are asymptotic inverses on finite stages:
   ```text
   ||psi_n(phi_n(x)) - x||_{2,U} <= epsilon_n       for x in F_n,
   ||phi_n(psi_n(y)) - y||_{2,U} <= epsilon_n       for y in G_n.
   ```

5. Coherence: for each fixed stage element `x` in the union of the `F_n`, the sequence `phi_n(x)` is Cauchy in `||.||_{2,U}` once defined; similarly, for each fixed `y`, the sequence `psi_n(y)` is Cauchy.

Then there is a unique W*-bundle isomorphism

```text
Phi : A -> B
```

such that for every fixed `x` in the union of the `F_n`,

```text
Phi(x) = lim_n phi_n(x)
```

in uniform `2`-norm. Its inverse is the map obtained from the limits of the `psi_n`.

**Proof.**

For each `x` in the dense union `F = union F_n`, define

```text
Phi_0(x) = lim_n phi_n(x)
```

in `B_{<=1}`. This limit exists by coherence and uniform `2`-norm completeness of the closed unit ball of `B`.

Similarly, for `y in G = union G_n`, define

```text
Psi_0(y) = lim_n psi_n(y)
```

in `A_{<=1}`.

The limiting maps preserve the algebraic structure on the dense sets. For example, if `x,x*,xy` are eventually in the finite stages, then

```text
Phi_0(x*) = Phi_0(x)*,
Phi_0(xy) = Phi_0(x)Phi_0(y),
```

because the corresponding errors are bounded by `epsilon_n -> 0`, and multiplication is uniformly `2`-continuous on norm-bounded sets. The same argument gives

```text
Psi_0(y*) = Psi_0(y)*,
Psi_0(yz) = Psi_0(y)Psi_0(z).
```

Likewise, the asymptotic `C(U)`-linearity gives

```text
Phi_0(fx) = f Phi_0(x),
Psi_0(fy) = f Psi_0(y),
```

for the relevant dense `C(U)`-linear stage data, and then by continuity for all `f in C(U)`.

Trace preservation also passes to the limit. Indeed,

```text
E_B(Phi_0(x)) = lim_n E_B(phi_n(x)) = E_A(x),
```

uniformly in `C(U)`, and similarly

```text
E_A(Psi_0(y)) = E_B(y).
```

Now the inverse identities pass to the limit. For `x in F`,

```text
Psi_0(Phi_0(x)) = x.
```

To justify this, approximate `Phi_0(x)` in uniform `2`-norm by elements of `G`, use the uniform `2`-continuity of `Psi_0` on bounded sets, and use the finite-stage inverse estimate

```text
||psi_n(phi_n(x)) - x||_{2,U} -> 0.
```

The same argument gives

```text
Phi_0(Psi_0(y)) = y
```

for `y in G`.

Because `Phi_0` is trace-preserving, it is an isometry for the uniform `2`-norm on the dense unit ball:

```text
||Phi_0(x)||_{2,U}^2
= ||E_B(Phi_0(x)^*Phi_0(x))||_\infty
= ||E_A(x^*x)||_\infty
= ||x||_{2,U}^2.
```

Thus `Phi_0` extends uniquely by uniform `2`-norm continuity from the dense unit ball of `A` to the whole closed unit ball of `A`, and then by homogeneity to all of `A`. Call the extension `Phi`. Similarly `Psi_0` extends to a map `Psi : B -> A`.

The algebraic identities extend from dense sets to all norm-bounded elements by uniform `2`-continuity of multiplication and involution. Hence `Phi` and `Psi` are unital `*`-homomorphisms, `C(U)`-linear, and trace-preserving. The inverse identities extend by continuity:

```text
Psi Phi = id_A,
Phi Psi = id_B.
```

Therefore `Phi` is a bijective unital `C(U)`-linear trace-preserving `*`-homomorphism, with inverse `Psi`. Hence `Phi` is a W*-bundle isomorphism.

This proves SC6.
