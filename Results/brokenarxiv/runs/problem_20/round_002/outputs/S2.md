## 1 Assignment Restatement

Prove the finite local input for the later approximate intertwining step:

Given an isomorphism `θ: M -> E_{x0}`, and finite data in the fixed II_1 factor `M`, choose lifts of that data to sections of `E` near `x0`. After shrinking to a clopen neighbourhood `U` of `x0`, these lifts preserve the prescribed finite algebraic and trace relations up to a chosen tolerance. Then package them as finite-stage, approximately multiplicative, approximately trace-preserving, `C(U)`-linear maps from finite-dimensional-looking pieces of `C_sigma(U,M)` into `E|_U`.

## 2 Subproof

Fix standard notation for a W*-bundle `E` over compact Hausdorff `X`: it has fibre maps `π_x: E -> E_x`, fibre traces `τ_x`, and uniform 2-norm

```text
||a||_{2,u} = sup_{x in X} τ_x(π_x(a)^*π_x(a))^{1/2}.
```

Standard background used: for each fixed `a in E`, the function

```text
x |-> ||π_x(a)||_{2,τ_x}
```

is continuous, and the quotient map `π_x0: E -> E_x0` is surjective. These are part of the usual W*-bundle framework.

### SC2: finite lifts near `x0`

Let `F ⊂ M` be finite and let `θ: M -> E_x0` be a unital trace-preserving *-isomorphism. For every `m in F`, choose `\tilde m in E` with

```text
π_x0(\tilde m) = θ(m).
```

This is possible because `π_x0` is surjective. Enlarging `F` if necessary, include `1`, adjoints, products, and any elements whose traces are to be checked. Choose the lifts compatibly where possible: set `\widetilde 1 = 1_E`.

For finite algebraic relations, define the finite error sections

```text
r_{m,n} = \widetilde{mn} - \tilde m \tilde n,
s_m = \widetilde{m^*} - \tilde m^*,
q_{α,m,β,n} = \widetilde{αm+βn} - α\tilde m - β\tilde n.
```

At `x0`, all these vanish in the fibre because `θ` is a *-homomorphism:

```text
π_x0(r_{m,n}) = 0,
π_x0(s_m) = 0,
π_x0(q_{α,m,β,n}) = 0.
```

By fibrewise 2-norm continuity, for any `ε > 0`, there is an open neighbourhood `V` of `x0` such that all these finitely many sections have fibre 2-norm `< ε` on `V`.

Since `X` has covering dimension zero and is compact Hausdorff, it has a clopen neighbourhood basis. Hence choose a clopen `U` with

```text
x0 ∈ U ⊂ V.
```

Then the restricted lifts `\tilde m|_U ∈ E|_U` satisfy the prescribed finite algebraic relations uniformly in 2-norm over `U`.

Trace relations are handled similarly. For `m in F`, the scalar function

```text
x |-> τ_x(π_x(\tilde m))
```

is continuous, and at `x0` it equals

```text
τ_{x0}(θ(m)) = τ_M(m).
```

Shrinking `U` again, still clopen, gives

```text
sup_{x in U} |τ_x(π_x(\tilde m)) - τ_M(m)| < ε
```

for all finitely many `m` under consideration.

This proves SC2.

### SC3: one-direction finite maps

Let `Ω ⊂ C_sigma(U,M)` be a finite set. For the finite-stage argument, assume `Ω` is contained in a finite `C(U)`-linear span

```text
Ω ⊂ span_{C(U)}{1 ⊗ m_1, ..., 1 ⊗ m_k},
```

with `m_i ∈ M`. This is the relevant finite piece used later in approximate intertwining.

Apply SC2 to a finite set `F ⊂ M` containing:

```text
1, m_1,...,m_k,
m_i^*, m_i m_j,
```

and any additional finite linear combinations needed to test the chosen finite relations.

Choose clopen `U` and lifts `\tilde m_i ∈ E|_U` satisfying the finite estimates from SC2.

Define a `C(U)`-linear map on the finite `C(U)`-module

```text
P = span_{C(U)}{1 ⊗ m_1, ..., 1 ⊗ m_k}
```

by

```text
Φ_U(Σ_i f_i ⊗ m_i) = Σ_i f_i \tilde m_i.
```

If linear dependence among the `m_i` is relevant, first choose `m_1,...,m_k` linearly independent over `C`, or quotient by the finite kernel of the presentation. On this finite piece the formula is well-defined.

The estimates from SC2 imply:

```text
||Φ_U(ab) - Φ_U(a)Φ_U(b)||_{2,u,U} < ε
```

for all tested products `a,b` in the chosen finite piece, after including the corresponding products in `F`.

They also imply approximate *-preservation:

```text
||Φ_U(a^*) - Φ_U(a)^*||_{2,u,U} < ε,
```

and approximate trace preservation:

```text
sup_{x in U} |τ_x(π_x(Φ_U(a))) - τ_M(a(x))| < ε
```

for all tested `a`.

Thus SC3 is established: finite pieces of the trivial bundle `C_sigma(U,M)` admit `C(U)`-linear maps into `E|_U` which, on prescribed finite data, are approximately multiplicative, approximately *-preserving, and approximately trace-preserving.
