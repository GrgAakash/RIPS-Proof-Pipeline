1. **Assignment Restatement**

Prove SC4 only: over one fixed clopen \(U\subset X\), construct finite-stage reverse maps from \(B=E|_U\) to \(A=C_\sigma(U,M)\), using chosen fibre isomorphisms \(B_x\cong M\), and prove the prescribed finite approximate inverse estimates.

2. **Subproof**

Let \(q_x:B\to B_x\) be the fibre quotient and \(\|\cdot\|_{2,u}\) the uniform fibre \(2\)-norm. Let \(\mathcal F\subset B_{\le 1}\), \(\mathcal G\subset A_{\le 1}\), \(\mathcal H\subset C(U)_{\le 1}\) be finite, and let \(\varepsilon>0\).

Standard background used:

- Dimension-zero partition fact: for finitely many continuous scalar functions and finitely many \(2\)-norm-continuous \(M\)-valued functions on compact \(U\), there is a finite clopen partition on which all prescribed oscillations are \(<\varepsilon\). This follows from the clopen basis/refinement characterization of compact Hausdorff dimension zero.
- W*-bundle continuity: for \(b\in B\), \(x\mapsto \|q_x(b)\|_{2,x}\) and \(x\mapsto \tau_x(q_x(b))\) are continuous.
- Fibre lifting: \(q_x:B\to B_x\) is surjective by definition of the fibre quotient.

Choose a finite clopen partition \(\mathcal P\) of \(U\) such that, for all \(V\in\mathcal P\) and \(s,t\in V\),

\[
\|g(s)-g(t)\|_{2,M}<\varepsilon,\quad g\in\mathcal G,
\]
\[
|h(s)-h(t)|<\varepsilon,\quad h\in\mathcal H,
\]
\[
|\tau_s(q_s(a))-\tau_t(q_t(a))|<\varepsilon,\quad a\in\mathcal F,
\]
and
\[
\big|\|q_s(a-a')\|_{2,s}-\|q_t(a-a')\|_{2,t}\big|<\varepsilon,\quad a,a'\in\mathcal F.
\]

For each \(V\in\mathcal P\), choose \(x_V\in V\) and a trace-preserving isomorphism
\[
\theta_V:M\cong B_{x_V}.
\]

Define the reverse sampling map \(\Psi:B\to A\) by

\[
\Psi(b)(t)=\theta_V^{-1}(q_{x_V}(b)),\qquad t\in V.
\]

This is locally constant, hence lies in \(C_\sigma(U,M)\). It is a unital \(*\)-homomorphism: evaluation at \(x_V\), followed by \(\theta_V^{-1}\), is a \(*\)-homomorphism on each clopen piece.

Now define compatible finite forward data on
\[
\mathcal Y=\mathcal G\cup \Psi(\mathcal F).
\]
For each \(y\in\Psi(\mathcal F)\), choose one representative \(r(y)\in\mathcal F\) with \(\Psi(r(y))=y\), and set \(\Phi(y)=r(y)\). For \(g\in\mathcal G\setminus \Psi(\mathcal F)\), choose lifts \(b_{V,g}\in B\) with
\[
q_{x_V}(b_{V,g})=\theta_V(g(x_V)),
\]
and set
\[
\Phi(g)=\sum_{V\in\mathcal P}1_V b_{V,g}.
\]

Then the reverse inverse estimates hold.

For \(a\in\mathcal F\), let \(a_0=r(\Psi(a))\). Since \(\Psi(a_0)=\Psi(a)\), for every \(V\),
\[
q_{x_V}(a_0-a)=0.
\]
By the partition control on \(x\mapsto \|q_x(a_0-a)\|_2\), for \(t\in V\),
\[
\|q_t(\Phi(\Psi(a))-a)\|_2=\|q_t(a_0-a)\|_2<\varepsilon.
\]
Hence
\[
\|\Phi(\Psi(a))-a\|_{2,u}<\varepsilon.
\]

For \(g\in\mathcal G\), if \(g\in\Psi(\mathcal F)\), then \(\Psi(\Phi(g))=g\) by definition. Otherwise, for \(t\in V\),
\[
\Psi(\Phi(g))(t)
=\theta_V^{-1}(q_{x_V}(b_{V,g}))
=g(x_V),
\]
so
\[
\|\Psi(\Phi(g))(t)-g(t)\|_2<\varepsilon.
\]
Thus
\[
\|\Psi(\Phi(g))-g\|_{2,u}<\varepsilon.
\]

The reverse map also has the finite W*-bundle controls needed later. For \(h\in\mathcal H\), \(a\in\mathcal F\), and \(t\in V\),
\[
\Psi(ha)(t)=h(x_V)\Psi(a)(t),
\]
so
\[
\|\Psi(ha)-h\Psi(a)\|_{2,u}<\varepsilon.
\]
Also,
\[
\tau_A(\Psi(a))(t)=\tau_{x_V}(q_{x_V}(a)),
\]
hence
\[
\|\tau_A(\Psi(a))-\tau_B(a)\|_\infty<\varepsilon.
\]

This proves SC4 in finite-stage form over the fixed clopen \(U\).
