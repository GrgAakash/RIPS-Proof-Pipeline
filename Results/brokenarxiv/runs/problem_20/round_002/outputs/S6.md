\[

\]

3. S3 gives finite reverse data over a fixed clopen \(U\): for finite tests in \(E|_U\) and \(C_\sigma(U,M)\), partitioning \(U\) into finitely many clopen pieces and sampling fibres gives reverse maps with finite inverse-control estimates.

4. S5 gives a valid formal approximate-intertwining lemma, but only conditional on having all finite-stage maps over one compact base \(U\), with coherence and errors tending to \(0\).

5. [KEY STEP] The missing assertion would be: there exists a single clopen neighbourhood \(U\ni x_0\) on which the S2/S3 finite-stage constructions can be performed for an increasing dense sequence of finite sets with errors tending to \(0\). S4 does not prove this. It only says to choose \(U\) after finitely many calibration estimates and then work inside \(E|_U\). That does not imply later finite-stage estimates remain controllable on the same \(U\). In S2, each new finite stage may require a further shrinking of the neighbourhood, and the intersection of countably many clopen neighbourhoods need not be an open neighbourhood of \(x_0\). No supplied argument prevents this shrinkage problem.

6. Therefore the hypotheses needed to invoke S5 are not established from the allowed materials. The proof cannot be completed without an additional lemma or argument proving fixed-neighbourhood approximate intertwining data.
