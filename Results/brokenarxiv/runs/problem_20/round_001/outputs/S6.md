<!-- BEGIN_FINAL_PROOF -->
FINAL PROOF NOT COMPLETED.

Blocking point: the supplied packet does not contain enough proved data to upgrade finite-stage approximate local trivializations to an exact W*-bundle isomorphism on one fixed neighbourhood of a point.

More precisely, let \(x\in X\), and let \(M\) denote the fixed \(\mathrm{II}_1\) factor to which every fibre \(E_y\) is isomorphic. By S1, \(X\) has a clopen neighbourhood basis. By S2, any finite subset of \(E_x\), or of \(M\) after choosing a fibre isomorphism, can be lifted to local sections near \(x\), and finite strict fibrewise trace and \(2\)-norm estimates persist after shrinking to a clopen neighbourhood. By S3, for each finite package of test data one can shrink to a clopen neighbourhood and obtain finite-stage approximate back-and-forth data between \(E|_U\) and the trivial bundle \(C_\sigma(U,M)\).

However, S3 explicitly supplies only finite-stage systems. It does not produce a single clopen neighbourhood \(U\) and coherent sequences of maps on increasing dense sets with uniform \(2\)-norm errors tending to zero. S4 explicitly states that the exactness step is unsolved without such data. In particular, there are no proved maps
\[
\alpha_n:E|_U\to C_\sigma(U,M),\qquad
\beta_n:C_\sigma(U,M)\to E|_U
\]
or finite-domain substitutes on one fixed \(U\), satisfying the quantitative hypotheses needed to define limiting maps and prove multiplicativity, trace preservation, fibrewise normality, central \(C(U)\)-linearity, and inverse identities.

[KEY STEP] The missing step is therefore the approximate-intertwining upgrade:
from finite-stage local back-and-forth estimates near \(x\), construct an exact W*-bundle isomorphism
\[
E|_U \cong C_\sigma(U,M)
\]
for some neighbourhood \(U\) of \(x\).
This step is not proved by S1-S5, is not an allowed supporting statement, and cannot be silently imported as standard background because it is a task-specific structural theorem carrying essentially the force needed for local triviality.

Consequently, the final assembly from S5 also remains conditional: if such an exact local W*-bundle isomorphism were available for every \(x\in X\), then those isomorphisms would be precisely local trivializations, and \(E\) would be locally trivial. But the required exact local isomorphisms have not been established from the supplied allowed inputs.
