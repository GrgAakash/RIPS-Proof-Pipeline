1. Target decomposition

target_label: Unspecified target theorem  
target_type: local triviality theorem for W*-bundles  
main_goal: Show that a W*-bundle over a compact Hausdorff zero-dimensional base with all fibres isomorphic to one fixed II_1 factor is locally trivial.  
variables_and_parameters:  
`X`: compact Hausdorff space with covering dimension `dim(X)=0`;  
`E`: W*-bundle over `X`;  
`M`: fixed II_1 factor;  
`E_x`: fibre of `E` at `x`;  
hypothesis: `E_x ≅ M` for every `x ∈ X`.  
conclusion_to_prove: For every `x ∈ X`, there is an open neighbourhood `U` of `x` such that the restricted W*-bundle `E|_U` is isomorphic, as a W*-bundle over `U`, to the trivial bundle with fibre `M`.

S5: Assemble the local argument for arbitrary `x ∈ X`, verify all bundle-isomorphism properties, and conclude that `E` is locally trivial.
