2. Subproof or Failure

Fix \(x\in X\), and fix a \(*\)-isomorphism \(\theta:M\to E_x\). Let \(F=\{m_1,\dots,m_n\}\subset M\) be finite.

For each \(m_i\in F\), the fibre \(E_x\) is, by definition of the W\(^*\)-bundle fibre, the quotient of \(E\) by the fibre ideal at \(x\), with quotient map
\[
\pi_x:E\to E_x,\qquad a\mapsto a_x.
\]
This quotient map is surjective. Since \(\theta(m_i)\in E_x\), choose \(a_i\in E\) such that
\[
(a_i)_x=\pi_x(a_i)=\theta(m_i).
\]
Thus every finite subset of \(M\) has chosen local section representatives near \(x\).

Now consider a finite family of fibrewise estimates at \(x\) involving the chosen sections. Concretely, let
\[
b_1,\dots,b_r\in E
\]
be finitely many elements obtained from the \(a_i\)’s, scalars, adjoints, sums, products, and other already chosen sections by finitely many algebraic operations. Suppose the required estimates at \(x\) are strict inequalities of the form
\[
\| (b_j)_x \|_{2,x}<\varepsilon_j
\]
or
\[
|\tau_x((b_j)_x)|<\varepsilon_j,
\]
and similarly for finite Boolean combinations reducible to finitely many such strict inequalities.

In a W\(^*\)-bundle, for each \(b\in E\), the function
\[
y\mapsto \|b_y\|_{2,y}
\]
is continuous on \(X\), where \(\|b_y\|_{2,y}=\tau_y(b_y^*b_y)^{1/2}\). Also
\[
y\mapsto \tau_y(b_y)
\]
is continuous, since the bundle trace/conditional expectation gives a continuous \(C(X)\)-valued function. Therefore each strict inequality holding at \(x\) persists on some open neighbourhood of \(x\).

Indeed, if \(\|(b_j)_x\|_{2,x}<\varepsilon_j\), choose \(\delta_j>0\) with
\[
\|(b_j)_x\|_{2,x}+\delta_j<\varepsilon_j.
\]
By continuity, there is an open neighbourhood \(U_j\) of \(x\) such that
\[
\|(b_j)_y\|_{2,y}<\varepsilon_j
\]
for all \(y\in U_j\). The trace estimates are identical, using continuity of \(y\mapsto \tau_y((b_j)_y)\). Intersecting over the finitely many estimates gives an open neighbourhood
\[
U=\bigcap_j U_j
\]
on which all estimates hold uniformly.

By SC1, \(X\) has a clopen basis. Hence there is a clopen neighbourhood \(V\) of \(x\) with
\[
x\in V\subseteq U.
\]
Restricting the sections \(a_i\) to \(V\), the fibre identities at \(x\) remain true, and all prescribed finite strict estimates hold for every \(y\in V\).

Thus finite subsets of \(M\) can be represented by sections near \(x\), and fibrewise strict estimates at \(x\) persist uniformly after shrinking to a clopen neighbourhood.
