1. Assignment restatement

S-ID: S3

assigned subclaim(s): construct finite-stage coherent approximate intertwining data between a local restriction \(E|_U\) and the trivial \(M\)-bundle over \(U\).

what must be proved: for a fixed \(x\in X\), a fibre isomorphism \(\alpha:E_x\cong M\), and any finite amount of requested test data, after shrinking to a clopen neighbourhood \(U\ni x\), one can arrange finite back-and-forth identifications between \(E|_U\) and the trivial bundle \(T_U:=C_\sigma(U,M)\) which are coherent from stage to stage and satisfy the requested finite algebraic, trace, \(2\)-norm, inverse, and central-linearity estimates.

declared prerequisite subclaims: SC1 in the standard form “zero-dimensional compact Hausdorff spaces have clopen neighbourhood bases.” SC2/SC3 only in the finite local form proved/used below: fibre-level finite identifications can be lifted to sections and finite \(2\)-norm/trace defects that vanish at \(x\) become uniformly small after shrinking to a clopen neighbourhood.

2. Subproof or failure

Fix \(x\in X\). Since \(E_x\cong M\), choose a trace-preserving \(*\)-isomorphism
\[
\alpha:E_x\to M.
\]
Let \(V\) be a clopen neighbourhood of \(x\). Let \(N<\infty\), let \(F_n\subset (E|_V)_1\) and \(G_n\subset (T_V)_1\) be finite sets for \(0\le n\le N\), and let \(\varepsilon_n>0\) be prescribed tolerances, together with finitely many requested \(*\)-polynomial, trace, \(2\)-norm, and \(C(V)\)-central-linearity tests at each stage.

Write \(q_x:E|_V\to E_x\) for the fibre quotient map. For \(a\in E|_V\), define
\[
\Theta(a):=\overline{\alpha(q_x(a))}\in T_V,
\]
the constant section with value \(\alpha(a_x)\).

For each \(b\) among the finitely many trivial-bundle sections that will appear in the construction, choose a section \(\widetilde b\in E|_V\) satisfying
\[
q_x(\widetilde b)=\alpha^{-1}(b(x)).
\]
This is possible because the quotient map onto the fibre is surjective. Choose these lifts coherently: whenever \(b=\Theta(a)\) for a previously chosen \(a\), take \(\widetilde b=a\). Extend linearly on the finite-dimensional spans, choosing self-adjoint lifts for self-adjoint basis elements and \(1\) as the lift of \(1\). Denote this finite partial reverse map by
\[
\Psi(b):=\widetilde b.
\]

Now define finite sets recursively. Start with finite-dimensional unital self-adjoint spans \(P_0\supset F_0\) in \(E|_V\) and \(Q_0\supset G_0\) in \(T_V\). Having defined \(P_n,Q_n\), enlarge finitely so that
\[
\Theta(P_n)\subset Q_{n+1},\qquad \Psi(Q_n)\subset P_{n+1},
\]
and include the finitely many elements needed to evaluate the requested tests at stage \(n+1\). Because \(N\) is finite, this produces only finitely many sections.

For every requested defect, form the corresponding scalar function on \(V\). Examples are:
\[
y\mapsto \|\Psi\Theta(a)_y-a_y\|_{2,y},
\]
\[
y\mapsto \|\Theta\Psi(b)(y)-b(y)\|_{2,M},
\]
\[
y\mapsto \left|\|p(a_1,\dots,a_k)_y\|_{2,y}
-\|p(\Theta a_1,\dots,\Theta a_k)(y)\|_{2,M}\right|,
\]
and the analogous trace defects and central-linearity defects. These functions are continuous by the defining continuity of the \(C(X)\)-valued trace/strict \(2\)-norm structure of a W*-bundle and by continuity of sections in the trivial bundle.

Each defect vanishes at \(x\). For instance,
\[
q_x(\Psi\Theta(a))=\alpha^{-1}(\alpha(q_x(a)))=q_x(a),
\]
so the first defect is \(0\) at \(x\). Similarly,
\[
\Theta\Psi(b)(x)=\alpha(q_x(\Psi b))=\alpha(\alpha^{-1}(b(x)))=b(x),
\]
and polynomial and trace tests agree at \(x\) because \(\alpha\) is a trace-preserving \(*\)-isomorphism.

There are only finitely many defects. Hence there is an open neighbourhood \(W\subset V\) of \(x\) on which every stage-\(n\) defect is \(<\varepsilon_n\). By zero-dimensionality, choose a clopen neighbourhood \(U\) with
\[
x\in U\subset W.
\]
Restrict all sections and finite-dimensional spans to \(U\). The maps \(\Theta_n:=\Theta|_{P_n|_U}\) and \(\Psi_n:=\Psi|_{Q_n|_U}\) are coherent because they are restrictions of the single fibre-defined maps \(\Theta,\Psi\). The uniform estimates over \(U\) hold by the choice of \(U\). Thus the restricted data form the required finite-stage coherent approximate intertwining system between \(E|_U\) and \(T_U\).
