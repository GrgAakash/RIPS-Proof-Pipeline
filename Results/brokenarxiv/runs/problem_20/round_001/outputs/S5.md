2. Subproof or failure

Let \(x \in X\) be arbitrary. By the declared local exactness prerequisite SC5, there exists a neighbourhood \(U \subseteq X\) of \(x\) and an exact W*-bundle isomorphism
\[
\Phi_U : E|_U \longrightarrow C_\sigma(U,\mathcal M),
\]
where \(\mathcal M\) is the fixed II\(_1\) factor to which every fibre of \(E\) is isomorphic.

We verify that this is precisely a local trivialization. The restricted object \(E|_U\) is the W*-bundle obtained by restricting the base algebra \(C(X)\) to \(C(U)\), or equivalently by considering the bundle over \(U\) whose fibre at \(y \in U\) is \(E_y\). The object \(C_\sigma(U,\mathcal M)\) is the trivial W*-bundle over \(U\) with constant fibre \(\mathcal M\).

Since \(\Phi_U\) is a W*-bundle isomorphism over \(U\), it is a unital \(*\)-isomorphism preserving the \(C(U)\)-module structure and the bundle trace/conditional expectation structure. Hence for each \(y \in U\), \(\Phi_U\) descends to a normal \(*\)-isomorphism on fibres
\[
(\Phi_U)_y : E_y \longrightarrow \mathcal M.
\]
Thus \(\Phi_U\) identifies \(E|_U\) with the constant bundle over \(U\) with fibre \(\mathcal M\), preserving all W*-bundle structure.

Because \(x \in X\) was arbitrary, every point of \(X\) has an open neighbourhood \(U\) on which \(E\) is isomorphic, as a W*-bundle over \(U\), to the trivial W*-bundle \(C_\sigma(U,\mathcal M)\). Therefore \(E\) is locally trivial.
