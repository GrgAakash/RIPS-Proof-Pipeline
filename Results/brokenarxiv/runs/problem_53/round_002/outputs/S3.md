S3 asks for a proof of the local PDE regularity criterion: in controlled graph coordinates near two approaching free-boundary sheets, bounded curvature and controlled local graph geometry should give uniform local parabolic estimates for one-phase viscous free-boundary Navier-Stokes with surface tension, yielding

## 2 Subproof Or Failure

I cannot genuinely prove this criterion from the allowed packet and standard background alone.

The obstruction is that bounded principal curvature plus local graph representability is not enough, by itself, to derive the asserted uniform parabolic estimates up to \(T_*\). After flattening a moving free boundary, the one-phase free-boundary Navier-Stokes system becomes a quasilinear parabolic system with coefficients depending on the graph parametrization and boundary conditions involving the mean curvature. To get uniform Schauder or Sobolev-parabolic estimates strong enough to imply an \(L^1_t L^\infty_x\) bound on \(\nabla u\), one needs uniform control of more than \(\sup |\kappa_i|\).

In particular, standard local parabolic estimates require quantitative bounds on the coordinate charts and coefficients, typically including Hölder or Sobolev control of the boundary graph beyond mere pointwise curvature bounds, control of time regularity, compatibility of the stress boundary condition, and nondegenerate parabolic constants. The dynamic boundary condition contains surface tension through curvature, so bounded curvature only gives bounded boundary forcing, not the derivative-level control needed to close estimates for \(\nabla u\) uniformly.

There is also a geometric degeneracy issue near two-sheet contact. If the fluid region between or around sheets develops a vanishing scale, local flattening neighborhoods or extension/collar constructions can degenerate. Assuming a uniform local chart radius, uniform constants, or a uniformly thick neighborhood tracking the sheets would be close to assuming the desired no-splash mechanism. The assignment specifically forbids using bounded curvature as a purely geometric no-splash statement, so that route would be circular.

Thus the key implication

\[
\text{bounded curvature + controlled local graph geometry}
\Longrightarrow
\int_{t_0}^{T_*}\|\nabla u\|_{L^\infty(\text{local})}\,dt<\infty
\]

remains an unresolved continuation theorem, not a consequence I can derive from the supplied data.
