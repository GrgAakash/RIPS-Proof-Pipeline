<!-- BEGIN_FINAL_PROOF -->

FINAL PROOF NOT COMPLETED.

1. Suppose, toward contradiction, that the bubble collapses at finite time \(T_*\) while
\[
K(t)=\sup_{\Gamma(t)}\max_i |\kappa_i(\cdot,t)|
\]
remains bounded as \(t\uparrow T_*\). Then there are distinct labels \(a\ne b\) such that
\[
|X(t,a)-X(t,b)|\to 0
\]
along \(t\uparrow T_*\), while for each fixed \(t<T_*\), embeddedness gives
\[
|X(t,a)-X(t,b)|>0.
\]
The kinematic condition gives
\[
\frac{d}{dt}X(t,a)=u(X(t,a),t),\qquad
\frac{d}{dt}X(t,b)=u(X(t,b),t).
\]

2. Under the assumed curvature bound \(K(t)\le M\), the two approaching sheets admit local graph descriptions with uniform \(C^{1,1}\) bounds on fixed-radius patches depending only on \(d\) and \(M\). This gives controlled local geometry of the two relevant sheets, but it does not by itself exclude approach or self-contact.

3. [KEY STEP] The missing step is the following local PDE estimate:
\[
\int_{t_0}^{T_*}\|\nabla u(s)\|_{L^\infty(U_s)}\,ds<\infty
\]
for a neighborhood \(U_s\) containing both material trajectories \(X(s,a)\) and \(X(s,b)\), obtained only from bounded curvature, local graph control, and the classical one-phase Navier-Stokes free-boundary equations with surface tension.

This estimate has not been supplied by S1-S5 and cannot be treated as genuinely standard background in the required form. Standard local parabolic estimates for the flattened moving-boundary problem require quantitative control beyond pointwise principal-curvature bounds, including higher spatial or Hölder/Sobolev control of the boundary parametrization, time regularity, pressure/stress control, compatibility data, and nondegenerate localization constants. Near possible two-sheet contact, uniform collars or neighborhoods may themselves degenerate. Thus this step is a genuine unresolved major subproof.

4. If the estimate in Step 3 were available, then setting
\[
Z(t)=X(t,a)-X(t,b),\qquad r(t)=|Z(t)|
\]
would give
\[
|Z'(t)|\le \|\nabla u(t)\|_{L^\infty(U_t)}\,|Z(t)|.
\]
Hence
\[
r(t)\ge r(t_0)\exp\left(-\int_{t_0}^{t}\|\nabla u(s)\|_{L^\infty(U_s)}\,ds\right).
\]
Since \(r(t_0)>0\), finite integrability would imply a positive lower bound for \(r(t)\) up to \(T_*\), contradicting collapse.

5. Therefore the intended contradiction proof is complete only conditional on the unresolved local PDE regularity criterion in Step 3. Without that criterion, the theorem is not proved from the supplied packet and allowed background.
