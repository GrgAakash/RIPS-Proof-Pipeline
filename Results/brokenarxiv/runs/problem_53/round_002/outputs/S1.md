2. Subproof or Failure

Assume, for contradiction against the target theorem, that a finite-time splash/self-intersection occurs at time \(T_*<\infty\) while the principal curvatures do not blow up. Thus there exist distinct boundary labels \(a\neq b\) such that
\[
|X(t,a)-X(t,b)|\to 0 \qquad \text{as } t\uparrow T_*,
\]
and there is a constant \(M<\infty\) such that, on the time interval under consideration near collapse,
\[
K(t)=\sup_{x\in \Gamma(t)}\max_i |\kappa_i(x,t)|\le M.
\]

By the definition of collapse/self-intersection supplied in the packet, for every \(t<T_*\), the free boundary \(\Gamma(t)\) is smooth and embedded. Since \(X(t,\cdot)\) is the boundary-label map parametrizing \(\Gamma(t)\), embeddedness implies injectivity of the parametrization on labels: for every \(t<T_*\),
\[
X(t,\alpha)=X(t,\beta) \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \alpha=\beta.
\]
In particular, for the distinct labels \(a\neq b\),
\[
X(t,a)\neq X(t,b) \qquad \text{for every } t<T_*.
\]

Fix any \(t_0\in [0,T_*)\). Since \(a\neq b\) and \(X(t_0,\cdot)\) is injective,
\[
X(t_0,a)\neq X(t_0,b).
\]
Therefore the initial separation at time \(t_0\),
\[
\delta_0:=|X(t_0,a)-X(t_0,b)|,
\]
is strictly positive:
\[
\delta_0>0.
\]

Finally, by the kinematic condition in the packet, each boundary label evolves by the fluid velocity:
\[
\frac{d}{dt}X(t,\alpha)=u(X(t,\alpha),t)
\]
for every label \(\alpha\) and every \(t<T_*\). Applying this to the selected labels gives
\[
\frac{d}{dt}X(t,a)=u(X(t,a),t),
\qquad
\frac{d}{dt}X(t,b)=u(X(t,b),t),
\]
on \([0,T_*)\). Thus the separation vector
\[
Y(t):=X(t,a)-X(t,b)
\]
is well-defined and nonzero for every \(t<T_*\), satisfies
\[
Y(t)\to 0 \quad \text{in norm as } t\uparrow T_*,
\]
and obeys the identity
\[
\frac{d}{dt}Y(t)
=
u(X(t,a),t)-u(X(t,b),t).
\]

This establishes the contradiction setup needed for later subclaims.
