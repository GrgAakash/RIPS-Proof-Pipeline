1. **Target Decomposition**

- `target_label`: Problem 53.
- `target_type`: necessary blow-up criterion for finite-time free-boundary self-intersection.
- `main_goal`: prove by contradiction that a finite-time self-intersection of the material free boundary cannot occur while the free-boundary curvature size remains bounded.
- `variables_and_parameters`: dimension `d >= 2`; smooth exterior fluid domain `Omega(t)=R^d \ B(t)`; compact free boundary `Gamma(t)=partial B(t)`; material parametrization `X(t,a)`; collapse time `T_*`; principal curvatures `kappa_i`; curvature size  
  `K(t)=sup_{x in Gamma(t)} max_i |kappa_i(x,t)|`.
- `conclusion_to_prove`:  
  If there exist distinct labels `a != b` such that `|X(t,a)-X(t,b)| -> 0` as `t upward T_*`, then
  \[
  \limsup_{t\uparrow T_*} K(t)=+\infty.
  \]
  Equivalently, `K(t)` cannot remain bounded on any terminal interval `[t0,T_*)`.
