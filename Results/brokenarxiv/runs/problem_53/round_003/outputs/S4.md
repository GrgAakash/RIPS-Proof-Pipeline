claim: SC5
proof: |
  Let a != b be two material boundary labels, and write
    x_a(t) = X(a,t),  x_b(t) = X(b,t).
  By the kinematic condition,
    d_t x_a(t) = u(x_a(t),t),  d_t x_b(t) = u(x_b(t),t).

  Assume the two trajectories remain in the local region where the spatial
  Lipschitz bound L(t) is valid and
    integral_0^T L(t) dt < infinity.

  Set r(t) = x_a(t) - x_b(t). Then
    r'(t) = u(x_a(t),t) - u(x_b(t),t).
  For every time with r(t) != 0,
    d/dt |r(t)|
      = (r(t)/|r(t)|) · (u(x_a(t),t) - u(x_b(t),t))
      >= -|u(x_a(t),t) - u(x_b(t),t)|
      >= -L(t)|r(t)|.

  Hence
    d/dt log |r(t)| >= -L(t).
  Integrating from 0 to t gives
    |X(a,t) - X(b,t)|
      >= |X(a,0) - X(b,0)| exp(-integral_0^t L(s) ds).

  Since a and b are distinct initial labels, the initial separation is positive.
  Since the local Lipschitz integral is finite, the exponential factor is also
  positive. Therefore the separation cannot vanish at any finite time covered by
  the estimate.

  If a first collision time tau <= T existed, then the same bound for t < tau
  and continuity of X would give
    |X(a,tau) - X(b,tau)|
      >= |X(a,0) - X(b,0)| exp(-integral_0^tau L(s) ds) > 0,
  contradicting collision. Thus finite local Lipschitz integral prevents two
  distinct material boundary labels from colliding.
