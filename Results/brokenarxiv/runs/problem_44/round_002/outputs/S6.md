2. Final proof
<!-- BEGIN_FINAL_PROOF -->
1. Let the uniform passport be \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) on \(n\) edges. By definition of passport partitions,
\[
n=ar=bs=ct.
\]

2. For a dessin with this passport, the numbers of black vertices, white vertices, faces, and edges are \(r,s,t,n\), respectively. The Euler characteristic formula gives
\[
r+s+t-n=2-2g.
\]
Since \(g\ge 2\), this implies
\[
n\left(\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac1c-1\right)=2-2g<0,
\]
so
\[
\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac1c<1.
\]

3. By the standard permutation model for connected dessins, it would suffice to construct permutations \(x,y,z\in S_n\) such that
\[
xyz=1,
\]
the subgroup \(\langle x,y,z\rangle\) is transitive, and the cycle types of \(x,y,z\) are exactly
\[
a^r,\qquad b^s,\qquad c^t.
\]
The dessin automorphism group is then the simultaneous centralizer
\[
C_{S_n}(x,y,z)=\{\tau\in S_n:\tau x=x\tau,\ \tau y=y\tau,\ \tau z=z\tau\}.
\]
Thus the target theorem would follow if one could make this centralizer trivial.

4. [KEY STEP] The missing required step is the following all-cases construction: for every admissible uniform hyperbolic datum \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) with \(g\ge2\), construct a transitive triple \((x,y,z)\) with exact cycle types \(a^r,b^s,c^t\), \(xyz=1\), and either trivial simultaneous centralizer or a rigid marker forcing trivial centralizer. The supplied S2 output explicitly leaves this construction unsolved, and S3 supplies only a conditional marker criterion depending on such a construction. No allowed supporting statement or internally verified auxiliary result supplies this existence theorem.

5. The conditional centralizer argument is valid: if a constructed transitive triple contains a distinguished edge \(e_0\) fixed by every simultaneous centralizer element, then every \(\tau\in C_{S_n}(x,y,z)\) is the identity. Indeed, for any edge \(e\), transitivity gives a word \(w=w(x,y,z)\) with \(e=w(e_0)\). Since \(\tau\) commutes with \(x,y,z\), it commutes with \(w\), so
\[
\tau(e)=\tau(w(e_0))=w(\tau(e_0))=w(e_0)=e.
\]
Thus \(\tau\) fixes every edge.

6. Equivalently, in the triangle-group formulation
\[
\Delta(a,b,c)=\langle X,Y,Z\mid X^a=Y^b=Z^c=XYZ=1\rangle,
\]
a transitive triple gives a coset action \(\Delta/H\), where \(H\) is a point stabilizer. Dessin automorphisms correspond to \(N_\Delta(H)/H\), so trivial automorphism group is equivalent to \(N_\Delta(H)=H\). This again depends on the missing construction from Step 4.

FINAL PROOF NOT COMPLETED. The exact blocking point is the absence of a permitted proof of the all-cases construction of a transitive exact-cycle permutation triple with trivial simultaneous centralizer, or with a rigid marker from which triviality follows.
