1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 44

target_type:
existence theorem for dessins with prescribed uniform passport and trivial automorphism group

main_goal:
Given any uniform passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) of genus \(g\ge 2\) on \(n\) edges, construct a connected dessin with that passport and no nontrivial automorphisms.

variables_and_parameters:
Positive integers \(a,b,c,r,s,t,n,g\), with
\[
n=ar=bs=ct,\qquad 2-2g=r+s+t-n,\qquad g\ge 2.
\]

conclusion_to_prove:
There exists a transitive permutation triple \((x,y,z)\in S_n^3\) with
\[
x^a=y^b=z^c=xyz=1,
\]
where \(x,y,z\) have cycle types \(a^r,b^s,c^t\), and whose simultaneous centralizer in \(S_n\) is trivial. Equivalently, in the coset action of
\[
\Delta(a,b,c)=\langle x,y,z\mid x^a=y^b=z^c=xyz=1\rangle,
\]
there is a point stabilizer \(H\) such that \(N_\Delta(H)=H\).

2. Available tools

tool:
triangle-group/coset-action formulation

source_status:
additional guidance item

exact_statement_or_fact:
A dessin with uniform passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) on \(n\) edges is equivalent to a transitive degree-\(n\) action of \(\Delta(a,b,c)\) in which the standard generators have all cycles of exact lengths \(a,b,c\). Trivial dessin automorphism group is equivalent to the point stabilizer \(H\) being self-normalizing.

intended_role_in_proof:
Translate the dessin problem into construction of a transitive permutation representation with prescribed cycle structure and self-normalizing stabilizer.

tool:
Euler characteristic / genus formula for dessins

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a dessin with \(n\) edges, \(r\) black vertices, \(s\) white vertices, and \(t\) faces,
\[
2-2g=r+s+t-n.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:
Convert \(g\ge 2\) into the hyperbolic inequality
\[
1/a+1/b+1/c<1
\]
and the numerical condition \(n-r-s-t=2g-2\ge 2\).

tool:
permutation-triple construction

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
For every admissible uniform passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) with \(g\ge 2\), one can explicitly construct permutations \(x,y\in S_n\) of cycle types \(a^r,b^s\) such that \(z=(xy)^{-1}\) has cycle type \(c^t\), and \(\langle x,y\rangle\) is transitive.

intended_role_in_proof:
Produce a connected dessin with the desired passport.

tool:
asymmetry marker construction

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
The above construction can be arranged so that the associated labeled incidence/coset structure contains a finite rigid marker: any permutation commuting with \(x,y,z\) must fix one chosen edge, then its neighboring forced cycles, and then all edges by connectivity.

intended_role_in_proof:
Prove the simultaneous centralizer of \(x,y,z\) is trivial, equivalently \(N_\Delta(H)=H\).

tool:
centralizer-normalizer equivalence

source_status:
standard background fact / additional guidance formulation

exact_statement_or_fact:
For the transitive action of a group \(G\) on \(G/H\), the automorphism group of the action is naturally \(N_G(H)/H\). In permutation-triple language, this is the simultaneous centralizer of the image of \(G\) in \(S_n\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Turn trivial centralizer of the constructed permutation triple into trivial dessin automorphism group.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC3/SC4

hardest_step_description:
The main difficulty is constructing, for every admissible uniform passport, a transitive permutation triple with exact cycle types \(a^r,b^s,c^t\) while also building in a rigid marker that destroys all automorphisms.

risk_if_wrong:
If the construction fails for small values of \(a,b,c\) or for low multiplicities \(r,s,t\), the theorem may only be proved generically rather than for every genus-at-least-2 uniform passport.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The final proof should give an explicit uniform construction with separate checks for boundary cases where one of \(r,s,t\) is small. It should not appeal to a vague “generic asymmetric patch.” The proof must verify cycle lengths, product relation, transitivity, and rigidity directly.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not cite any theorem saying every sufficiently large or every hyperbolic passport has an asymmetric dessin. The construction and self-normalizing property must be proved inside the current proof.

full_theorem_check:
The blueprint must cover every uniform passport of genus at least \(2\), not only large \(n\), pairwise coprime \((a,b,c)\), or cases with many repeated cycles.

source_check:
Only the target statement, the guidance formulation, standard dessin/permutation background, and internally proved subclaims are used.

hypothesis_check:
The genus condition is essential and should be used through \(n-r-s-t=2g-2\ge 2\). The construction must respect \(n=ar=bs=ct\).

notation_check:
Use \(x,y,z\) both for triangle-group generators and their permutation images only after explicitly saying this abuse is being made.

standard_background_check:
Allowed standard facts should be stated precisely: dessin-permutation correspondence, Euler characteristic, and \(N_G(H)/H\) for automorphisms of transitive coset actions.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Prove the numerical setup and equivalence. Starting from \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\), derive \(n=ar=bs=ct\), \(2-2g=r+s+t-n\), and \(1/a+1/b+1/c<1\). Then state precisely the dessin/permutation-triple/coset-action correspondence and the equivalence between trivial automorphism group and \(N_\Delta(H)=H\).

S2:
Construct the transitive permutation triple. Give an explicit construction of permutations \(x,y,z\in S_n\) with \(xyz=1\), exact cycle types \(a^r,b^s,c^t\), and \(\langle x,y\rangle\) transitive. Include all small or boundary cases required by \(g\ge2\).

S3:
Modify or specify the S2 construction so that it contains a rigid marker. The marker must be described purely in terms of the three cycle decompositions and must be unique under any simultaneous centralizer of \(x,y,z\).

S4:
Prove centralizer triviality. Show that any \(\tau\in S_n\) commuting with \(x,y,z\) fixes the rigid marker, then fixes one edge, and then fixes all edges by connectivity/transitivity.

S5:
Assemble the proof blueprint into the final implication. From the constructed triple, define the subgroup \(H\le \Delta(a,b,c)\) as a point stabilizer, prove \(N_\Delta(H)=H\), translate back to dessins, and conclude the target theorem.
