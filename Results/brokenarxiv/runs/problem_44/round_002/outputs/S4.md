3. result:
```yaml
solved: true
claim: >
  Let E be the n-edge set and let x,y,z in S_E satisfy xyz=1, with
  G=<x,y,z> transitive. Suppose the construction contains a rigid marker
  M, defined purely from the x-, y-, and z-cycle incidence data, and M
  contains a distinguished edge e0 such that every permutation preserving
  that marked configuration fixes e0. Then every tau in S_E commuting with
  x,y,z is the identity.
proof: >
  Let tau in S_E commute with x,y,z. Since tau x = x tau, tau sends each
  x-cycle to an x-cycle and preserves cyclic adjacency inside that cycle.
  The same argument applies to y and z. Hence tau preserves every incidence
  and local relation expressible from the three permutations x,y,z, including
  the rigid marker M.
  
  By rigidity and uniqueness of the marker, tau cannot move M to a distinct
  copy. Therefore tau fixes M setwise. By the defining property of the marker,
  its setwise stabilizer in the simultaneous centralizer fixes the
  distinguished edge e0, so tau(e0)=e0.
  
  Now use transitivity. For any edge e in E, there is a word w in x,y,z and
  their inverses such that e=w(e0), because G=<x,y,z> acts transitively on E.
  Since tau commutes with x,y,z, it also commutes with every such word w.
  Therefore
    tau(e)=tau(w(e0))=w(tau(e0))=w(e0)=e.
  Thus tau fixes every edge e in E, so tau is the identity permutation.
conclusion: >
  The simultaneous centralizer C_{S_n}(x,y,z) is trivial.
```
