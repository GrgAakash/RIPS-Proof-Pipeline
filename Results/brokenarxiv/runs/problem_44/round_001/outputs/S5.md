assigned subclaim(s):
Assemble the final argument from S1-S4, check small or boundary cases separately if needed, and conclude existence of a dessin with the prescribed uniform passport and trivial automorphism group.

what must be proved:
Given the prerequisite subclaims C1-C6, show that every uniform passport of genus at least 2 is realized by a connected dessin d’enfant with that passport and trivial automorphism group.

declared prerequisite subclaims:
C1: dessins with a given passport are equivalently represented by transitive permutation triples with the corresponding cycle types, and dessin automorphisms correspond to the common centralizer of the triple.

C2: if the uniform passport is \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) of genus at least \(2\), then
\[
\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac1c<1.
\]

C3: for every uniform hyperbolic passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\), meaning \(ar=bs=ct=n\) and \(1/a+1/b+1/c<1\), there is a connected permutation triple realizing the prescribed cycle types.

C4: the construction can be refined by inserting a finite asymmetric patch, preserving all cycle lengths \(a,b,c\), preserving connectedness, and making the patch occur exactly once.

C5: every automorphism of the refined object fixes the asymmetric patch pointwise.

C6: pointwise fixing of the patch propagates to all edges, so the common centralizer of the refined triple is trivial.

2. Subproof or failure

Let the given uniform passport be
\[
(a^r,b^s,c^t)
\]
on \(n\) edges, so \(ar=bs=ct=n\). Since the passport has genus at least \(2\), prerequisite C2 gives
\[
\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac1c<1.
\]
Thus the passport is hyperbolic in the sense required by C3.

By C3, there exists a connected permutation triple
\[
(\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_\infty)
\]
on the \(n\) edges whose cycle types are respectively \((a^r),(b^s),(c^t)\), with \(\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_\infty=1\), and whose generated group acts transitively on the \(n\) edges.

By C4, this realization may be chosen or refined so that it still has the same three cycle types \((a^r),(b^s),(c^t)\), remains connected, and contains a unique finite asymmetric patch. Let the resulting triple be
\[
(\tau_0,\tau_1,\tau_\infty).
\]
Then \((\tau_0,\tau_1,\tau_\infty)\) is still transitive and still realizes the given passport.

Let \(\gamma\) be an automorphism of the dessin represented by this refined triple. Equivalently, by C1, \(\gamma\) is a permutation of the edge set commuting with each of \(\tau_0,\tau_1,\tau_\infty\). By C5, \(\gamma\) fixes the asymmetric patch pointwise. By C6, pointwise fixing of that patch forces \(\gamma\) to fix every edge. Hence \(\gamma\) is the identity permutation.

Therefore the common centralizer of \(\tau_0,\tau_1,\tau_\infty\) is trivial. By C1, the corresponding connected dessin d’enfant has passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) and trivial automorphism group.

No additional small or boundary cases remain at the assembly stage: the genus-at-least-\(2\) hypothesis is used precisely through C2 to enter the hyperbolic range, and C3-C6 are declared to cover every uniform hyperbolic passport.
