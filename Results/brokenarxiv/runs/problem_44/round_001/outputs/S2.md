assigned subclaim(s):
C3: construct a connected permutation triple realizing an arbitrary uniform hyperbolic passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\).

what must be proved:
Given positive integers \(a,b,c,r,s,t,n\) with
\[
ar=bs=ct=n,\qquad 1/a+1/b+1/c<1,
\]
and with the genus determined by
\[
2-2g=r+s+t-n
\]
at least \(2\), construct permutations \(\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_\infty\in S_n\) such that

\[
\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_\infty=1,
\]
\(\sigma_0\) has cycle type \(a^r\), \(\sigma_1\) has cycle type \(b^s\), \(\sigma_\infty\) has cycle type \(c^t\), and the group
\[
\langle \sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_\infty\rangle
\]
acts transitively on the \(n\) letters.

declared prerequisite subclaims:
None from S3-S5. Conversion from such a transitive triple to a connected dessin is the S1 interface fact, not proved here.

2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED.

The missing obstacle is an explicit general construction of a transitive permutation action of the triangle presentation
\[
\Delta(a,b,c)=\langle x,y,z\mid x^a=y^b=z^c=xyz=1\rangle
\]
on \(n\) letters in which \(x,y,z\) act with all cycles of exact lengths \(a,b,c\), respectively.

Indeed, a solution to S2 is equivalent to such an action: from the desired triple, send \(x\mapsto\sigma_0\), \(y\mapsto\sigma_1\), \(z\mapsto\sigma_\infty\). Conversely, such an action supplies the desired triple. Transitivity of the action is exactly transitivity of the subgroup generated by the three permutations. The exact cycle-length condition is stronger than merely requiring the generator orders to divide \(a,b,c\).

The supplied packet does not provide a uniform block construction, a polygon-gluing construction, or a torsion-free finite-index action theorem for \(\Delta(a,b,c)\). I cannot derive the required construction from the allowed materials alone without inserting such a lemma.
