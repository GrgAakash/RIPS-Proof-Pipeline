2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED

The assigned subclaim cannot be proved from the supplied packet alone, because the actual asymmetric patch and propagation mechanism from S3 are not specified.

A conditional argument would be straightforward if S3 supplied the three prerequisite properties above. Namely, let \(D\) be the constructed dessin and let \(P\subset D\) be the patch. An automorphism of a dessin preserves black vertices, white vertices, face boundaries, incidence, and the cyclic order induced by the embedding. Since \(P\) occurs exactly once, any automorphism \(\phi\in\operatorname{Aut}(D)\) must send \(P\) to itself. Since \(P\) has trivial internal automorphism group, the restriction \(\phi|_P\) must be the identity on \(P\). Finally, by the stated propagation property, any automorphism fixing \(P\) pointwise fixes all remaining edges of \(D\). Therefore \(\phi\) fixes every edge, hence is the identity automorphism.

However, without the actual patch or a proved propagation rule, this only proves a conditional lemma, not the assigned S4 subclaim for the construction intended by S0.
