1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 44

target_type:
Existence theorem for dessins with prescribed uniform passport and trivial automorphism group.

main_goal:
Construct, for every uniform passport of genus at least 2, at least one transitive permutation triple realizing the passport whose common centralizer is trivial.

variables_and_parameters:
Let the uniform passport be
\[
(a^{r}, b^{s}, c^{t})
\]
on \(n\) edges, so \(ar=bs=ct=n\). Here \(a,b,c\) are the common black, white, and face degrees, and \(r,s,t\) are the numbers of black vertices, white vertices, and faces.

conclusion_to_prove:
There exists a connected dessin d’enfant with black degrees all \(a\), white degrees all \(b\), face degrees all \(c\), and with trivial automorphism group.

2. Available tools

tool:
Permutation-triple model of dessins

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
A connected dessin with \(n\) edges is equivalently encoded by a transitive triple \((\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_\infty)\in S_n^3\) satisfying
\[
\sigma_0\sigma_1\sigma_\infty=1.
\]
The cycle lengths of \(\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_\infty\) are respectively the black degrees, white degrees, and face degrees.

intended_role_in_proof:
Convert the target into a finite permutation construction problem.

tool:
Automorphisms as common centralizer

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
The automorphism group of the dessin represented by \((\sigma_0,\sigma_1,\sigma_\infty)\) is the centralizer in \(S_n\) of the subgroup generated by \(\sigma_0,\sigma_1\), equivalently the set of permutations commuting with all three elements of the triple.

intended_role_in_proof:
Reduce trivial automorphism group to proving the common centralizer is trivial.

tool:
Genus formula for dessins

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a dessin with \(n\) edges, \(r\) black vertices, \(s\) white vertices, and \(t\) faces,
\[
2-2g=r+s+t-n.
\]
For a uniform passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\), this becomes
\[
2-2g=n\left(\frac1a+\frac1b+\frac1c-1\right).
\]

intended_role_in_proof:
Extract numerical restrictions from the genus-at-least-2 hypothesis.

tool:
Elementary polygon/gluing construction of dessins

source_status:
standard background fact / must be stated precisely in final proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
A permutation triple of prescribed cycle types can be constructed by arranging labeled half-edges around black vertices and white vertices, then reading the induced face permutation. Conversely, specifying two permutations \(\sigma_0,\sigma_1\) with prescribed cycle types determines the face permutation \(\sigma_\infty=(\sigma_0\sigma_1)^{-1}\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Provide the construction mechanism for the desired dessin.

tool:
Asymmetric local defect principle

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
If a transitive permutation triple contains a uniquely identifiable finite local configuration, invariantly definable from the triple, then every automorphism fixes that configuration pointwise; if the rest of the labels are then forced by propagation through the connected graph, the common centralizer is trivial.

intended_role_in_proof:
Give a general method for proving trivial automorphism group.

tool:
Uniform block construction

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
For every admissible uniform passport of genus at least 2, one can build a connected dessin with that passport by assembling several identical regular blocks and then modifying finitely many connections to create a unique asymmetric defect while preserving all black, white, and face degrees.

intended_role_in_proof:
Main constructive existence step.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
C4

hardest_step_description:
The hardest point is modifying a uniform regular construction to insert a unique asymmetric patch while preserving every black degree, every white degree, and every face degree exactly.

risk_if_wrong:
If the patch changes even one face length or vertex degree, the constructed dessin no longer has the required passport. If the patch is not uniquely recognizable, nontrivial automorphisms may survive.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The final proof should give an explicit finite replacement move in permutation notation. It should list the affected cycles before and after the move, verify that their lengths remain \(a,b,c\), verify that the generated group remains transitive, and then identify an invariant feature occurring exactly once.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not assume the target theorem or any equivalent statement such as “every hyperbolic uniform passport has an asymmetric dessin.” The asymmetric construction must be explicit.

full_theorem_check:
The proof must cover all uniform passports of genus at least 2, including small values of \(a,b,c\) and small admissible \(n\). Any exceptional small cases must be handled explicitly.

source_check:
No external classification theorem or enumeration of dessins may be used. Only the permutation-triple model, Euler characteristic, and elementary permutation construction are allowed as standard background.

hypothesis_check:
The proof must use uniformity only as \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) with \(ar=bs=ct=n\), and genus at least 2 through
\[
2-2g=r+s+t-n.
\]

notation_check:
The proof should consistently use \(a,b,c\) for degrees and \(r,s,t\) for counts. Avoid confusing the number of edges \(n\) with the number of darts in alternative dessin conventions.

standard_background_check:
Any claimed construction of a permutation triple must be written explicitly enough that it is not relying on unstated map-existence results.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Prove the equivalence between dessins and transitive permutation triples for this setting, state the automorphism-centralizer correspondence, and derive the numerical consequence of genus at least 2 for a uniform passport.

S2:
Construct a connected permutation triple realizing an arbitrary uniform hyperbolic passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\). The construction must explicitly verify the cycle types and transitivity.

S3:
Refine S2’s construction by inserting a finite asymmetric patch. The patch must preserve all cycle lengths \(a,b,c\), preserve connectedness, and occur exactly once in the resulting dessin.

S4:
Prove that the asymmetric patch kills all automorphisms. Show first that every automorphism fixes the patch pointwise, then that the construction forces all remaining edges by propagation.

S5:
Assemble the final argument from S1-S4, check small or boundary cases separately if needed, and conclude existence of a dessin with the prescribed passport and trivial automorphism group.
