## 3. What Can Be Proved Locally

The following conditional lemma is the exact S4 mechanism that would prove trivial automorphism once S2/S3 supply the required construction data.

Let a dessin be encoded by a transitive permutation triple  
\[
(x,y,z)\in S_n^3,\qquad xyz=1,
\]
where cycles of \(x\) are black vertices, cycles of \(y\) are white vertices, and cycles of \(z\) are faces. An automorphism is a permutation \(\pi\in S_n\) such that
\[
\pi x=x\pi,\qquad \pi y=y\pi,\qquad \pi z=z\pi .
\]

Suppose the constructed dessin contains a rooted colored cyclic-incidence marker consisting of a distinguished oriented edge \(e_0\), together with a finite local incidence pattern around its adjacent black vertex, white vertex, and face, such that:

1. the marker occurs exactly once in the whole dessin;
2. every automorphism preserves black/white/face colors and cyclic orders;
3. the underlying rotation system is connected.

Then every automorphism fixes \(e_0\). Indeed, an automorphism must send the unique occurrence of the marker to another occurrence of the same marker, but there is only one, so the rooted oriented edge \(e_0\) is fixed.

Now if \(\pi(e)=e\), then because \(\pi\) commutes with \(x,y,z\),
\[
\pi(x^i e)=x^i\pi(e)=x^i e,
\]
and similarly
\[
\pi(y^j e)=y^j e,\qquad \pi(z^k e)=z^k e .
\]
Thus fixedness of one edge propagates to every edge lying in the same black vertex cycle, white vertex cycle, or face cycle.

Since the dessin is connected, every edge is reachable from \(e_0\) by a finite alternating path through black vertices, white vertices, and faces; equivalently, every edge lies in the orbit of \(e_0\) under the group generated by \(x,y,z\). Therefore every edge is fixed by \(\pi\). Hence \(\pi=1\), so the automorphism group is trivial.
