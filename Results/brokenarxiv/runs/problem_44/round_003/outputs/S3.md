## 4. Useful Conditional Lemma

If S2 constructs the labels as edges of a bipartite embedded graph whose underlying black-white incidence graph is connected, then `<x,y,z>` is transitive.

Proof: `x` cycles the edge labels around black vertices, and `y` cycles the edge labels around white vertices. Thus two edge labels incident to the same black vertex lie in the same `<x,y>`-orbit, and likewise for a white vertex. Along any path in the connected underlying bipartite graph, consecutive edges share a black or white vertex, so their labels lie in the same `<x,y>`-orbit. Hence all edge labels lie in one `<x,y>`-orbit. Since `<x,y> ≤ <x,y,z>`, the full group is transitive. Also `z=(xy)^{-1}`, so `<x,y,z>=<x,y>`.

Thus S3 can be discharged once S2 explicitly proves the underlying incidence graph of its assembled dessin is connected.
