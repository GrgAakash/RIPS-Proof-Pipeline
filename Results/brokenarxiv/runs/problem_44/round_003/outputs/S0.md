1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 44

target_type:
Existence theorem for dessins d'enfant with prescribed uniform passport and trivial automorphism group.

main_goal:
Given any uniform passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) on \(n\) edges with genus \(g\ge 2\), construct a connected dessin d'enfant realizing that passport and having trivial automorphism group.

variables_and_parameters:
Positive integers \(a,b,c,r,s,t,n,g\), with
\[
ar=bs=ct=n,\qquad r+s+t-n=2-2g,\qquad g\ge 2.
\]

conclusion_to_prove:
There exists a transitive permutation triple \((x,y,z)\in S_n^3\) such that \(xyz=1\), every cycle of \(x\) has length \(a\), every cycle of \(y\) has length \(b\), every cycle of \(z\) has length \(c\), and the resulting dessin has trivial automorphism group.

2. Available tools

tool:
Permutation-triple model of dessins

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
A connected dessin with \(n\) edges and passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) is equivalent to a transitive triple \((x,y,z)\in S_n^3\) with \(xyz=1\), where the cycle lengths of \(x,y,z\) are respectively the black degrees, white degrees, and face degrees.

intended_role_in_proof:
Translate the geometric problem into constructing a transitive permutation triple with prescribed uniform cycle types.

tool:
Euler characteristic / genus formula

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a dessin with \(r\) black vertices, \(s\) white vertices, \(t\) faces, and \(n\) edges,
\[
r+s+t-n=2-2g.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:
Verify that the constructed dessin has the prescribed genus and that the numerical hypotheses match the target passport.

tool:
Triangle-group formulation

source_status:
additional guidance item

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a uniform passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\), a dessin with that passport is equivalent to a transitive degree-\(n\) action of
\[
\Delta(a,b,c)=\langle x,y,z\mid x^a=y^b=z^c=xyz=1\rangle
\]
in which \(x,y,z\) have all cycles of exact lengths \(a,b,c\). Trivial automorphism group is equivalent to the point stabilizer \(H\) being self-normalizing.

intended_role_in_proof:
Provide a clean group-action formulation and a criterion for trivial automorphism group.

tool:
Automorphisms as centralizers

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a transitive permutation representation generated by \(\langle x,y,z\rangle\le S_n\), dessin automorphisms are exactly the permutations of the \(n\) edges commuting with \(x,y,z\). Thus the automorphism group is the centralizer \(C_{S_n}(\langle x,y,z\rangle)\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Reduce triviality of automorphisms to showing any edge-permutation commuting with the constructed rotations is the identity.

tool:
Direct block/gluing construction

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
For every admissible uniform passport of genus at least \(2\), one can assemble labeled \(a\)-, \(b\)-, and \(c\)-cycles into a connected rotation system with product relation \(xyz=1\), prescribed cycle types, and a unique rooted colored cyclic-incidence marker.

intended_role_in_proof:
This is the main construction. It must be proved by explicit combinatorial assembly, not cited.

tool:
Unique marker kills automorphisms

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
If a connected dessin contains an edge characterized uniquely by its finite colored cyclic-incidence neighborhood, and the construction propagates this uniqueness through the incidence graph, then every automorphism fixes that edge and then fixes all edges.

intended_role_in_proof:
Show the constructed dessin has trivial automorphism group.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC2/SC3

hardest_step_description:
The hardest part is giving a uniform all-cases construction of the permutation triple, especially when one or more of \(a,b,c\) is small. The construction must not rely on an undefined “asymmetric patch”; it must explicitly assemble the cycles, enforce \(xyz=1\), and remain connected.

risk_if_wrong:
If the construction fails in small cases, produces incorrect face cycles, or silently changes the passport, the theorem is not proved. If asymmetry is only asserted informally, the automorphism conclusion is also unsupported.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The final proof should present the construction algorithmically: label the \(n\) edges, define \(x\) and \(y\), define \(z=(xy)^{-1}\), then prove by direct tracing that the cycles of \(z\) are exactly the required \(c\)-cycles. It should isolate any small exceptional parameter patterns and handle them explicitly.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not cite any theorem asserting existence of asymmetric dessins for arbitrary passports, surface subgroups with prescribed branching, generic monodromy, or random permutation asymmetry. The construction and self-normalizing/trivial-centralizer argument must be internal.

full_theorem_check:
The proof must cover every uniform passport of genus at least \(2\), not merely sufficiently large passports or a generic family.

source_check:
Only the target statement, allowed standard dessin/permutation facts, and the two guidance items may be used. No web sources or external classifications are allowed.

hypothesis_check:
The argument must use the admissibility conditions \(ar=bs=ct=n\) and \(g\ge2\). Any case with \(a=1\), \(b=1\), or \(c=1\) must be checked rather than dismissed.

notation_check:
The same symbols \(a,b,c,r,s,t,n,g,x,y,z\) should be used consistently. The product convention \(xyz=1\) must match the face-cycle convention.

standard_background_check:
Standard facts may include permutation triples for dessins, transitivity as connectedness, Euler characteristic, and automorphisms as centralizers. Anything stronger must be proved.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Set up the permutation-triple and triangle-group formulation. Prove or state precisely the equivalence between connected dessins with passport \((a^r,b^s,c^t)\) and transitive triples \((x,y,z)\) with \(xyz=1\) and prescribed cycle types. Include the Euler genus formula.

S2:
Construct the candidate permutations. Give an explicit labeling and assembly method from \(r\) black \(a\)-cycles, \(s\) white \(b\)-cycles, and \(t\) face \(c\)-cycles. Verify \(xyz=1\) and the exact cycle types, especially for \(z\).

S3:
Prove connectedness of the constructed dessin. Equivalently, prove that the subgroup generated by \(x,y,z\) acts transitively on the \(n\) edge labels. Handle all small parameter cases forced by genus at least \(2\).

S4:
Prove triviality of automorphisms. Identify the unique rooted colored cyclic-incidence marker in the construction, prove every automorphism fixes it, then prove fixedness propagates through the connected rotation system until every edge is fixed.

S5:
Perform the global consistency checks: confirm that the construction realizes exactly \(r\) black vertices, \(s\) white vertices, \(t\) faces, \(n\) edges, and genus \(g\). Check that no step assumes a stronger theorem than the target.
