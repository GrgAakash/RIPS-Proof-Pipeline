**1. Assignment Restatement**

S2 is assigned to prove SC3: a dimension-free bound for the truncated sampled kernel operators
\[
T_R^{(d,k)}f(n)=\sum_{0<|m|\le R} c_d\frac{m_k}{|m|^{d+1}}f(n-m)
\]
on \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\), with constant depending only on \(p\), independent of \(d,k,R\).

**2. Subproof Or Failure**

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED.

SC3 is false as stated. Take \(R=1\) and \(f=\delta_0\). Since the only lattice points with \(|m|\le 1\) and \(m_k\ne 0\) are \(m=\pm e_k\),
\[
T_1 f(n)=c_d(f(n-e_k)-f(n+e_k)).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
T_1\delta_0(e_k)=c_d,\qquad T_1\delta_0(-e_k)=-c_d,
\]
and \(T_1\delta_0\) vanishes elsewhere. Hence
\[
\|T_1\delta_0\|_{\ell^p}=2^{1/p}c_d,\qquad \|\delta_0\|_{\ell^p}=1.
\]
Therefore
\[
\|T_1\|_{\ell^p\to\ell^p}\ge 2^{1/p}c_d.
\]

But
\[
c_d=\frac{\Gamma((d+1)/2)}{\pi^{(d+1)/2}}
\]
is unbounded in \(d\). Indeed,
\[
\frac{c_{d+2}}{c_d}
=\frac{\Gamma((d+3)/2)}{\pi^{(d+3)/2}}
\frac{\pi^{(d+1)/2}}{\Gamma((d+1)/2)}
=\frac{d+1}{2\pi},
\]
which grows without bound, so along each parity subsequence \(c_d\to\infty\).

Thus no constant \(C_p\) independent of \(d\) can satisfy SC3.
