## 1 Assignment Restatement

Prove SC4: assuming SC2 pointwise convergence \(T_R f(n)\to R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f(n)\) for finitely supported \(f\), and SC3 uniform truncated bounds
\[
\|T_R f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}\le C_p\|f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\]
with \(C_p\) independent of \(d,k,R\), show
\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)} f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\le C_p\|f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}.
\]

## 2 Subproof Or Failure

Let \(f\) be finitely supported on \(\mathbb Z^d\). For \(R>0\), write
\[
T_R f(n)=\sum_{0<|m|\le R} c_d\frac{m_k}{|m|^{d+1}}f(n-m).
\]

By SC2, for every \(n\in\mathbb Z^d\),
\[
T_R f(n)\to R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f(n)
\qquad \text{as } R\to\infty.
\]

Choose any sequence \(R_j\to\infty\). Then pointwise,
\[
|T_{R_j}f(n)|^p\to |R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f(n)|^p.
\]

Applying Fatou’s lemma on the counting measure space \(\mathbb Z^d\),
\[
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z^d}|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f(n)|^p
\le
\liminf_{j\to\infty}\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z^d}|T_{R_j}f(n)|^p.
\]

By SC3, for every \(j\),
\[
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z^d}|T_{R_j}f(n)|^p
=
\|T_{R_j}f\|_{\ell^p}^p
\le
C_p^p\|f\|_{\ell^p}^p.
\]

Therefore
\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f\|_{\ell^p}^p
\le C_p^p\|f\|_{\ell^p}^p.
\]

Taking \(p\)-th roots gives
\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\le
C_p\|f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}.
\]

Thus SC4 follows from SC2 and SC3 with the constant exactly \(C_p\).
