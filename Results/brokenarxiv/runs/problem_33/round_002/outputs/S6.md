\[
R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}\delta_0(n)=K_k(n).
\]
In particular,
\[
K_k(e_k)=c_d,\qquad K_k(-e_k)=-c_d,
\]
since \(|e_k|=1\). Therefore
\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}\delta_0\|_{\ell^p}
=\|K_k\|_{\ell^p}
\ge \bigl(|c_d|^p+|-c_d|^p\bigr)^{1/p}
=2^{1/p}c_d .
\]
Thus
\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}\|_{\ell^p\to \ell^p}\ge 2^{1/p}c_d .
\]
But
\[
c_d=\frac{\Gamma((d+1)/2)}{\pi^{(d+1)/2}}
\]
is unbounded in \(d\), since
\[
\frac{c_{d+2}}{c_d}=\frac{d+1}{2\pi},
\]
which tends to infinity along each parity subsequence. Hence no constant depending only on \(p\) can bound the operator norms uniformly over all \(d\ge2\).

Therefore the target theorem cannot be true with the stated normalization.
