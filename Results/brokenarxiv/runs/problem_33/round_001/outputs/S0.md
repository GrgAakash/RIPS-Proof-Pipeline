1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 33

target_type:
Dimension-free operator norm estimate for a discrete singular integral convolution operator.

main_goal:
Prove that the discrete convolution operator
\[
R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)} f(n)=\sum_{m\in \mathbb Z^d} K_k(m)f(n-m)
\]
is bounded on \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\) with norm controlled by a constant depending only on \(p\), not on \(d\) or \(k\).

variables_and_parameters:
\(d\ge 2\) integer; \(k\in\{1,\dots,d\}\); \(1<p<\infty\); \(f\in \ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\); kernel
\[
K_k(m)=c_d\frac{m_k}{|m|^{d+1}},\qquad m\ne 0,\quad K_k(0)=0,
\]
where
\[
c_d=\frac{\Gamma((d+1)/2)}{\pi^{(d+1)/2}}.
\]

conclusion_to_prove:
There exists \(C_p>0\), depending only on \(p\), such that
\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)} f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\le C_p \|f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\]
for all \(d\ge 2\), all \(k\), and all finitely supported \(f\), hence by density for all \(f\in\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\).

2. Available tools

tool:
Discrete convolution notation and \(\ell^p\) norms.

source_status:
provided definition / notation / assumption.

exact_statement_or_fact:
The operator is defined by convolution with \(K_k\) on \(\mathbb Z^d\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Used to reduce the theorem to proving a uniform bound for a lattice singular integral operator.

tool:
Oddness of the kernel.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
Since \(K_k(-m)=-K_k(m)\), the kernel has cancellation in symmetric sums.

intended_role_in_proof:
Needed to justify principal value truncations and control singular behavior near the origin.

tool:
Continuous Riesz transform kernel.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
The continuous \(k\)-th Riesz transform on \(\mathbb R^d\) has kernel
\[
\mathcal K_k(x)=c_d\frac{x_k}{|x|^{d+1}},
\]
interpreted in the principal value sense.

intended_role_in_proof:
Identifies the discrete kernel as the lattice restriction of the continuous Riesz kernel.

tool:
Dimension-free \(L^p(\mathbb R^d)\) boundedness of continuous Riesz transforms.

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For every \(1<p<\infty\), there exists \(A_p>0\), independent of \(d\) and \(k\), such that
\[
\|R_k g\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^d)}\le A_p\|g\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^d)}.
\]

intended_role_in_proof:
Provides the dimension-free analytic input to transfer to the lattice.

tool:
Calderon transference / discretization principle.

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof.

exact_statement_or_fact:
If a homogeneous Calderon-Zygmund convolution operator on \(\mathbb R^d\) has an \(L^p\) bound \(A_p\), then its lattice convolution operator obtained from the sampled kernel has an \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\) bound controlled by a constant depending only on \(p\) times \(A_p\), under the required cancellation and truncation assumptions.

intended_role_in_proof:
Main bridge from the continuous dimension-free estimate to the desired discrete dimension-free estimate.

tool:
Uniform truncated-kernel estimates.

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For symmetric truncations
\[
K_{k,N}(m)=K_k(m)\mathbf 1_{\{0<|m|\le N\}},
\]
the convolution operators \(T_N f=K_{k,N}*f\) satisfy
\[
\sup_N \|T_N f\|_{\ell^p}\le C_p\|f\|_{\ell^p}
\]
with \(C_p\) independent of \(d,k,N\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Avoids ambiguity from conditional convergence and permits passage to the full operator.

tool:
Principal value / limit passage in \(\ell^p\).

source_status:
must be proved inside the current proof.

exact_statement_or_fact:
The truncated operators \(T_N f\) converge for finitely supported \(f\) to the convolution defining \(R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f\), and the uniform bounds pass to the limit.

intended_role_in_proof:
Converts truncated estimates into the final boundedness statement.

tool:
Density of finitely supported functions in \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\).

source_status:
standard background fact.

exact_statement_or_fact:
For \(1\le p<\infty\), finitely supported functions are dense in \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Extends the estimate from finitely supported functions to all \(\ell^p\) functions.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC3 / SC4

hardest_step_description:
The delicate part is proving a dimension-free discretization or transference step from the continuous Riesz transform to the lattice convolution operator with the sampled kernel.

risk_if_wrong:
A naive comparison between the lattice sum and the continuous integral may introduce constants depending on \(d\), or may fail near the singularity at the origin.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
Use symmetric truncations, exploit odd cancellation, and formulate the transference argument so that the only analytic operator norm input is the dimension-free \(L^p\) bound for the continuous Riesz transform. Every approximation error must be controlled uniformly in \(d\), or shown to vanish before any dimension-dependent estimate is introduced.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not cite the target theorem, any equivalent discrete dimension-free Riesz bound, or any stronger discrete Calderon-Zygmund theorem unless it is proved inside the current proof.

full_theorem_check:
The proof must cover every \(d\ge2\), every coordinate \(k\), and every \(1<p<\infty\), with a constant depending only on \(p\).

source_check:
No web sources, prior writeups, answer keys, hidden lemmas, or later statements are available. The continuous dimension-free Riesz bound may be used only as standard background.

hypothesis_check:
The assumptions \(d\ge2\), \(k\in\{1,\dots,d\}\), and \(1<p<\infty\) must be retained throughout. Density fails for \(p=\infty\), but that endpoint is not included.

notation_check:
Distinguish the continuous kernel \(\mathcal K_k(x)\) on \(\mathbb R^d\) from the sampled discrete kernel \(K_k(m)\) on \(\mathbb Z^d\). Truncations should be symmetric.

standard_background_check:
Acceptable standard tools are density of finitely supported functions, basic convolution notation, odd-kernel cancellation, and the known dimension-free \(L^p\) boundedness of continuous Riesz transforms. The discrete transference argument should be proved, not merely asserted as the theorem.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Prove the basic kernel facts: homogeneity away from zero, oddness \(K_k(-m)=-K_k(m)\), cancellation over symmetric lattice truncations, and the legitimacy of using symmetric truncated sums.

S2:
State and prepare the continuous comparison theorem: the continuous Riesz transform kernel is \(c_d x_k/|x|^{d+1}\), and its \(L^p(\mathbb R^d)\) norm is bounded by a constant depending only on \(p\). Do not prove the deep continuous theorem unless required; treat it as standard background.

S3:
Build the discretization/transference approximation. Embed finitely supported \(f\) as a step function on \(\mathbb R^d\), apply continuous truncated Riesz transforms, average over unit cubes, and show that the resulting expression converges to the discrete truncated operator.

S4:
Use the continuous \(L^p\) bound and the embedding identities between \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\) and step functions in \(L^p(\mathbb R^d)\) to prove the uniform estimate for all truncated operators \(T_N\), with constants independent of \(d,k,N\).

S5:
Pass from truncated operators to the full discrete operator for finitely supported functions, then extend by density to all of \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\). Verify that the final constant depends only on \(p\).
