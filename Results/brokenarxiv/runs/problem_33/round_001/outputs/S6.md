\[
K_k(-m)=-K_k(m),
\]
and therefore cancels on every finite symmetric set \(E=-E\subset \mathbb Z^d\setminus\{0\}\). In particular,
\[
\sum_{0<|m|\le N}K_k(m)=0.
\]
Thus the symmetric truncations
\[
T_N f(n)=\sum_{0<|m|\le N}K_k(m)f(n-m)
\]
are finite sums and are well defined on finitely supported \(f\).

By S2, the continuous Riesz transform on \(\mathbb R^d\) with kernel
\[
K_k^{\mathrm{cont}}(x)=c_d\frac{x_k}{|x|^{d+1}}
\]
satisfies the dimension-free estimate
\[
\|R_k^{\mathrm{cont}}g\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^d)}
\le A_p\|g\|_{L^p(\mathbb R^d)}
\]
for all \(d\ge2\), all \(1\le k\le d\), and all \(g\in L^p(\mathbb R^d)\), where \(A_p\) depends only on \(p\).

However, the supplied S3/S4 material does not establish the necessary implication from this continuous estimate to the sampled discrete estimate
\[
\|T_N f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\le C_p\|f\|_{\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)}
\]
with \(C_p\) independent of \(d,k,N\). S3 gives only pointwise approximation of discrete truncations by certain averaged continuous operators, while S4 explicitly notes that the available averaging controls coefficients
\[
\int_Q\int_Q c_d\frac{(m+u-v)_k}{|m+u-v|^{d+1}}\,du\,dv
\]
rather than the sampled coefficients
\[
c_d\frac{m_k}{|m|^{d+1}}.
\]
Thus the central uniform truncated estimate remains unproved.

\[
\|R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}f\|_{\ell^p}
\le C_p\|f\|_{\ell^p}.
\]
Density of finitely supported functions in \(\ell^p(\mathbb Z^d)\) would then extend \(R_{\mathrm{dis}}^{(k)}\) uniquely to all of \(\ell^p\) with the same bound.

Because the dimension-free uniform truncated bound is neither proved by S1-S5 nor supplied as allowed background, the target theorem is not proved from the provided packet.
