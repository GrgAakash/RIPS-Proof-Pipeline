**Assignment restatement**

Prove, using only the continuous dimension-free `L^p(R^d)` Riesz transform bound and standard step-function embeddings `ell^p(Z^d) -> L^p(R^d)`, that every truncated discrete operator `T_N` associated to

```text
K_k(m) = c_d m_k / |m|^{d+1},   m != 0
```

satisfies

```text
||T_N f||_{ell^p(Z^d)} <= C_p ||f||_{ell^p(Z^d)}
```

with `C_p` independent of `d`, `k`, and `N`.

**Subproof or failure**

This subproblem cannot be proved from the stated inputs alone.

The standard step-function embedding sends `f in ell^p(Z^d)` to

```text
F(x) = sum_{n in Z^d} f(n) 1_{n+[0,1)^d}(x),
```

with the exact identity

```text
||F||_{L^p(R^d)} = ||f||_{ell^p(Z^d)}.
```

The continuous Riesz transform bound gives

```text
||R_k F||_{L^p(R^d)} <= C_p ||F||_{L^p(R^d)}
```

with `C_p` independent of `d` and `k`.

However, this does not imply the desired bound for the sampled-kernel discrete truncations

```text
T_N f(n) = sum_{0 < |m| <= N} c_d m_k / |m|^{d+1} f(n-m).
```

The obstruction is that the discrete coefficients `K_k(m)` are point samples of the continuous kernel, while step-function transference naturally produces averaged coefficients of the form

```text
A_k(m) =
int_{[0,1)^d} int_{[0,1)^d}
c_d (m+u-v)_k / |m+u-v|^{d+1} du dv,
```

or related cube-averaged variants. The continuous bound controls the operator with averaged coefficients, not the operator with sampled coefficients.

To pass from `A_k(m)` to `K_k(m)`, one needs an additional comparison estimate or Calderon-Zygmund discretization theorem, uniformly in `d`, such as

```text
sum_m |K_k(m) - A_k(m)| <= C
```

or a dimension-free multiplier comparison. Such a statement is not among the allowed supporting statements. In fact, proving such a comparison uniformly in dimension is precisely part of the hard discretization step, not a consequence of the embedding identity alone.

Therefore the assigned subproblem fails under the stated restrictions.
