2. Subproof or failure

Let
\[
c_d=\frac{\Gamma((d+1)/2)}{\pi^{(d+1)/2}}>0,
\qquad
K_k(m)=c_d\frac{m_k}{|m|^{d+1}}
\]
for \(m\in\mathbb Z^d\setminus\{0\}\), with \(K_k(0)=0\).

First, the kernel is homogeneous of degree \(-d\) away from zero in the following precise sense. If \(m\ne0\) and \(t>0\) is such that \(tm\in\mathbb Z^d\), then
\[
K_k(tm)
=c_d\frac{tm_k}{|tm|^{d+1}}
=c_d\frac{tm_k}{t^{d+1}|m|^{d+1}}
=t^{-d}K_k(m).
\]
Equivalently, the function \(x\mapsto c_d x_k/|x|^{d+1}\) on \(\mathbb R^d\setminus\{0\}\) is homogeneous of degree \(-d\).

Second, \(K_k\) is odd. For \(m\ne0\),
\[
K_k(-m)
=c_d\frac{(-m)_k}{|-m|^{d+1}}
=-c_d\frac{m_k}{|m|^{d+1}}
=-K_k(m).
\]
Also \(K_k(0)=0=-K_k(0)\), so oddness holds on all of \(\mathbb Z^d\).

Now let \(E\subset \mathbb Z^d\setminus\{0\}\) be finite and symmetric, meaning \(E=-E\). Then
\[
\sum_{m\in E}K_k(m)=0.
\]
Indeed, the involution \(m\mapsto -m\) preserves \(E\), has no fixed point in \(E\) because \(0\notin E\), and pairs each term \(K_k(m)\) with \(K_k(-m)=-K_k(m)\).

In particular, for every \(N>0\), the Euclidean lattice truncation
\[
E_N=\{m\in\mathbb Z^d:0<|m|\le N\}
\]
is finite and symmetric, hence
\[
\sum_{0<|m|\le N}K_k(m)=0.
\]
The same conclusion holds for any truncation set depending only on \(|m|\), such as \(0<|m|<N\), because such sets are invariant under \(m\mapsto -m\).

Finally, symmetric truncated sums are legitimate. For any function \(f:\mathbb Z^d\to\mathbb C\), any \(n\in\mathbb Z^d\), and any finite symmetric \(E\subset\mathbb Z^d\setminus\{0\}\), the truncated expression
\[
T_E f(n)=\sum_{m\in E}K_k(m)f(n-m)
\]
is a finite sum and is therefore unambiguously defined. Using the pairing \(m\leftrightarrow -m\), one also has the equivalent cancellation form
\[
T_E f(n)
=\frac12\sum_{m\in E}K_k(m)\bigl(f(n-m)-f(n+m)\bigr).
\]
Indeed,
\[
\sum_{m\in E}K_k(m)f(n+m)
=\sum_{m\in E}K_k(-m)f(n-m)
=-\sum_{m\in E}K_k(m)f(n-m),
\]
where the change of variables uses \(E=-E\). Thus the displayed identity follows. Therefore symmetric principal-value-type truncations such as
\[
T_N f(n)=\sum_{0<|m|\le N}K_k(m)f(n-m)
\]
are finite, well-defined, cancellation-compatible sums.
