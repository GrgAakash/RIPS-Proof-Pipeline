Problem 6:
For every integer $k \ge 3$, there exists a countable group $G$ generated by a finite symmetric set $S$, a generating probability measure $\mu$ on $G$ (meaning the support of $\mu$ generates $G$ as a semigroup), and a $\mu$-stationary random subgroup $\nu$ of $G$ (a Borel probability measure on the Chabauty space of subgroups $\text{Sub}*G$ satisfying $\\nu = \\sum*{g \in G} \mu(g) (g.\nu)$ under the conjugation action $g.H = gHg^{-1}$), such that for $\nu$-almost every subgroup $H$, the associated Schreier graph with respect to $S$ (having vertex set $H \backslash G$ and edges $Hg \to Hgs$ for $s \in S$) has exactly $k$ ends.
