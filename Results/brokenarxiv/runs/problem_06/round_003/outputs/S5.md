1. Assignment restatement

For fixed `k >= 3`, assume the earlier construction has produced a probability law `P_k` on connected rooted Schreier diagrams over the finite alphabet `S_k` for

`G = *_{s in S_k} C_2 = < S_k | s^2 = e for all s in S_k >`

and assume `P_k` is stationary for the root-moving kernel with probabilities `mu(s) > 0` on every `s in S_k`. Also assume `P_k`-almost every underlying graph has exactly `k` ends.

The assigned task is to prove only the stabilizer-pushforward and assembly step: define `nu = Stab_* P_k`, prove the stabilizer map is Borel and equivariant, prove `nu` is `mu`-stationary on `Sub(G)`, prove `mu` is generating, and prove the canonical Schreier graph of `nu`-almost every subgroup has exactly `k` ends.

2. Subproof or failure

Let `Omega` be the standard Borel space of connected rooted Schreier diagrams over `G` with root `o`. For `x = (Gamma,o) in Omega`, the labelled edges define a transitive left action of `G` on the vertices of `Gamma`: each generator `s` acts by moving along the unique `s`-edge, and the relation `s^2=e` holds because the `s`-edge operation is an involution.

Define

`Theta(x) = Stab(x) = { g in G : g o = o }`.

This is a subgroup of `G`, since it is the stabilizer of a point under a group action.

Borelness. Give `Sub(G)` the usual Chabauty/product Borel structure as a subspace of `{0,1}^G`. For each `g in G`, choose once and for all a word `w_g` in the generators representing `g`. The coordinate event

`{ x in Omega : g in Theta(x) }`

is exactly the event that following the finite labelled path `w_g` from the root returns to the root. This is determined by the finite rooted labelled ball of radius `|w_g|`, hence is a cylinder event and is Borel. Since the coordinate maps generate the Borel structure on `{0,1}^G`, `Theta: Omega -> Sub(G)` is Borel.

Equivariance. Let `G` act on `Omega` by rerooting: `a . (Gamma,o) = (Gamma, a o)`. Let `G` act on `Sub(G)` by conjugation: `a . H = a H a^{-1}`. Then for every `a,h in G`,

`h in Theta(a . x)`
iff `h(a o)=a o`
iff `a^{-1} h a o=o`
iff `a^{-1} h a in Theta(x)`
iff `h in a Theta(x) a^{-1}`.

Thus

`Theta(a . x) = a Theta(x) a^{-1}`,

so `Theta` is equivariant.

Stationarity transfer. Let

`nu = Theta_* P_k`.

Since `P_k` is stationary for the root-moving kernel,

`P_k = sum_{s in S_k} mu(s) s_* P_k`.

Using equivariance,

`sum_s mu(s) s_* nu
= sum_s mu(s) s_* Theta_* P_k
= sum_s mu(s) Theta_* s_* P_k
= Theta_* (sum_s mu(s) s_* P_k)
= Theta_* P_k
= nu`.

Therefore `nu` is a `mu`-stationary random subgroup of `G`.

Generation. The set `S_k` is finite and symmetric because every generator is an involution, so `s^{-1}=s`. Since `mu(s)>0` for every `s in S_k`, the semigroup generated by `supp(mu)` contains every finite word in `S_k`, hence is all of `G`. Thus `mu` is generating.

Ends of the resulting Schreier graphs. For `x=(Gamma,o)` and `H=Theta(x)`, define

`Phi_x: G/H -> V(Gamma)` by `Phi_x(gH)=g o`.

This is well-defined because if `gH=g'H`, then `g'=gh` for some `h in H`, so `g'o=g h o=g o`. It is injective by the reverse implication, surjective because `Gamma` is connected and generated by the labelled `S_k`-moves, and label-preserving because the `s`-edge from `gH` goes to `sgH`, which maps to `s(g o)`. Hence the canonical Schreier graph `Sch(G/H,S_k)` is rooted label-isomorphic to `Gamma`.

Graph ends are preserved under graph isomorphism. Since `P_k`-almost every `Gamma` has exactly `k` ends, it follows that for `nu`-almost every subgroup `H`, the Schreier graph `Sch(G/H,S_k)` has exactly `k` ends.

Thus, conditional on the earlier construction of such a stationary rooted Schreier-diagram law `P_k`, the final theorem follows with `G=*_{s in S_k} C_2`, finite symmetric generating set `S_k`, generating measure `mu`, and stationary random subgroup `nu=Stab_*P_k`.
