1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 6

target_type:
Existence theorem.

main_goal:
For each integer \(k\ge 3\), construct a finitely generated countable group \(G\), finite symmetric generating set \(S\), generating probability measure \(\mu\), and \(\mu\)-stationary random subgroup \(\nu\) such that \(\nu\)-a.e. Schreier graph \(H\backslash G\) with labels from \(S\) has exactly \(k\) ends.

variables_and_parameters:
\(k\in\mathbb Z\), \(k\ge 3\). Constructed objects may depend on \(k\): \(G_k,S_k,\mu_k,\nu_k\).

conclusion_to_prove:
There is a Borel probability measure \(\nu_k\) on \(\operatorname{Sub}(G_k)\) satisfying
\[
\nu_k=\sum_{g\in G_k}\mu_k(g)\,g_*\nu_k
\]
under conjugation, with \(\operatorname{supp}(\mu_k)\) generating \(G_k\) as a semigroup, and for \(\nu_k\)-a.e. \(H\), the Schreier graph \(\operatorname{Sch}(H\backslash G_k,S_k)\) has exactly \(k\) ends.

tool:
Ends of locally finite graphs

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
A connected locally finite graph has exactly \(k\) ends if, after removing some finite vertex set, exactly \(k\) infinite connected components remain, and no finite removal can produce more than \(k\) infinite components.

intended_role_in_proof:
Used to prove the constructed Schreier graphs have exactly \(k\) ends.

tool:
Free-group Schreier correspondence

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a finite symmetric alphabet \(S\), every connected rooted regular \(S\)-labeled Schreier diagram determines a subgroup \(H\le F(S)\), namely the stabilizer of the root in the induced action of \(F(S)\) on vertices; conversely the Schreier graph of \(H\le F(S)\) recovers that rooted diagram.

intended_role_in_proof:
Convert a stationary random rooted Schreier diagram into a stationary random subgroup of a finitely generated countable group.

tool:
Stationarity transfers through equivariant maps

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
If \(X\) is a \(G\)-space, \(\rho\) is \(\mu\)-stationary on \(X\), and \(\Phi:X\to Y\) is Borel and \(G\)-equivariant, then \(\Phi_*\rho\) is \(\mu\)-stationary on \(Y\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Push stationarity from rooted diagrams to \(\operatorname{Sub}(G)\).

tool:
Construction of a \(k\)-ended stationary random Schreier diagram

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
For every \(k\ge 3\), construct a finite symmetric label set \(S_k\), a probability measure \(\rho_k\) on rooted connected regular \(S_k\)-labeled graphs, and a probability measure \(\mu_k\) on \(F(S_k)\), with \(\operatorname{supp}(\mu_k)\) generating \(F(S_k)\) as a semigroup, such that \(\rho_k\) is stationary under rerooting by \(\mu_k\), and \(\rho_k\)-a.e. underlying graph has exactly \(k\) ends.

intended_role_in_proof:
This is the main constructive engine; once proved, the theorem follows by the Schreier correspondence.

tool:
Finite labeling and loop padding do not change ends

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
Adding or changing finitely many labels, adding loops, or replacing an unlabeled bounded-degree graph by a bounded-degree regular labeled Schreier diagram on the same coarse graph does not change the number of ends, provided no new infinite connections are introduced between different ends.

intended_role_in_proof:
Ensures the labeled regular Schreier diagrams used in the construction still have exactly \(k\) ends.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC3

hardest_step_description:
The delicate part is constructing the stationary law \(\rho_k\) on rooted Schreier diagrams without accidentally producing an invariant/unimodular law, which would obstruct finite end number \(k>2\). The final proof must give explicit transition balances for rerooting by \(\mu_k\), not just appeal to compactness.

risk_if_wrong:
If the stationarity equations fail, the pushforward measure \(\nu_k\) will not be a stationary random subgroup. If the construction secretly gives an invariant random rooted graph, the claimed finite number of ends \(k\ge 3\) is likely impossible.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
Write the rooted-diagram state space and \(\mu_k\)-rerooting transitions explicitly. Verify the stationarity identity on cylinder events or on a generating algebra of events, then extend by standard measure arguments. Separately verify that every generator and its inverse lie in the semigroup generated by \(\operatorname{supp}(\mu_k)\).

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
Do not assume the target theorem or any pre-existing stationary subgroup construction. The stationary random Schreier diagram must be built and checked inside the proof.

full_theorem_check:
The construction must work uniformly for every integer \(k\ge 3\), with \(G_k\) countable and finitely generated, \(S_k\) finite symmetric, \(\mu_k\) generating, and \(\nu_k\) Borel probability.

source_check:
No allowed supporting statements are available. All non-definition facts must be either genuinely standard background, explicitly stated, or proved inside the current proof.

hypothesis_check:
The only input hypothesis is \(k\ge 3\). No amenability, invariance, transitivity, or finite-index assumption may be inserted.

notation_check:
Use right cosets \(H\backslash G\). Check carefully whether rerooting by \(g\) corresponds to conjugating \(H\) by \(g\) or \(g^{-1}\), and choose \(\mu_k\) or its reflected measure consistently.

standard_background_check:
Allowed standard background should be limited to basic graph ends, free groups and Schreier diagrams, Borel pushforward of stationary measures, and elementary measure extension from cylinder sets.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Construct the unlabelled \(k\)-ended model graph \(C_k\). Prove it is connected, locally finite, and has exactly \(k\) ends using finite-separator arguments.

S2:
Add a finite symmetric labeling to the \(C_k\)-type graphs to obtain regular rooted Schreier diagrams. Prove the labeling defines an action of a finitely generated free group and does not change the number of ends.

S3:
Construct the stationary probability law \(\rho_k\) on rooted labeled diagrams and the generating probability measure \(\mu_k\). Verify stationarity explicitly on cylinder events and verify semigroup generation.

S4:
Prove the Schreier-diagram-to-subgroup map into \(\operatorname{Sub}(F(S_k))\) is Borel and equivariant with conjugation, including the right-coset convention.

S5:
Assemble \(G_k=F(S_k)\), \(S_k\), \(\mu_k\), and \(\nu_k\). Prove \(\nu_k\) is \(\mu_k\)-stationary and that \(\nu_k\)-almost every Schreier graph has exactly \(k\) ends.
