1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Problem 6

target_type:
Existence theorem for stationary random subgroups with prescribed finite number of Schreier ends.

main_goal:
For each integer \(k \ge 3\), construct one explicit finitely generated countable group action model whose stabilizer law is stationary and whose Schreier graphs have exactly \(k\) ends almost surely.

variables_and_parameters:
\(k \in \mathbb Z\), \(k \ge 3\). Constructed objects: a countable group \(G\), a finite symmetric generating set \(S\), a generating probability measure \(\mu\) on \(G\), and a Borel probability measure \(\nu\) on \(\operatorname{Sub}(G)\).

conclusion_to_prove:
\(\nu\) is \(\mu\)-stationary for conjugation, and for \(\nu\)-almost every subgroup \(H \le G\), the Schreier graph on \(H\backslash G\) with labels in \(S\) has exactly \(k\) ends.

2. Available tools

tool:
Definitions from the target statement

source_status:
provided definition / notation / assumption

exact_statement_or_fact:
A \(\mu\)-stationary random subgroup is a Borel probability \(\nu\) on \(\operatorname{Sub}(G)\) satisfying
\[
\nu=\sum_{g\in G}\mu(g)\,g.\nu,
\qquad g.H=gHg^{-1}.
\]
The Schreier graph of \(H\le G\) with respect to \(S\) has vertices \(H\backslash G\) and edges \(Hg\to Hgs\) for \(s\in S\).

intended_role_in_proof:
These are the formal target properties to verify for the constructed \((G,S,\mu,\nu)\).

tool:
Additional guidance strategy

source_status:
additional guidance item

exact_statement_or_fact:
Do not seek stationarity from a probability distribution on one countable conjugacy orbit. Instead construct a non-atomic stationary law on rooted Schreier diagrams, preferably through a \(k\)-canopy/local-limit construction from finite labelled diagrams, and push the law to subgroups by the stabilizer map.

intended_role_in_proof:
Guides the construction method and prevents the final proof from trying the invalid countable-orbit approach.

tool:
Ambient group choice

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
For the fixed \(k\), take
\[
G=\langle s_1,\dots,s_r\mid s_i^2=e\rangle
\]
for some finite \(r\) large enough to label all local edge types in the construction, for example \(r=k+4\) or any larger explicitly chosen value. Then \(G\) is countable and \(S=\{s_1,\dots,s_r\}\) is finite and symmetric.

intended_role_in_proof:
Using involutive generators lets finite regular edge-coloured graphs be interpreted as Schreier diagrams of \(G\).

tool:
Generating measure

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
Let \(\mu(s_i)>0\) for every \(s_i\in S\), for instance \(\mu\) uniform on \(S\). Since every generator is in the support, \(\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\) generates \(G\) as a semigroup.

intended_role_in_proof:
Verifies the “generating probability measure” requirement.

tool:
Finite labelled Schreier diagrams

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
A finite connected graph whose edges are coloured by \(S\), with exactly one incident \(s\)-edge at each vertex for every \(s\in S\), allowing loops for missing colours, defines a transitive finite \(G\)-set and hence a finite Schreier diagram over \(G\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Turns the finite \(k\)-canopy approximants into honest finite Schreier diagrams.

tool:
Uniform-root stationarity in finite diagrams

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
If a finite Schreier diagram is rooted uniformly at a vertex, then for every \(s\in S\), re-rooting across the \(s\)-edge preserves the uniform root law. Therefore the uniformly rooted law is \(\mu\)-stationary for any probability \(\mu\) on \(S\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Provides stationary finite laws before passing to a limit.

tool:
\(k\)-canopy approximants

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
Construct finite connected \(S\)-labelled diagrams \(D_{n,k}\) whose underlying unlabelled geometry is a truncated \(k\)-canopy: a central finite separating region with \(k\) labelled canopy arms, capped at depth \(n\) by label-preserving finite matchings or loops so every generator acts everywhere. The local weak limit of the uniformly rooted laws is a non-atomic law \(\lambda_k\) on infinite rooted Schreier diagrams.

intended_role_in_proof:
This is the main construction. It must be explicitly defined and checked in the final proof.

tool:
Exact \(k\)-ended limiting geometry

source_status:
proved inside the current proof

exact_statement_or_fact:
For \(\lambda_k\)-almost every limiting rooted diagram, deleting a suitable finite central separator leaves exactly \(k\) infinite one-ended components, while deleting any finite set cannot produce more than those \(k\) persistent directions. Loops, finite caps lost in the limit, and finite decorations do not change the number of ends.

intended_role_in_proof:
Verifies the almost-sure Schreier graph end count.

tool:
Compactness of bounded-degree rooted labelled graphs

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a fixed finite label set and bounded degree, the space of rooted connected labelled graphs with the local topology is compact metrizable after restricting to complete deterministic labelled diagrams, and probability measures have weakly convergent subsequences.

intended_role_in_proof:
Justifies taking a local weak limit of the finite rooted diagram laws.

tool:
Closedness of stationarity under weak limits

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
If \(\lambda_n\) are \(\mu\)-stationary probability measures on rooted labelled diagrams and \(\lambda_n\Rightarrow\lambda\), then \(\lambda\) is \(\mu\)-stationary, because re-rooting by each fixed label is continuous on complete rooted Schreier diagrams.

intended_role_in_proof:
Transfers stationarity from finite approximants to the limiting rooted diagram law.

tool:
Stabilizer map

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a rooted connected Schreier diagram over \(G\), the stabilizer of the root is a subgroup of \(G\). The map
\[
\operatorname{Stab}:(\text{rooted Schreier diagrams})\to \operatorname{Sub}(G)
\]
is Borel and equivariant with respect to re-rooting by \(g\) and conjugation by \(g\).

intended_role_in_proof:
Pushes the stationary rooted diagram law \(\lambda_k\) to the desired stationary random subgroup \(\nu\).

tool:
Schreier reconstruction from stabilizer

source_status:
standard background fact

exact_statement_or_fact:
For a connected rooted Schreier diagram over \(G\), the diagram is label-preservingly isomorphic to the Schreier graph of the stabilizer of its root.

intended_role_in_proof:
Ensures that the end count proved for limiting diagrams is the same end count for the Schreier graphs of \(\nu\)-random subgroups.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
C4

hardest_step_description:
The hardest part is proving that the local weak limit is supported on infinite diagrams with exactly \(k\) ends. The finite caps and regularizing loops must disappear locally or remain end-neutral, while the \(k\) canopy arms must survive as exactly \(k\) infinite components after deleting a finite separator.

risk_if_wrong:
If the limiting geometry has one end, two ends, infinitely many ends, or extra ends created by caps/decorations, the theorem fails even if stationarity and the stabilizer pushforward are correct.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
The final proof should explicitly define the limiting \(k\)-canopy model, name a finite separator, prove that each of the \(k\) post-separator components is infinite and one-ended, and then prove an upper bound of \(k\) ends by showing every ray eventually belongs to one of those components. It should separately note that loops and finite decorations do not affect ends.

5. Failure-mode checks

circularity_check:
The proof must not assume the existence of \(\nu\) or of \(k\)-ended stationary random subgroups. Stationarity must come from finite rooted diagram stationarity plus weak limits, and the end count must come from direct analysis of the constructed limit.

full_theorem_check:
The blueprint covers every required object: countable \(G\), finite symmetric \(S\), generating \(\mu\), Borel probability \(\nu\), \(\mu\)-stationarity, and \(\nu\)-almost sure exact \(k\)-ended Schreier graphs.

source_check:
No allowed supporting statements exist. All construction lemmas, finite diagram checks, and end-count checks must be proved inside the current proof. Only explicitly listed standard background facts may be used without reproof.

hypothesis_check:
The only input hypothesis is \(k\ge3\). The construction should choose the number of canopy arms equal to this \(k\), and all constants such as \(|S|\) may depend on \(k\).

notation_check:
Use right cosets \(H\backslash G\) and edges \(Hg\to Hgs\). The conjugation action in stationarity is \(g.H=gHg^{-1}\). Since the chosen generators are involutions, \(S=S^{-1}\).

standard_background_check:
The final proof may use compactness of bounded-degree rooted labelled graph space, closedness of stationarity under weak limits, Borel/equivariant stabilizer map, and Schreier reconstruction from a rooted diagram. Any other nontrivial fact should be stated and proved.

6. Subproblem assignment table

S1:
Construct \(G,S,\mu\) and the finite diagrams \(D_{n,k}\). Work with \(G=\ast_{s\in S}C_2\), choose \(|S|\) large enough for the \(k\)-canopy edge colours, put positive mass on every generator, and prove each \(D_{n,k}\) is a connected complete finite Schreier diagram.

S2:
For the uniformly rooted laws \(\lambda_{n,k}\), prove finite stationarity under \(\mu\), use compactness to extract a local weak limit \(\lambda_k\), and prove stationarity survives in the limit. Also verify the limit is non-atomic, in the sense required to avoid the forbidden countable-orbit construction.

S3:
Analyze the limiting \(k\)-canopy geometry. Prove \(\lambda_k\)-almost surely the graph is infinite, locally finite, connected, and has exactly \(k\) ends. Treat loops, labels, finite decorations, and finite caps as end-neutral.

S4:
Prove the stabilizer-map step. Show that rooted Schreier diagrams over \(G\) map Borel-measurably to \(\operatorname{Sub}(G)\), that re-rooting by \(g\) corresponds to conjugation by \(g\), and therefore \(\nu=\operatorname{Stab}_\ast\lambda_k\) is \(\mu\)-stationary.

S5:
Assemble the final theorem from C1-C6. Check all quantifiers for every \(k\ge3\), verify \(\operatorname{supp}(\mu)\) semigroup-generates \(G\), and transfer the exact \(k\)-end property from \(\lambda_k\)-random diagrams to Schreier graphs of \(\nu\)-random subgroups.
