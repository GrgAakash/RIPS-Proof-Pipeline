1. Assignment restatement

For a shifted \((d+1)\)-uniform family \(\mathcal F\), define

\[
\mathcal S_F=\{A\in\mathcal F:1\in A\},
\quad
\mathcal H=\{A\in\mathcal F:1\notin A,\ \{2,3,4\}\subseteq A\}.
\]

The assigned task is to use the injection from S3 to prove the intended counting reduction.

2. Subproof or failure

The literal claim

\[
|\mathcal F|\le |\mathcal S_F|+|\mathcal H|
\]

cannot be proved from the stated definitions, because \(\mathcal S_F\) and \(\mathcal H\) are disjoint subfamilies of \(\mathcal F\). If

\[
\mathcal B=\{A\in\mathcal F:1\notin A,\ \{2,3,4\}\nsubseteq A\},
\]

then

\[
|\mathcal F|=|\mathcal S_F|+|\mathcal H|+|\mathcal B|.
\]

Thus the literal inequality is equivalent to \(|\mathcal B|=0\), which is stronger than what the S3 injection supplies.

The corrected S4 counting step is:

Let

\[
\mathcal S_1=\{T\in {[n]\choose d+1}:1\in T\}
\]

be the full \(1\)-star. If S3 provides an injection

\[
\phi:\mathcal B\hookrightarrow \mathcal S_1\setminus \mathcal S_F,
\]

then

\[
|\mathcal B|\le |\mathcal S_1\setminus \mathcal S_F|
=|\mathcal S_1|-|\mathcal S_F|.
\]

Therefore

\[
|\mathcal F|
=|\mathcal S_F|+|\mathcal H|+|\mathcal B|
\le |\mathcal S_F|+|\mathcal H|+|\mathcal S_1|-|\mathcal S_F|
=|\mathcal S_1|+|\mathcal H|.
\]

So the usable conclusion is

\[
|\mathcal F|\le |\mathcal S_1|+|\mathcal H|.
\]
