assigned subclaim(s): C3

what must be proved: For each `A in H`, where `H={A in F: 1 notin A}`, there exists a proper subset `T subsetneq A` such that no present star set `{1} union B in F` satisfies `B cap A = T`.

declared prerequisite subclaims: None. The subproof assumes the local S3 hypotheses: `F` is a `(d+1)`-uniform family with `VCdim(F) <= d`, and `A in H`.

2. Subproof or failure

Let `A in H`. Then `A in F`, `1 notin A`, and, since `F` is `(d+1)`-uniform, `|A|=d+1`.

Suppose for contradiction that the desired conclusion fails. Then for every proper subset `T subsetneq A`, there is some `B` such that `{1} union B in F` and `B cap A = T`. Since `1 notin A`, this also means
`({1} union B) cap A = T`.

Thus every proper subset of `A` is realized as a trace on `A` by some member of `F`. The full subset `A` is also realized, namely by the member `A in F`, because `A cap A = A`.

Therefore every subset of `A` is realized as `F' cap A` for some `F' in F`. Hence `A` is shattered by `F`. But `|A|=d+1`, so this implies `VCdim(F) >= d+1`, contradicting `VCdim(F) <= d`.

Therefore there must exist some proper subset `T subsetneq A` which is not realized by any present star set `{1} union B in F` with `B cap A = T`.
