2. Subproof or failure

We prove the degree check conditional on the S2 construction.

Let `R` be any commutative ring, and suppose S2 has selected a polynomial
`g in R[x_1,x_2]` of the form
`g(x_1,x_2) = u + v x_1 + w x_2 + z x_1 x_2`,
where `u,v,w,z in R`. The monomials appearing here are `1`, `x_1`, `x_2`, and `x_1 x_2`. Their total degrees are respectively `0`, `1`, `1`, and `2`. Therefore every monomial appearing in `g` has total degree at most `2`, so `g` has total degree at most `2`. Thus the degree bound is independent of `X`, of `f`, and of the particular ring `R`.

Hence, for the general construction, the universal integer may be recorded as

`d = 2`.

It remains only to check the degenerate finite sets.

If `X = empty`, then `X^2 = empty`, and there is exactly the empty function `f : X^2 -> X`. Choose any commutative ring, for instance `R = Z`. The unique map `j : empty -> Z` is injective. Let `g = 0 in Z[x_1,x_2]`. Its total degree is at most `2`, and the required identity
`j(f(a,b)) = g(j(a),j(b))`
has no instances to check, since there are no pairs `(a,b) in X^2`. Thus the embedding condition holds.

If `|X| = 1`, write `X = {x}`. Then necessarily `f(x,x) = x`. Choose `R = Z`, define `j(x) = 0`, and let `g = 0 in Z[x_1,x_2]`. The map `j` is injective because its domain has one element. Also
`j(f(x,x)) = j(x) = 0 = g(0,0) = g(j(x),j(x))`.
The polynomial `g = 0` has total degree at most `2`. Thus the embedding condition holds in the singleton case.

Therefore S4 is proved: the construction has a universal degree bound `d = 2`, and the empty and singleton edge cases satisfy the theorem with this same bound.
