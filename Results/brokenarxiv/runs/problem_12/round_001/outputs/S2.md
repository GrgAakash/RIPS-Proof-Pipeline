2. Subproof or failure

Let `X` be finite and let `f : X^2 -> X` be given. Introduce one indeterminate `E_x` for each `x in X`, and let

`A = Z[E_x : x in X]`.

For every ordered pair `(a,b) in X^2`, impose the relation

`E_a E_b - E_{f(a,b)} = 0`.

Since `X` is finite, the set of such relations is finite. Let `I` be the ideal of `A` generated by these relations, and set

`R = A / I`.

Write `e_x` for the image of `E_x` in `R`. Define

`g(x_1,x_2) = x_1 x_2 in R[x_1,x_2]`.

This polynomial has total degree `2`.

Now fix any ordered pair `(a,b) in X^2`. In `R`,

`g(e_a,e_b) = e_a e_b`.

By definition of quotient multiplication, this is the image of `E_aE_b` in `A/I`. Since `E_aE_b - E_{f(a,b)}` is one of the generators of `I`, its image in `R` is zero. Therefore the images of `E_aE_b` and `E_{f(a,b)}` in `R` are equal, so

`e_a e_b = e_{f(a,b)}`.

Thus

`g(e_a,e_b)=e_{f(a,b)}`

for every ordered pair `(a,b) in X^2`.

This proves the assigned S2 subclaim.
