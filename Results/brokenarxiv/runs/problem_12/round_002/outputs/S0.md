1. Target Decomposition

target_label:
Universal bounded-degree polynomial embedding for binary functions.

target_type:
Existence theorem.

main_goal:
Construct one universal integer `d >= 1` such that every finite binary function `f : X^2 -> X` has a polynomial representation of total degree at most `d` over some commutative ring.

variables_and_parameters:
`X` finite set; `f : X^2 -> X`; commutative ring `R`; injection `j : X -> R`; polynomial `g in R[x_1,x_2]`.

conclusion_to_prove:
There exist `d`, `R`, `j`, and `g` with `deg(g) <= d` such that
`j(f(a,b)) = g(j(a),j(b))` for all `(a,b) in X^2`.

2. Available Tools

tool:
Free commutative ring quotient construction.
source_status:
standard background fact.
exact_statement_or_fact:
Given generators and polynomial relations over `Z`, the quotient of a polynomial ring by an ideal is a commutative ring satisfying exactly the imposed relations.
intended_role_in_proof:
Build a ring tailored to the finite operation table of `f`.

tool:
Adjoin formal symbols for elements of `X`.
source_status:
provided definition / notation / assumption.
exact_statement_or_fact:
Since `X` is finite, we may choose one formal generator `u_a` for each `a in X`.
intended_role_in_proof:
Define the embedding candidate `j(a) = [u_a]`.

tool:
Ordered-pair selector relations.
source_status:
proved inside the current proof.
exact_statement_or_fact:
Introduce auxiliary generators `p_a`, `q_b`, and impose relations making `p_a(u_c) = delta_{a,c}` and `q_b(u_c) = delta_{b,c}` in a suitable formal sense, while keeping ordered variables separate.
intended_role_in_proof:
Permit a degree-2 polynomial such as `sum j(f(a,b)) p_a(x_1) q_b(x_2)` to select the value for the ordered pair `(a,b)`.

tool:
Linear selectors encoded by coefficients.
source_status:
proved inside the current proof.
exact_statement_or_fact:
Instead of high-degree interpolation, one may build the ring so that degree-1 expressions in one input evaluate to prescribed idempotent-like selector values on the finite embedded set.
intended_role_in_proof:
Keep total degree bounded independently of `|X|`.

tool:
Finite-product ring alternative.
source_status:
standard background fact.
exact_statement_or_fact:
A finite direct product of commutative rings is a commutative ring, with operations coordinatewise.
intended_role_in_proof:
Give a clean concrete realization of selectors and injectivity without relying on hidden interpolation.

tool:
Coordinatewise construction over `Z`.
source_status:
proved inside the current proof.
exact_statement_or_fact:
Take `R = Z^X x Z^{X^2}` or a similar finite product ring, define `j(a)` with coordinates encoding both identity and ordered-pair behavior, and choose a low-degree polynomial whose coordinates recover `j(f(a,b))`.
intended_role_in_proof:
Main construction route.

tool:
Guidance item 1.
source_status:
additional guidance item.
exact_statement_or_fact:
Do not use the commutative quotient with `g=x_1 x_2` for arbitrary binary functions; in any commutative ring it forces symmetry.
intended_role_in_proof:
Ensures the construction uses an order-sensitive polynomial, e.g. using separate `x_1` and `x_2` roles.

4. Hardest Step Prediction

hardest_step_id:
SC2.

hardest_step_description:
Building ordered-pair selectors of uniformly bounded degree without ordinary high-degree interpolation.

risk_if_wrong:
If the selectors require degree depending on `|X|`, the theorem is not proved. If they are symmetric, they cannot encode arbitrary binary functions.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
Use a custom commutative ring with enough coordinates or imposed relations so that selectors are built into the ring structure. The proof should explicitly evaluate the candidate selector on arbitrary ordered pairs and verify the Kronecker-delta behavior.

5. Failure-Mode Checks

circularity_check:
Do not assume the target theorem or any universal interpolation theorem equivalent to it. The construction of `R`, `j`, and `g` must be explicit.

full_theorem_check:
The proof must cover every finite set `X` and every binary function `f : X^2 -> X`, not only operations with symmetry or algebraic structure.

source_check:
Use only definitions in the theorem, standard commutative-ring constructions, and the additional guidance. No external theorem or paper-specific lemma is available.

hypothesis_check:
`R` may depend on `X` and `f`; only `d` must be universal.

notation_check:
The polynomial has variables `x_1,x_2`; total degree must be bounded by the chosen universal `d`.

standard_background_check:
Finite products of commutative rings and polynomial quotient rings are acceptable standard background. Any special selector behavior must be proved directly.
