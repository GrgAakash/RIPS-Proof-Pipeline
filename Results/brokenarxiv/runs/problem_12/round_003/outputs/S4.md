1. Assignment restatement

Assume the weak downstream selector identity is available: for each ordered pair `(a,b) in X^2`, there is a polynomial `Delta_{a,b}(x,y)` such that for all `u,v,c in X`,
`Delta_{a,b}(j(u),j(v)) * j(c)` equals `j(c)` if `(u,v)=(a,b)`, and equals `0` otherwise.

Show that defining
`g(x,y)=sum_{(a,b) in X^2} Delta_{a,b}(x,y) j(f(a,b))`
gives the exact evaluation table for `f`.

2. Subproof or failure

Let `R` be the commutative ring containing the image of `j`, and define

```text
g(x,y) = sum_{(a,b) in X^2} Delta_{a,b}(x,y) * j(f(a,b)).
```

This is a polynomial over `R`, since `X` is finite and each `j(f(a,b))` is a coefficient in `R`.

Fix arbitrary `u,v in X`. Evaluating at `(j(u),j(v))` gives

```text
g(j(u),j(v))
= sum_{(a,b) in X^2} Delta_{a,b}(j(u),j(v)) * j(f(a,b)).
```

For each summand, apply the weak downstream selector identity with `c=f(a,b)`. If `(a,b) != (u,v)`, then

```text
Delta_{a,b}(j(u),j(v)) * j(f(a,b)) = 0.
```

For the unique summand `(a,b)=(u,v)`, the same identity gives

```text
Delta_{u,v}(j(u),j(v)) * j(f(u,v)) = j(f(u,v)).
```

Therefore all summands vanish except the `(u,v)` summand, and hence

```text
g(j(u),j(v)) = j(f(u,v)).
```

Since `u,v` were arbitrary, `g` realizes the full binary table of `f` on `j(X)^2`.

If each `Delta_{a,b}` has total degree at most `D`, then `g` also has total degree at most `D`, because multiplying by the coefficient `j(f(a,b))` and taking a finite sum do not increase total degree.
