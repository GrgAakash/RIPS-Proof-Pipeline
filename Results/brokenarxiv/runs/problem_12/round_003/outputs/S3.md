1. Assignment restatement

Prove that the proposed weak-selector quotient does not collapse distinct embedded elements \(j(a)\), \(j(b)\). Since the exact S2 construction is not present in this standalone packet, give a conditional no-collapse criterion and identify the obstacle.

2. Subproof or failure

SETUP FAILURE.

Missing required inputs:

- The actual prequotient commutative ring \(P\).
- The quotient ideal \(I\) defining \(A_X=P/I\).
- The chosen lifts \(\tilde j(a)\in P\) whose images are \(j(a)\).
- The exact weak-selector relations imposed by S2.

Conditional criterion:

Let \(A_X=P/I\), and suppose \(j(a)=\tilde j(a)+I\). Then \(j:X\to A_X\) is injective iff
\[
\tilde j(a)-\tilde j(b)\notin I
\]
for every \(a\ne b\).

A sufficient faithful-representation test is: find a commutative ring \(B\) and a homomorphism \(\rho:P\to B\) such that \(I\subseteq\ker\rho\) and the elements \(\rho(\tilde j(a))\) are pairwise distinct. Then \(\rho\) factors through \(A_X\). If \(j(a)=j(b)\) in \(A_X\), applying the factored map gives \(\rho(\tilde j(a))=\rho(\tilde j(b))\), contradiction.

Pairwise separating maps also suffice: if for each \(a\ne b\) there is \(\rho_{a,b}:P\to B_{a,b}\) with \(I\subseteq\ker\rho_{a,b}\) and \(\rho_{a,b}(\tilde j(a))\ne\rho_{a,b}(\tilde j(b))\), then the product map into \(\prod_{a\ne b}B_{a,b}\) separates all \(j(a)\).

Obstacle:

The weak-selector interface alone does not determine whether any difference \(\tilde j(a)-\tilde j(b)\) lies in the quotient ideal. That depends on the exact S2 relations and their multiplicative consequences. Therefore no unconditional injectivity proof is possible from the supplied packet alone.
