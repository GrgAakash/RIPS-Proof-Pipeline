1. Assignment restatement

Audit only: verify edge-case handling and assumptions needed for the final theorem proof, specifically fixed degree independent of `|X|`, empty/singleton `X`, commutative ring conventions/unitalization, and avoidance of the two stated failure modes.

2. Subproof or failure

The audit can be completed conditionally on the intended downstream-selector construction.

Let the final construction provide, for every finite `X`, a commutative ring `A_X`, an injection `j : X -> A_X`, and polynomials `Delta_{a,b}(x,y)` whose total degrees are bounded by a single integer `D` independent of `X`, `a,b`, and `f`, with the downstream action
`Delta_{a,b}(j(u),j(v)) * j(c) = j(c)` when `(u,v)=(a,b)`, and `0` otherwise.

Then the assembled polynomial
`g(x,y)= sum_{(a,b) in X^2} Delta_{a,b}(x,y) * j(f(a,b))`
has total degree at most `D`, since multiplying by the coefficient `j(f(a,b)) in A_X` does not increase degree, and finite sums do not increase the maximum degree. The number of summands may depend on `|X|`, but the total-degree bound does not. Thus the universal degree is `d = max(1,D)`.

Empty case: if `X = emptyset`, there is a unique binary function `X^2 -> X` only in the vacuous sense since `X^2=emptyset`; take any commutative ring, for example the zero ring or `Z`, and the unique injection `emptyset -> R`. The zero polynomial works vacuously. If using a nonzero unital convention, take `R=Z`.

Singleton case: if `X={a}`, every binary function sends `(a,a)` to `a`. Take any commutative ring with a chosen element, for example `R=Z`, `j(a)=0`, and `g(x,y)=0`. This has degree `0 <= d` and satisfies the embedding identity.

Ring convention: if commutative rings are required to be unital, the construction should explicitly produce a commutative unital ring or pass to a unitalization only if the defining relations and injection proof survive. If the zero ring is disallowed as a unital ring by convention, the empty case should use `Z`, not the zero ring. The theorem only needs “some commutative ring,” so choosing a unital ring throughout is safe if the main construction supports it.

Failure-mode audit: the final proof must not use `g=x_1 x_2` or any construction whose only ordered-pair signal is the commutative monomial `xy`, since that cannot distinguish `(a,b)` from `(b,a)` after evaluation in a commutative ring. The selector gadget must contain an order-sensitive pattern, such as an asymmetric expression, and the injection proof must verify that this asymmetry is not collapsed by quotient relations.

Second, the final proof must not claim ordinary degree-`<=2` Kronecker pair selectors with values exactly `1_R` and `0_R` on all of `j(X)^2`. The allowed interface is weaker: `Delta_{a,b}` only needs to act correctly after multiplication by the intended output element `j(c)`. The proof of assembly must use exactly this downstream identity, not an ordinary `0/1` selector identity.

5. Interface notes for S6
