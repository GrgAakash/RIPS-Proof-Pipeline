1. Assignment restatement

S-ID:

assigned subclaim(s):
SC3, the layer-cake Rayleigh bound using level sets.

what must be proved:
For a nonnegative vector \(x=(x_v)_{v\in V(G)}\), with level sets
\[
L_t=\{v\in V(G):x_v>t\},
\]
prove a double-integral representation for \(x^T A_G x\), then apply the SC2 cut bound to obtain a level-set integral upper bound.

declared prerequisite subclaims:
SC2 is required as a prerequisite: there is a constant \(K_p>0\), depending only on \(p\), such that for all \(A,B\subseteq V(G)\),
\[
e_G^\to(A,B)\le K_p d_p(G)\max\{|A|,|B|\}\min\{|A|,|B|\}^{p-1},
\]
where
\[
e_G^\to(A,B)=\sum_{u\in A}\sum_{v\in B}{\bf 1}_{uv\in E(G)}
\]
counts ordered adjacent pairs from \(A\) to \(B\). If SC2 is stated with an unordered convention, the same displayed form follows after changing \(K_p\) by an absolute factor.

2. Subproof or failure

Let \(A_G\) be the adjacency matrix of \(G\), and let \(x_v\ge 0\) for all \(v\in V(G)\). For \(t\ge 0\), define
\[
L_t=\{v\in V(G):x_v>t\}.
\]

For any two nonnegative real numbers \(a,b\),
\[
ab=\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty {\bf 1}_{s<a}{\bf 1}_{t<b}\,dt\,ds,
\]
because the integrand is the indicator of the rectangle \([0,a)\times[0,b)\), whose area is \(ab\). Applying this with \(a=x_u\), \(b=x_v\), and summing over ordered adjacent pairs gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
x^T A_G x
&=\sum_{u,v\in V(G)} A_G(u,v)x_u x_v\\
&=\sum_{u,v\in V(G)} A_G(u,v)
\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty
{\bf 1}_{s<x_u}{\bf 1}_{t<x_v}\,dt\,ds\\
&=\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty
\sum_{u,v\in V(G)} A_G(u,v)
{\bf 1}_{u\in L_s}{\bf 1}_{v\in L_t}\,dt\,ds\\
&=\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty e_G^\to(L_s,L_t)\,dt\,ds.
\end{aligned}
\]
The interchange of the finite sum and the integrals is justified since \(V(G)\) is finite and the integrands are nonnegative.

Now apply the SC2 prerequisite to \(A=L_s\) and \(B=L_t\). For every \(s,t\ge 0\),
\[
e_G^\to(L_s,L_t)
\le
K_p d_p(G)
\max\{|L_s|,|L_t|\}\min\{|L_s|,|L_t|\}^{p-1}.
\]
Therefore
\[
x^T A_G x
\le
K_p d_p(G)
\int_0^\infty\int_0^\infty
\max\{|L_s|,|L_t|\}\min\{|L_s|,|L_t|\}^{p-1}
\,dt\,ds.
\]

Since the level sets are nested, \(s\le t\) implies \(L_t\subseteq L_s\), hence \(|L_t|\le |L_s|\). The integrand is symmetric in \(s,t\), so, ignoring the diagonal \(s=t\), which has measure zero,
\[
x^T A_G x
\le
2K_p d_p(G)
\int_0^\infty
\int_0^t
|L_s|\,|L_t|^{p-1}
\,ds\,dt.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
x^T A_G x
\le
2K_p d_p(G)
\int_0^\infty
|L_t|^{p-1}
\left(\int_0^t |L_s|\,ds\right)
dt.
\]
This proves SC3, conditional on SC2.
