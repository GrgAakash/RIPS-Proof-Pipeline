2. Subproof or failure

SUBPROBLEM UNSOLVED as a proof of C3, because C3 is false.

Take
\[
P=Q=[0,10]\subset \mathbb R.
\]
For every integer `n >= 0`,
\[
nP=[0,10n],
\]
so
\[
L_P(n)=|[0,10n]\cap \mathbb Z|=10n+1.
\]
Similarly,
\[
L_Q(n)=10n+1.
\]
Thus both `P` and `Q` are Ehrhart positive: their Ehrhart polynomials have coefficients `10` and `1`, both non-negative.

Now form the convolution polynomial
\[
H(n)=\sum_{k=0}^n (10k+1)(10(n-k)+1).
\]
Expanding,
\[
H(n)
=100\sum_{k=0}^n k(n-k)
+10\sum_{k=0}^n k
+10\sum_{k=0}^n (n-k)
+\sum_{k=0}^n 1.
\]
Using
\[
\sum_{k=0}^n k=\frac{n(n+1)}2,\qquad
\sum_{k=0}^n k^2=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6,
\]
we get
\[
\sum_{k=0}^n k(n-k)
=n\sum_{k=0}^n k-\sum_{k=0}^n k^2
=\frac{n^3-n}{6}.
\]
Therefore
\[
H(n)
=100\frac{n^3-n}{6}+10n(n+1)+(n+1),
\]
so
\[
H(n)=\frac{50}{3}n^3+10n^2-\frac{17}{3}n+1.
\]
The coefficient of `n` is `-17/3`, which is negative.

Hence coefficientwise positivity of
\[
\sum_{k=0}^n L_P(k)L_Q(n-k)
\]
does not follow from coefficientwise positivity of `L_P` and `L_Q`, even when `L_P` and `L_Q` are Ehrhart polynomials of Ehrhart-positive lattice polytopes.
