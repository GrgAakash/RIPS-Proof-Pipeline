1. Target decomposition

target_label:
Join Ehrhart positivity closure

target_type:
Universal implication about lattice polytopes

main_goal:
Show that Ehrhart positivity of `P` and `Q` implies Ehrhart positivity of `P * Q`.

variables_and_parameters:
`P \subset R^p`, `Q \subset R^q`; lattice polytopes; Ehrhart polynomials `L_P(n)`, `L_Q(n)`, `L_{P*Q}(n)`.

conclusion_to_prove:
All coefficients of the Ehrhart polynomial of `P * Q` are non-negative.

4. Hardest step prediction

hardest_step_id:
C3

hardest_step_description:
The proof would need a coefficientwise positivity theorem for the discrete convolution of two Ehrhart-positive polynomials.

risk_if_wrong:
The entire target theorem fails. Indeed, for segments with `L_P(n)=an+1` and `L_Q(n)=bn+1`, the join has Ehrhart polynomial
`(ab/6)n^3 + ((a+b)/2)n^2 + (1 + (a+b)/2 - ab/6)n + 1`,
whose linear coefficient is negative when `ab > 3(a+b)+6`, for example `a=b=10`.

how_final_proof_should_handle_it:
A final proof should not proceed as if C3 were true. The target theorem should be flagged as false unless additional hypotheses are supplied.
