Produce the complete list of DIRECT dependencies of the target.

A. statement_deps - items needed merely to UNDERSTAND the target's statement:
   every paper-defined term, notation, object, or prior construction the
   statement text uses.

B. proof_deps - items the PROOF relies on, each tagged with kind:
   - "explicit": linked by an exact source label/reference already represented
     in statement_index. Verify and COMPLETE the mechanical list. Phrases such
     as "the previous lemma", "as above", "by (3.2)", and printed theorem or
     equation numbers are evidence that a dependency exists, but are NOT stable
     identities: if the statement_index does not provide an unambiguous
     source-backed id, put the exact phrase under unresolved instead of guessing.
   - "implicit-definition": a paper-defined term or notation used without
     citation.
   - "implicit-result": a prior result of this paper used silently - an
     estimate applied, a construction reused, a case handled "as before".
   - "standing": standing assumptions (gc- items) the argument invokes.

C. external_deps - results from OTHER papers used by statement or proof
   ("[12, Theorem 2.4]", "by a classical result of Serre"). Record the
   citation key if present, the sentence where it is used (verbatim, field
   "usage_quote"), and whether the paper itself states the result precisely
   (if yes, copy that statement verbatim into "statement_tex").
   citation_key must be a literal \cite{...} key appearing in the text;
   when a result is invoked by NAME without \cite ("by Hoeffding's lemma"),
   leave citation_key empty - never put the name in the key field.

Rules:
1. Reference ids from statement_index or global_context only, copied
   character-for-character; never invent or reformat ids. A needed concept
   with no index entry goes under "unresolved" with the exact phrase.
   Never map a printed theorem/equation number to an id by apparent numbering,
   ordering, proximity, or mathematical plausibility.
2. DIRECT dependencies only; transitive closure is computed downstream.
3. Every dependency carries an EVIDENCE quote - the exact words in the
   target where it arises - and confidence "high" | "medium" | "low".
4. Bias toward recall: when unsure, include with confidence "low".
5. If the proof is "NO PROOF GIVEN IN PAPER", proof_deps must be [].
