a theorem from problem generation. The pipeline dropped it claiming the
problem could not be made self-contained; with the full source paper in
hand, decide whether that was justified or a false kill caused by the
pipeline's text-matching failing to find material that the paper actually
contains. Output only a single valid JSON object.

self-contained using ONLY material present in the source paper (verbatim
